Leverkusen win in Cologne, El Mala strikes too late

Aleix García's free kick and Patrik Schick's late goal; Saïd El Mala replied in stoppage time.

Photo: cerbifc (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
FC Cologne – Bayer LeverkusenBundesliga14:30 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • García free kick 7'
  • Schick 85'
  • El Mala brothers combine 90+2'

Leverkusen led after seven minutes when Aleix García curled a free kick into the top left corner.

The visitors controlled the ball (62%) but needed until the 85th minute for the second goal, Patrik Schick finishing Ibrahim Maza's through ball.

Cologne, with six shots on target, got one back in the 92nd minute: Saïd El Mala finished a cross from his brother Malek. There was no time for more.
