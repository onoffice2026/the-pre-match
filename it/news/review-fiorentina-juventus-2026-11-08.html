Fiorentina and Juventus share four goals in Florence

Twice Fiorentina led, twice Juventus came back: the match at the Franchi ended 2-2.

Photo: Sailko (via Openverse) · CC BY 3.0
Fiorentina – JuventusSerie A19:45 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Three goals from set pieces
  • Juventus 12 corners
  • Fiorentina 22 shots

Fiorentina scored in the fourth minute from a corner: Pote crossed and Mateo Pellegrino headed in from close range.

Nick Woltemade equalised for Juventus after 32 minutes, set up by Randal Kolo Muani. Alieu Njie put Fiorentina back in front in the 54th, again from a set piece.

Juventus levelled for the second time in the 66th minute, when Bremer finished from close range after a Douglas Luiz corner.

Set pieces shaped the evening: three of the four goals came from corners or set pieces, and Juventus won 12 corners. Fiorentina had fewer of the ball (41%) but more shots, 22 to 18.
