Match review

Frosinone 2-0 Torino: Ghedjemis and Calò goals earn home win

Farès Ghedjemis scored early in the second half and Giacomo Calò added a late second as Frosinone outshot Torino 20-12.

Photo: Simone Perciballi · CC BY-SA 4.0
Frosinone – TorinoSerie A14:00 UTC
Punti chiave
  • Goals: Ghedjemis 48', Calò 90+3'
  • Frosinone: 20 shots, 8 on target
  • Torino: 54.4% possession

Frosinone beat Torino 2-0 at the Stadio Benito Stirpe, scoring once at the start of the second half and once in stoppage time.

The first half ended goalless, but three minutes after the restart Frosinone went ahead from a corner. Giorgi Kvernadze set up Farès Ghedjemis, who finished with his left foot from the centre of the box into the bottom left corner.

Torino had more of the ball, with 54.4 per cent possession, but Frosinone created more. The hosts had 20 shots, eight of them on target, while Torino managed 12 shots and three on target.

The second goal arrived in the third minute of stoppage time. Wisdom Amey led a fast break and Giacomo Calò struck from outside the box into the bottom left corner.

Both sides won seven corners. Torino received three yellow cards and Frosinone one.
