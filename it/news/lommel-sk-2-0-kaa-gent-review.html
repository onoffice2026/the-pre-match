Match review

Lommel 2-0 Gent: two late goals in five minutes

Juho Talvitie and Djordje Gordic scored in the last eight minutes as Lommel beat Gent at the Soeverein Stadion.

Photo: Bram Vaesen · CC BY-SA 4.0
Lommel SK – KAA GentBelgian Pro League17:30 UTC
Punti chiave
  • Goals: Talvitie 82', Gordic 87'
  • Lommel: 7 shots on target
  • Gent: 3 yellow cards

Lommel SK beat KAA Gent 2-0 at the Soeverein Stadion, with both goals coming late in the game.

The match was goalless for more than 80 minutes. Juho Talvitie broke the deadlock in the 82nd minute and Djordje Gordic added the second in the 87th minute.

Lommel had 58.7 per cent of the ball and 16 shots, seven of them on target. Gent managed 15 attempts but only four on target.

Gent finished the match with three yellow cards, for Mathias Delorge-Knieper, Tibe De Vlieger and Tiago Araújo. Lommel were not booked.
