Match review

Vargas misses from the spot as Sevilla and Alavés share a goalless draw

Sevilla had 60% of the ball but only three shots on target; Rubén Vargas' penalty was saved after 32 minutes.

Punti chiave
  • Vargas penalty saved (32')
  • Sevilla 60% possession, 3 shots on target
  • Alavés keep a clean sheet after 1-6 in Barcelona

On an emotional night at the Sánchez-Pizjuán, Sevilla and Alavés could not find a goal. The best chance fell to Rubén Vargas in the 32nd minute, but his right-footed penalty was saved low to the keeper's left.

Sevilla controlled the ball (60%) and had 13 shots, but only three on target – exactly the problem Luis García Plaza described before the game, when he asked his side to make more of their superior spells.

Alavés, who arrived after a 6-1 defeat in Barcelona, defended well and collected three yellow cards doing it. A clean sheet is a welcome step for Quique's side after a difficult run.

Our take: a point Alavés will value more than Sevilla. For Sevilla, the lack of a cutting edge is becoming a pattern.
