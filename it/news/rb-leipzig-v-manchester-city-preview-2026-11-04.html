Leipzig v Man City: Demichelis and Maresca, friends on opposite benches

City have one win in four; Leipzig have not beaten a top European side since 2022.

Photo: Philipp (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
RB Leipzig – Manchester CityChampions League20:00 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • City: one win in their last four
  • Leipzig: winless against Europe's top sides since 2022
  • Estève targets debut against Haaland

Martín Demichelis and Enzo Maresca, team-mates at Málaga in 2011, meet as coaches. Demichelis calls Maresca a brother and joked that his friend 'wanted to know the line-up yesterday'.

Manchester City arrive in poor domestic form: a home defeat to Brighton, a draw with Brighton and a loss at Aston Villa in their last five, with a 5-1 Champions League win over AEK in between.

Leipzig have won their last three, but were beaten 6-1 at Bayern and 4-1 at Real Madrid in October. Kicker notes the club has not beaten a top international side since 2022.

Defender Maxime Estève, who scored two own goals at the Etihad with Burnley, hopes for his Champions League debut and explained how he plans to stop Erling Haaland, whom he calls the best striker.
