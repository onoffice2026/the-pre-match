Real Sociedad v Lyon: a hurt Real host an OL whose coach wants to leave

Real lost the Basque derby to a controversial penalty; Paulo Fonseca has asked to leave Lyon, with Sporting waiting.

Real Sociedad – LyonEuropa League17:45 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Real lost 2-1 at Athletic after six games unbeaten
  • Fonseca has asked to leave Lyon
  • Lyon lost 3-0 to Hoffenheim in Europe

Real Sociedad's run ended on Sunday. They had won four league games in a row and were unbeaten in six when they lost 2-1 at Athletic Club, where even Athletic coach Edin Terzic admitted the decisive penalty was a generous decision.

Pellegrino Matarazzo's side beat Lillestrom 6-0 in their last European game. Takefusa Kubo also received good news, as he will not be called up for January's Asian Cup.

Lyon arrive in an unusual situation. Coach Paulo Fonseca, in tears after Sunday's 2-0 win over Angers, has asked his board to let him leave, with Sporting CP expected to appoint him. Ernest Nuamah and Corentin Tolisso scored on Sunday.

In Europe, Lyon lost 3-0 to Hoffenheim in their last game. The match at Anoeta kicks off at 18:45 local time with an experienced Greek referee appointed by UEFA.
