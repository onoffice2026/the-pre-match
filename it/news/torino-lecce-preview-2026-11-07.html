Torino want to bounce back as Lecce's slide continues

Torino lost at Frosinone after a run of three wins; Lecce have lost three in a row, the latest at home to Genoa.

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Torino lost their last two after three straight wins
  • Lecce: three defeats in a row, 1-2 v Genoa at home
  • Aboukhlal targeted by racist abuse after the Frosinone game

Torino host Lecce looking to reset after a difficult week. A run of three straight wins – including 3-0 at Parma and 3-2 against Monza – ended with defeats to Como and at Frosinone, where the injury to Raimondo turned into an ugly social-media storm around Zakaria Aboukhlal.

Lecce's trend is worse. They lost 2-1 at home to Genoa last weekend in a direct fight at the bottom, with Messias the inspiration for Daniele De Rossi's side, and before that they were beaten 5-1 at Frosinone. Eusebio Di Francesco's team have lost three in a row.

Lecce's one good memory this autumn is the 3-0 win at Udinese in October. Their problem is that they concede too easily on the road, and Torino at home, when confident, score.

The verdict: Torino are the likelier winners, with the home crowd demanding a quick response. If Lecce keep it tight early, it could become a nervous afternoon, but on current form the gap is clear.
