Atalanta v Parma: Sarri warns against underestimating Parma

Maurizio Sarri says Atalanta want to build "a new, great cycle", but his squad is stretched by injuries, Gianluca Scamacca among them.

Punti chiave
  • Sarri: "We can't underestimate Parma"
  • Scamacca injured
  • Atalanta drew at Fiorentina in midweek

Atalanta host Parma on Monday with Maurizio Sarri insisting the club are aiming to build "a new, great cycle". He also warned that his side cannot underestimate Parma, Football Italia reported.

Sarri has an injury crisis to manage. Corriere dello Sport reported that Gianluca Scamacca is the latest player to be ruled out.

Atalanta avoided defeat at Fiorentina in midweek, with Mario Pašalić coming on in the second half.
