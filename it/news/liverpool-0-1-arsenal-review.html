Match review

Liverpool 0-1 Arsenal: Havertz goal ends a 16-year wait at Anfield

An early error by Alisson led to Kai Havertz's winner, and Arsenal moved to the top of the Premier League.

Photo: Rodhullandemu · CC BY-SA 4.0
Liverpool – ArsenalPremier League16:30 UTC
Punti chiave
  • Goal: Havertz 16'
  • Liverpool: 17 shots, none on target
  • Arsenal's first league win at Anfield since 2012

Arsenal won a Premier League match at Anfield for the first time in 16 years, beating Liverpool 1-0 thanks to a goal that began with a mistake by goalkeeper Alisson Becker. The win took Mikel Arteta's side to the top of the table.

The decisive moment came in the 16th minute. Alisson's pass to Jérémy Jacquet left the centre-back no chance of keeping the ball, Arsenal won it back and moved it to the right, and Noni Madueke and Declan Rice combined for Kai Havertz to tap in at the far post.

The goal took Havertz to 46 in the Premier League, past Ilkay Gündogan as the competition's top German scorer.

Liverpool had most of the ball after going behind, finishing with 56.4 per cent possession and 17 shots, but not one of them was on target and David Raya did not have to make a save. Jurriën Timber's tug on Alexander Isak's shirt as he went for a header was not reviewed by VAR.

The match produced eight yellow cards, four for each side, with Virgil van Dijk booked deep in stoppage time.
