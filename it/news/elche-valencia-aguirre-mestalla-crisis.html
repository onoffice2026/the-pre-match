Elche v Valencia: Aguirre's Valencia still searching for a spark

One home win in nine, late defeats and a derby lost in stoppage time: Valencia travel to Elche under pressure.

Photo: Magicloren · CC BY-SA 4.0
Elche – ValenciaLaLiga17:30 UTC
Punti chiave
  • Valencia lost to Villarreal in stoppage time.
  • Valencia have one win in nine at Mestalla.
  • David Otorbi has renewed until 2031.

Valencia's difficult season continues. They lost the derby against Villarreal deep in stoppage time, when former Ajax striker Georges Mikautadze struck just as Valencia thought they had a point.

Marca's numbers are stark: one win in nine at Mestalla, with three recent defeats conceded in the final minutes under both Corberán and his successor Aguirre. Marca also notes that slow starts are a pattern for Aguirre, who has turned teams around before.

There is some good news for the club's future. Nineteen-year-old winger David Otorbi has extended his contract until 2031, with a reported 60 million euro clause, while he recovers from a muscle injury.

Elche will see an opportunity against a side short on confidence.
