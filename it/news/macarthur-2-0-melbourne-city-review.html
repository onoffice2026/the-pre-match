Match review

Macarthur strike twice early to see off Melbourne City

Apostolos Stamatelopoulos and Ryan Fraser scored inside 33 minutes at Campbelltown.

Photo: MichaelGT (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Macarthur FC – Melbourne City FCA-League

Centro partita →

Macarthur did their damage early and then held on to beat Melbourne City 2-0 at Campbelltown Sports Stadium.

Apostolos Stamatelopoulos opened the scoring after five minutes with a right-footed finish from very close range. Ryan Fraser doubled the lead on 33 minutes with a shot from outside the box into the bottom left corner.

Possession was split almost exactly in half, and City actually had more shots, 12 to 10. The difference was accuracy: Macarthur put five of their efforts on target, City managed one all game.

City's frustration showed in the discipline count: 17 fouls and three yellow cards, for Quinn MacNicol, Harrison Delbridge and Alessandro Lopane, against 10 fouls and one booking for Liam Rose of Macarthur.
