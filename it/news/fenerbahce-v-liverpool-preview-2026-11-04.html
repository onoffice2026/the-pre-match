Fenerbahce v Liverpool: Kuyt faces his old club in Kadıköy

Liverpool chase a fourth straight Champions League win; Fenerbahce's interim coach calls it a huge chance.

Photo: Sakhalinio · CC BY-SA 4.0
Fenerbahce – LiverpoolChampions League17:45 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Liverpool: three wins from three in this Champions League
  • Fenerbahce: one win in five, Talisca out
  • Gravenberch misses the trip with a heel fracture

Liverpool arrive in Istanbul with a 100% record in this season's Champions League and the aim of a fourth straight win in the competition. Andoni Iraola said his side must sharpen up in attack and wants to 'kill the confidence' Fenerbahce draw from a raucous home crowd.

Fenerbahce are led by Dirk Kuyt, the former Liverpool forward, in only his second match as interim coach. Kuyt called it a 'very special' reunion but said he has a job to do, describing Liverpool as a great team while insisting Fenerbahce are one too.

“A great team, but Fenerbahçe is too.”Dirk Kuyt, Fenerbahce interim coach

The hosts are in poor shape: one win in their last five, with defeats to Göztepe and Galatasaray and a 6-0 loss at Aston Villa. They are also without attacker Talisca, who has 19 goals in the last year.

Liverpool will be without Ryan Gravenberch, who is managing a heel fracture, though Iraola said the midfielder could return at the weekend. Cody Gakpo recalled Kuyt's famous Feyenoord hat-trick when asked about his former idol.
