Leverkusen v Stuttgart: Werkself look for league reaction after Hoffenheim blow

A 3-0 defeat at Hoffenheim and a cup scare against Nürnberg have shaken Leverkusen; Stuttgart arrive after a late cup winner from Deniz Undav.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Bayer Leverkusen – VfB StuttgartBundesliga14:30 UTC
Punti chiave
  • Leverkusen lost 3-0 at Hoffenheim and needed a comeback from 2-0 down to beat Nürnberg in the cup.
  • Edmond Tapsoba led the cup comeback.
  • Stuttgart lost 3-2 at home to Gladbach, then won 1-0 at Paderborn in the cup with a stoppage-time Undav goal.

Bayer Leverkusen go into Saturday's home game against VfB Stuttgart needing a response. Hoffenheim beat them 3-0 last weekend - Hoffenheim's third straight win over Leverkusen - in a game that also left defender Loïc Badé hurt after a heavy collision, although the club later gave a cautious all-clear.

Referee Robert Hartmann explained afterwards why he did not stop play immediately after Badé's clash, a decision that caused debate in Germany.

In midweek, Leverkusen came close to a surprise DFB-Pokal exit. Trailing 2-0 to Nürnberg, they turned it around largely thanks to captain Edmond Tapsoba, who led the comeback almost single-handedly, according to kicker.

Stuttgart's week was mixed. They lost 3-2 at home to bottom side Borussia Mönchengladbach, who recorded their first win of the season, but then reached the cup last 16 thanks to Deniz Undav's goal in the first minute of stoppage time at Paderborn.

Both sides arrive with doubts. For Leverkusen, the home crowd will want to see the defensive solidity that went missing in Sinsheim.
