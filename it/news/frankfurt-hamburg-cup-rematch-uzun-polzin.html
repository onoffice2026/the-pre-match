Frankfurt v Hamburg: a quick rematch after the cup, and an injury crisis for HSV

Eintracht knocked Hamburg out of the DFB-Pokal on Tuesday. Merlin Polzin must now rebuild a depleted side for the league meeting.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Eintracht Frankfurt – Hamburg SVBundesliga16:30 UTC
Punti chiave
  • Frankfurt beat HSV 3-1 in the DFB-Pokal on Tuesday.
  • Can Uzun set up two goals in the cup win.
  • HSV are without Nadir and Fabio Vieira long term; Bornauw and Poulsen also missing.

Eintracht Frankfurt and Hamburger SV meet again only days after their DFB-Pokal tie, which Frankfurt won 3-1 in Hamburg to reach the last 16. Can Uzun set up two goals and drew praise from his coach, while Younes Ebnoutalib also made a case for a starting place.

The cup tie was not without controversy. Several debatable decisions went Frankfurt's way, and referee Tobias Stieler admitted afterwards that awarding a penalty 'would have been the better decision' in one situation.

“A penalty would have been the better decision.”Referee Tobias Stieler on a cup incident

For Hamburg the bigger worry is the injury list. Coach Merlin Polzin was already without six regular starters before the cup game, then lost Sebastiaan Bornauw after the break. Bilal Nadir and Fabio Vieira are out long term, and Yussuf Poulsen is also missing this week.
