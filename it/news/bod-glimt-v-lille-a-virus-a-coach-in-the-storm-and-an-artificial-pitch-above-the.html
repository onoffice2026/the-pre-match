Bodø/Glimt v Lille: a virus, a coach in the storm and an artificial pitch above the Arctic Circle

Lille are without Bentaleb (ill) and Ethan Mbappé (suspended); Bodø's Kjetil Knutsen faces questions after a clash with Viking's coach.

Photo: LittleWhites · CC BY-SA 4.0
Bodo/Glimt – LilleChampions League20:00 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Bentaleb ill, Ethan Mbappé suspended
  • Bodø won 3-2 v Viking; both coaches sent off
  • Referee: João Pinheiro (Portugal)

Lille landed in Bodø on Monday lunchtime without Nabil Bentaleb, who is ill, and Ethan Mbappé, who is suspended. Davide Ancelotti calls the trip a very complicated exam but wants to build on Friday's 1-0 derby win over Lens.

Hakon Haraldsson, Lille's Icelandic playmaker, has warned his team-mates about the artificial pitch and the northern conditions, which French and Brazilian players rarely meet.

The hosts have their own problems. The squad was hit by a virus, and coach Kjetil Knutsen is under fire after a face-to-face with the Viking coach on Friday, in a 3-2 win in which both coaches were sent off.

Young winger Soriba Diaoune is the bright spot in a Lille attack hit by injuries. Portuguese referee João Pinheiro will officiate.
