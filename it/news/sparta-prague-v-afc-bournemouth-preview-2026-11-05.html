Sparta Prague v Bournemouth: six wins, six clean sheets

Bournemouth have won six in a row without conceding; Evanilson has scored in four straight Premier League games.

Sparta Prague – AFC BournemouthEuropa League17:45 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Bournemouth: six wins in a row, no goals conceded
  • Evanilson scored in four straight league games
  • Sparta: one point from the last two European games

Bournemouth arrive in Prague as one of the form teams in Europe. Saturday's 2-0 win over Leeds was their sixth victory in a row in all competitions, all without conceding a goal.

That run includes a 3-0 win over AC Milan in this competition, as well as 3-0 wins over Manchester United and Aston Villa. Evanilson has scored in four consecutive Premier League matches.

Sparta's European form is weaker: they lost 1-0 to Olympiacos and drew 1-1 with Lillestrom in their last two games in the competition.
