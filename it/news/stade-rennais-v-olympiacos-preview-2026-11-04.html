Rennes v Olympiacos: a wounded Rennes meet a Greek side that only wins

Olympiacos arrive on eight straight wins; Rennes come off a 5-2 hiding in Nice and are still without top scorer Esteban Lepaul.

Photo: EL Loko Foto · CC BY 4.0
Stade Rennais – OlympiacosEuropa League15:30 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Olympiacos: eight straight wins
  • Rennes lost 5-2 at Nice
  • Lepaul (Rennes) and El Kaabi (Olympiacos) both out

Olympiacos travel to Roazhon Park on a run of eight wins in a row. Their last five include 1-0 European wins over Marseille and Sparta Prague and a 5-0 league rout of Kalamata.

Rennes are far less settled. Their last league outing ended in a 5-2 defeat at Nice, where Elye Wahi scored a hat-trick. Before that they beat Strasbourg 3-0 but lost 4-3 to Paris FC, and in Europe they drew 2-2 with Juventus.

Franck Haise is still without Esteban Lepaul, who hurt his ankle in mid-October and has not played since. The striker has 20 goals in the last 365 days, around 28% of Rennes' total.

Olympiacos also miss a key forward: Ayoub El Kaabi, with 13 goals in the last year, has been out since late October.

The kick-off is in the afternoon, 16:30 in France, a time set around the protests that have also moved Rennes' league schedule.
