Cagliari need a win and Nzola – Frosinone arrive without Raimondo

Cagliari have lost four in a row; high-flying Frosinone lost their breakout striker Antonio Raimondo to a serious ankle injury.

Cagliari – FrosinoneSerie A14:00 UTC

Centro partita →

Punti chiave
  • Cagliari: four defeats in a row; Nzola chasing a third straight goal
  • Frosinone unbeaten in four, 5-1 v Lecce and 2-0 v Torino
  • Raimondo out with a multi-ligament ankle injury

Cagliari need points, and fast. Fabio Pisacane's side have lost four straight, the latest a 2-1 defeat at Lazio after leading, and they remain without Sebastiano Esposito, out since September. The good news is M'Bala Nzola, who has taken over the attack with goals and experience and is chasing a third in a row.

Frosinone are one of the stories of the season – 'Frosinone of wonders', as Corriere dello Sport put it. Unbeaten in four, they scored five against Lecce and beat Torino 2-0 last weekend.

That Torino win came at a price. Antonio Raimondo, the 22-year-old striker who has been the revelation of their campaign, suffered a multi-ligament injury to his left ankle. The injury was followed by a wave of shameful racist abuse on social media aimed at Torino's Zakaria Aboukhlal.

Our view: Frosinone's form makes them dangerous, but losing Raimondo hurts their attack. Cagliari at home, with Nzola in form, have a real chance to end their run – a close game with goals is the likeliest outcome.
