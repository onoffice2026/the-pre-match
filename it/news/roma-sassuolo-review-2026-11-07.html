Roma cruise past Aquilani's Sassuolo

AS Roma 3-0 Sassuolo: 28 shots and 12 corners for Gasperini's side.

Mario Hermoso opened the scoring after 27 minutes and Sebastian Walukiewicz turned the ball into his own net three minutes later.

Rodrigo Mora added the third on 58 from Donyell Malen's pass. Roma had 65% of the ball and 28 shots; Alberto Aquilani's return to the Olimpico ended without a goal for Sassuolo.
