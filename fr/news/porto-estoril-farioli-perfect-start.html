Porto v Estoril: Farioli's side still perfect after nine league games

Porto bounced back from their Champions League defeat to PSV and remain flawless in the league, with Benfica keeping pace.

Photo: Edgar Jiménez from Porto, Portugal · CC BY-SA 2.0
FC Porto – EstorilPrimeira Liga20:30 UTC
Points clés
  • Porto have won all nine league games.
  • William Gomes and Pietuszewski are injured.
  • Estoril's Antef Tsoungui is wanted by Rangers and Southampton.

FC Porto host Estoril with a perfect domestic record. Francesco Farioli's team responded to their Champions League defeat against PSV by beating Gil Vicente and are still flawless after nine league matches.

Benfica are keeping pace: Vangelis Pavlidis helped them turn around a deficit to win narrowly at Santa Clara on Monday night.

Porto do have injuries on the flanks. With William Gomes and Pietuszewski out, Brazilian winger Gabriel Mec has been preparing for his first-team debut in the Allianz Cup tie against Académico de Viseu.

Off the pitch, the club is issuing bonds worth up to 40 million euros over four years, with interest that can rise with sporting success. Estoril, meanwhile, have attracted interest in midfielder Antef Tsoungui from Rangers and Southampton, according to ESPN sources.
