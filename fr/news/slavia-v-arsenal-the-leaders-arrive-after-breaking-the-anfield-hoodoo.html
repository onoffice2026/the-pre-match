Slavia v Arsenal: the leaders arrive after breaking the Anfield hoodoo

Kai Havertz's goal gave Arsenal their first win at Anfield in 14 years; Slavia have found their scoring touch at home.

Photo: User:Zafer · CC BY-SA 4.0
Slavia Prague – ArsenalChampions League20:00 UTC

Centre du match →

Points clés
  • Arsenal won at Anfield for the first time in 14 years
  • Slavia: 4-0 and 5-0 in their last two games
  • Arsenal: 7 unbeaten

Arsenal won 1-0 at Liverpool on Sunday, their first league win at Anfield in 14 years. Kai Havertz scored the only goal after a poor clearance by Alisson.

Slavia, leaders of the Czech league, ended a run of four games without a win by beating Brno 4-0 in the cup and Jablonec 5-0 away, the heaviest home defeat in Jablonec's history.

Arsenal are unbeaten in seven games.
