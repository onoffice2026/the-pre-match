Abdallah's late goal gives Marseille a 2-1 win at Lens

Amine Gouiri scored after two minutes, Florian Thauvin levelled, and Keyliane Abdallah decided it late.

Photo: Supporterhéninois · CC0
Lens – MarseilleLigue 119:45 UTC

Centre du match →

Points clés
  • Gouiri 2'
  • Thauvin 20'
  • Abdallah 82'

Marseille started fast: Amine Gouiri scored from the left side of the six-yard box in the second minute.

Lens equalised in the 20th minute through a Florian Thauvin header from a Florian Sotoca cross.

The match stayed level until the 82nd minute, when Keyliane Abdallah finished from the right side of the six-yard box. Marseille had 59% of the ball; Lens had six shots on target to Marseille's five.
