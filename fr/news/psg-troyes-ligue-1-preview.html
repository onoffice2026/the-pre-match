Ten in a row: rampant PSG host a Troyes side just out of the drop zone

Six goals in Villarreal, five in Le Havre; Troyes beat Le Mans to climb out of the bottom three.

Points clés
  • PSG: ten straight wins, 11 goals in their last two games
  • Troyes out of the bottom three after beating Le Mans 2-0

Paris Saint-Germain go into Saturday night's game against Troyes (21:05 local time) on a run of ten straight wins in all competitions. The last two were emphatic: 6-0 at Villarreal in the Champions League on Tuesday and 5-1 at Le Havre the previous Friday, after a 2-0 win over Lyon.

Troyes took an important step last weekend, beating Le Mans 2-0 at home with goals from Mouhamed Diop in the 33rd minute and Boura in the 71st to move out of the relegation zone. Before that they drew 0-0 at Toulouse and lost 3-1 at home to Lens.

On paper it is the most uneven fixture of the Ligue 1 weekend, and the question is less whether PSG can win than how hard Troyes can make it for them.
