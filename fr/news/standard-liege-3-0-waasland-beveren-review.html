Match review

Dennis Dargahi hat-trick as Standard Liège beat Waasland-Beveren 3-0

Dargahi scored after five minutes, then twice in three minutes after the hour, one of them a penalty.

Photo: Vberger · Public domain
Standard Liege – Waasland-BeverenBelgian Pro League19:45 UTC

Centre du match →

Points clés
  • Dargahi 5', 64' (pen), 67'
  • Standard 62% possession, 16 shots
  • Three yellow cards for Waasland-Beveren

Dennis Dargahi was the man of the night in Liège. He opened the scoring after five minutes, converted a penalty in the 64th minute and completed his hat-trick three minutes later.

Standard controlled the game with 62% of the ball and 16 shots. Waasland-Beveren, who collected three yellow cards, had only three efforts on target.

Our take: a comfortable home win and a big night for Dargahi. Waasland-Beveren struggled to get out of their own half for long spells.
