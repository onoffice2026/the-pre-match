Thun v Hearts: the Swiss champions are sinking

Thun have lost four of their last five; Hearts drew 2-2 with Rangers but went out of the League Cup to Hibernian.

Photo: Rectilinium · CC BY-SA 3.0
FC Thun – Heart of MidlothianConference League17:45 UTC

Centre du match →

Points clés
  • Thun: four defeats in five
  • Hearts lost the League Cup semi-final to Hibernian
  • Hearts drew 2-2 with Rangers last week

FC Thun, the Swiss champions, are in a bad spell. They lost their last two European games 1-0, to Hajduk Split and FC Twente, and were beaten by Young Boys and Basel in the league.

Hearts arrive in a mixed mood. They drew 2-2 with Rangers and beat Motherwell 2-0, but were beaten 2-0 by Trabzonspor in Europe and 7-1 by Celtic in October.

They also lost the League Cup semi-final to city rivals Hibernian, giving Hibs coach Marink Reedijk his first win in charge.
