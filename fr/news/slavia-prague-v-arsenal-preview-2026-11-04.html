Slavia v Arsenal: Saliba back as the leaders come to Prague

Arsenal won at Anfield for the first time in 14 years; Slavia have lost four of their last five.

Photo: Kieran Lynam (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Slavia Prague – ArsenalChampions League20:00 UTC

Centre du match →

Points clés
  • Arsenal: unbeaten in five, 1-0 at Anfield
  • Saliba in the squad for the first time this season
  • Slavia: no win yet in this Champions League

Arsenal top the Premier League and arrive in Prague after a 1-0 win at Liverpool, their first league victory at Anfield in fourteen years. Kai Havertz scored the only goal.

William Saliba is back in Mikel Arteta's squad for the first time this season. The French defender has not played since the World Cup semi-final because of a back injury, and his return gives Arteta a selection dilemma in defence.

Slavia are still looking for a first win in this season's Champions League: a 2-2 draw at Fenerbahce, then home defeats to Lens (2-3) and Barcelona (2-4). At home in the league they are flying, after a 5-0 win over Jablonec. The last meeting with Arsenal ended in a 3-0 defeat.
