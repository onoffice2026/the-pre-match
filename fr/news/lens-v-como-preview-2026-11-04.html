Lens v Como: Fabregas's flyers walk into a wild atmosphere

Como have won five in a row; Lens have lost three of their last five.

Photo: Supporterhéninois · CC0
Lens – ComoChampions League20:00 UTC

Centre du match →

Points clés
  • Como: five wins in a row
  • Lens: three defeats in the last five
  • Fabregas: priority is managing energy

Como arrive in northern France on five straight wins, including 4-1 against Manchester United and 3-1 at Fiorentina. Cesc Fabregas warned his players of a 'wild atmosphere' at Lens and singled out the quality of Florian Thauvin.

Fabregas also spoke about managing energy and about Como being penalised by the UEFA list rules, saying the priority lies elsewhere.

Lens have been inconsistent: three defeats in five, including 3-0 at Club Brugge and 2-0 at home to Sporting CP in Europe. Como goalkeeper Jean Butez, born in Lille and fourteen seasons at LOSC, faces Lens keeper Robin Risser for the first time.
