Match review

Real Betis 2-1 Málaga: Bartra and Fornals strike from set pieces

Two first-half set-piece goals were enough for Betis, despite a second-half reply from Málaga's Adrián Niño at La Cartuja.

Photo: Валерий Дед · CC BY 3.0
Real Betis – MálagaLaLiga17:30 UTC
Points clés
  • Goals: Bartra 38', Fornals 45+2' (Betis); Adrián Niño 68' (Málaga)
  • Both Betis goals came from set pieces
  • Betis: 23 shots, 10 corners

Real Betis beat Málaga 2-1 at La Cartuja, with both of their goals coming from set pieces before half-time.

Marc Bartra opened the scoring in the 38th minute, heading Isco's set-piece delivery into the bottom left corner. In first-half stoppage time Pablo Fornals doubled the lead with a right-footed shot from outside the box, again after a set piece.

Málaga hit back in the 68th minute. Adrián Niño found space in the centre of the box and finished with his left foot into the bottom right corner.

Betis held on to the end, with Nelson Deossa and Aitor Ruibal both booked in stoppage time as the visitors pushed for an equaliser.

The hosts had 23 shots to Málaga's ten and won ten corners to three, although only six of their efforts were on target.
