Amed SFK 5-1 Eyupspor: Ballet hat-trick, then a late red card

Samuel Ballet scored three times, including a penalty, before being sent off in stoppage time as Amed beat Eyupspor in Diyarbakir.

Photo: Chansey · CC BY-SA 4.0
Amed SFK – EyupsporSüper Lig10:30 UTC
Kernpunten
  • Goals: Krasniqi 13', Orban 35', Ballet 42', 51', 83' (pen) (Amed); Abdullahi 73' (Eyupspor)
  • Samuel Ballet: hat-trick and a red card
  • Amed: 20 shots, 7 on target

Amed SFK beat Eyupspor 5-1 in the Turkish Süper Lig at the Diyarbakir Stadyumu, with Samuel Ballet scoring a hat-trick.

Ermal Krasniqi opened the scoring in the 13th minute, and Gift Orban made it 2-0 in the 35th minute. Ballet added the third shortly before half-time, in the 42nd minute.

Ballet scored again early in the second half, in the 51st minute. Eyupspor pulled one back in the 73rd minute through Ahmed Abdullahi.

Ballet completed his hat-trick from the penalty spot in the 83rd minute, but his afternoon ended with a red card in the first minute of stoppage time.

Amed had 20 shots, seven of them on target, and won seven corners to Eyupspor's two. Eyupspor managed eight shots and two on target. Amed completed 411 passes to Eyupspor's 324.
