Basaksehir 5-2 Samsunspor: five different scorers for the hosts

Eldor Shomurodov, Andreas Skov Olsen, Emin Bayram, Ousseynou Ba and Abbosbek Fayzullaev scored as Basaksehir beat Samsunspor.

Photo: Sakhalinio · CC BY-SA 4.0
Istanbul Basaksehir – SamsunsporSüper Lig13:00 UTC
Kernpunten
  • Goals: Shomurodov 10', Skov Olsen 25', Bayram 41', Ba 75', Fayzullaev 90+1' (Basaksehir); Coulibaly 22', Tavsan 84' (Samsunspor)
  • Basaksehir: 24 shots, 10 on target
  • Five different scorers for Basaksehir

Istanbul Basaksehir beat Samsunspor 5-2 in the Turkish Süper Lig, with five different players on the scoresheet.

Eldor Shomurodov opened the scoring in the 10th minute. Tanguy Coulibaly equalised for Samsunspor in the 22nd minute, but Basaksehir went back in front three minutes later through Andreas Skov Olsen.

Emin Bayram made it 3-1 in the 41st minute. After the break the hosts kept pressing, and Ousseynou Ba added a fourth in the 75th minute.

Elayis Tavsan pulled one back for Samsunspor in the 84th minute, but Abbosbek Fayzullaev scored a fifth in the first minute of stoppage time.

Basaksehir had 24 shots, ten of them on target, and won eight corners to Samsunspor's two. The Samsunspor goalkeeper made five saves, and Basaksehir completed 445 passes to Samsunspor's 283.
