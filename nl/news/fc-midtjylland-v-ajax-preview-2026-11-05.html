Midtjylland v Ajax: Danish leaders against an Ajax back in the title race

Ajax have won three league games in a row, including their first win over AZ in four and a half years.

FC Midtjylland – AjaxConference League17:45 UTC

Wedstrijdcentrum →

Kernpunten
  • Midtjylland lead the Danish league
  • Ajax: three league wins in a row
  • Ajax beat AZ for the first time in 4.5 years

Ajax continue their Conference League campaign in Denmark against the league leaders. FC Midtjylland only beat Nordsjaelland 3-2 on Sunday with a goal in the final moments, but have won their last three league games.

Voetbal International describes Midtjylland as a chaotic, very direct team. Their European form is mixed: a 1-0 defeat to Sint-Truiden and a 1-1 draw with Egnatia.

Ajax are back in the Eredivisie title race after three straight wins, the last a 1-0 over AZ, their first win against them in four and a half years. Dutch pundits were not convinced by the football, however.

In Europe, Ajax beat Atalanta 2-0 on 22 October after a defeat to Hajduk Split.
