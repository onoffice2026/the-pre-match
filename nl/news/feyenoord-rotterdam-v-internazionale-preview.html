Feyenoord v Inter: Dutch leaders look to bounce back from late blow

Feyenoord let a win slip against Fortuna Sittard, and Inter arrive after losing the Milan derby.

Photo: Author and original uploader was ART at nl.wikipedia · Public domain
Feyenoord Rotterdam – InternazionaleChampions League20:00 UTC

Wedstrijdcentrum →

Kernpunten
  • Feyenoord drew 1-1 with Fortuna Sittard
  • Ajax are one point behind Feyenoord
  • Referee: Glenn Nyberg (Sweden)

Feyenoord host Internazionale in the Champions League on Tuesday after dropping points at the top of the Eredivisie. They looked set to beat Fortuna Sittard on Saturday, but their own loanee Shiloh 't Zand equalised in the final minutes for a 1-1 draw.

Ajax then beat AZ for the first time since March 2022 and moved within one point of Feyenoord. Giovanni van Bronckhorst made only one substitution against Fortuna, explaining that after a week of rest every player could last 90 minutes.

Inter come into the game after a 3-1 defeat to AC Milan in the derby, which ended their nine-match unbeaten run in Serie A.

UEFA has appointed Swedish referee Glenn Nyberg, who Voetbal International noted is well known to Dutch sides in the Champions League.
