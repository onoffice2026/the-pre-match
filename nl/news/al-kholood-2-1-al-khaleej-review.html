Match review

Al Kholood turn it round against Al Khaleej with a third of the ball

Joshua King's early penalty was cancelled out by Coba da Costa before Julien Domingues won it after the break.

Photo: Rami Al-Khowaiter · CC BY 2.0
Al Kholood – Al KhaleejSaudi Pro League

Wedstrijdcentrum →

Al Kholood came from behind to beat Al Khaleej 2-1 at the King Abdullah Sport City Stadium, and they did it while seeing far less of the ball: 39 per cent possession against 61.

The visitors made the perfect start. Joshua King converted a penalty after seven minutes and Al Khaleej settled into long spells of passing, completing 460 passes to Al Kholood's 270.

Possession did not turn into chances, though. Al Kholood were the side that kept shooting, 17 attempts to 9, and five of theirs were on target against only one from Al Khaleej. Coba da Costa equalised on 36 minutes, and Julien Domingues put the hosts ahead on 58.

It was a scrappy afternoon in places: five yellow cards between the 38th and 54th minutes, three of them for Al Khaleej, and 34 fouls in all. Al Kholood committed 20 of those, a sign of how hard they worked without the ball.

The numbers tell the story of the game: the team with the ball had one shot on target all match, and the team without it took the points.
