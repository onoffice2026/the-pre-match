Five things to watch in the Champions League this week

A Ballon d'Or winner in Madrid, a coach fighting for his job in Manchester and Liverpool's response at Fenerbahçe.

1. Kane in Madrid. The new Ballon d'Or winner leads Bayern against an Atlético side unbeaten in nine.

2. Carrick's night. United have gone nine games without a win, and Roma bring Europe's top scorer of 2026, Donyell Malen.

3. Can anyone stop PSG? Nine straight wins and a perfect start in the league phase; next stop Villarreal.

4. Barcelona's response. A 6-1 at the weekend, but a loss in Paris last time out in Europe; Emery's Villa are perfect so far.

5. Liverpool after Arsenal. Still angry about a penalty refused at Anfield, Liverpool face a Fenerbahçe side in crisis.
