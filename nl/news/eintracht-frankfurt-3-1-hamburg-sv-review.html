Match review

Eintracht Frankfurt 3-1 Hamburg SV: Onyedika and Ebnoutalib seal it

An early own goal, a strike straight after half-time and a counter-attack finish gave Frankfurt the win over Hamburg.

Photo: Patrik Meyer · CC BY-SA 4.0
Eintracht Frankfurt – Hamburg SVBundesliga16:30 UTC
Kernpunten
  • Goals: Omari 10' og, Onyedika 47', Ebnoutalib 69' (Frankfurt); Moffi 14' (Hamburg)
  • Frankfurt: 10 shots on target
  • Hamburg: 8 saves, 4 yellow cards

Eintracht Frankfurt beat Hamburg SV 3-1 at the Deutsche Bank Park, scoring twice in the second half after the sides went in level.

Frankfurt took the lead in the 10th minute through an own goal by Warmed Omari. Hamburg equalised four minutes later, when Terem Moffi finished from very close range after a pass from Albert Grønbæk.

The hosts went back in front two minutes into the second half, Raphael Onyedika scoring from close range after a corner.

The third goal came from a fast break in the 69th minute. Can Uzun sent Younes Ebnoutalib through and he finished into the top left corner.

Frankfurt had ten shots on target from 15 attempts, and Hamburg's goalkeeper made eight saves. Hamburg collected four yellow cards, Omari among them.
