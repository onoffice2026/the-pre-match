Severino's Arouca, three from three, test Famalicão's mixed form

Arouca keep winning 1-0 under their new coach; Famalicão have alternated big wins and heavy defeats.

Photo: Agencia LUSA · CC BY 3.0
FC Famalicao – AroucaPrimeira Liga20:45 UTC

Wedstrijdcentrum →

Kernpunten
  • Arouca: three 1-0 wins in three games under Severino
  • Famalicão lost 0-3 at Braga last time out
  • Arouca without Barbero (13 goals in a year)

Vítor Severino has made a perfect start at Arouca: three league games, three wins, all by 1-0, against Estrela, at Alverca and against Nacional. He is now chasing Daniel Sousa's record at the club, and before Friday's trip to Famalicão he said his team want to be "proactive, but not greedy" in a game that "can be tactically very interesting".

Famalicão coach Ricardo Silva described the challenge in simple terms: Severino's Arouca "don't concede and always score". His own side have been hard to read, winning 4-0 at Nacional and at Rio Ave but losing 1-4 at home to Alverca and 0-3 at Braga last weekend.

“They don't concede and they always score, and that is certainly a challenge for us.”Ricardo Silva, Famalicão coach

Arouca will again be without striker Barbero, out long-term after scoring 13 goals in the last year, about a quarter of the team's total. Away from the pitch, general director Joel Pinho said he is "not a fortune teller" but believes the club can have a historic season, and that January will not be a time for a sell-off.
