Manchester United v Roma: Carrick under pressure after nine without a win

The 2-0 defeat at Chelsea extended Manchester United's worst winless run for 42 years, while Roma scored five at Udinese.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Manchester United – AS RomaChampions League20:00 UTC

Wedstrijdcentrum →

Kernpunten
  • Man United: nine games without a win
  • Lisandro Martínez sent off in the 2-0 defeat at Chelsea
  • Roma won 5-2 at Udinese

Manchester United face AS Roma in the Champions League on Tuesday on the back of their worst winless run for 42 years. The 2-0 defeat at Chelsea on Saturday, with goals from Rogers and a Palmer penalty after Lisandro Martínez had been sent off, stretched the run to nine matches.

Michael Carrick said he expected to remain in charge for the Roma game, Sky Sports reported. Captain Bruno Fernandes returned to the starting line-up at Stamford Bridge after being dropped the previous weekend.

Tottenham's 2-0 win over Crystal Palace kept United out of the Premier League relegation zone overnight, Mundo Deportivo noted.

Roma travel in good form. They won 5-2 at Udinese on Saturday, with Bryan Cristante, Rodrigo Mora, Donyell Malen, Evan Ndicka and Daniele Ghilardi all on the scoresheet.
