Bodø/Glimt 0-0 Lille: one card, no goals in the Arctic

A balanced game at the Aspmyra Stadion ended goalless, with six shots on target in total.

Bodø/Glimt – LilleChampions League20:00 UTC
Kernpunten
  • Possession 50.8% - 49.2%
  • Bodø/Glimt: 4 shots, all on target
  • One yellow card in the whole match

Bodø/Glimt and Lille shared the ball almost exactly (50.8% to 49.2%) and neither side found a way through.

Bodø/Glimt shot only four times, but all four were on target. Lille had nine shots, two on target.

It was a clean game: Nathan Ngoy's yellow card in the 83rd minute was the only card of the night.
