616 days later: can De Zerbi's Spurs finally win three in a row?

Tottenham have not won three Premier League games in succession since early 2025. A revived side under Roberto De Zerbi gets the chance against a Crystal Palace team in difficulty.

Tottenham Hotspur – Crystal PalacePremier League17:30 UTC
Key points
  • Spurs last won three league games in a row 616 days ago.
  • Tottenham won at Chelsea for the first time in eight years.
  • Crystal Palace coach Pierre Sage is described as under heavy pressure.

By the time Tottenham Hotspur kick off against Crystal Palace on Saturday, 616 days will have passed since they last won three Premier League games in a row. Three managers have come and gone since then, none able to stretch a winning run beyond two.

Roberto De Zerbi now has that chance. Spurs' second consecutive win came at Stamford Bridge, their first victory at Chelsea in eight years, after a dramatic start to the season.

De Zerbi put the turnaround down to a change in spirit within the group, which he says is translating into better individual performances on the pitch.

Crystal Palace arrive in a very different mood. The French press describes the situation of Palace coach Pierre Sage as critical, and Foot Mercato placed Palace alongside Manchester United among the clubs whose crisis continued last weekend.

There is a Tottenham subplot to the week as well: former striker Harry Kane, now at Bayern Munich, won the Ballon d'Or - although, as Foot Mercato reports, Spurs will receive no bonus from it.
