Toulouse play the better football – Strasbourg arrive with the confidence

Toulouse have been praised for their style but not rewarded in results; Strasbourg crushed Auxerre 5-1 last weekend to end four straight defeats.

Key points
  • Toulouse: praised for their play, but one point from the last two games
  • Donnum suspended, Messali injured, Amougou ineligible v his parent club
  • Strasbourg ended four straight defeats with 5-1 v Auxerre

Friday night in Toulouse brings together two teams heading in different directions. L'Équipe asked this week whether Toulouse need to play 'less beautifully' to win more often: the football under Jens Berthel Askou has drawn praise, but the last two league games brought a 0-0 with Troyes and a 2-0 defeat in Marseille.

Askou has absences to manage. Aaron Donnum is suspended, Rafik Messali is injured, and Mathis Amougou cannot face the club that loaned him. Goalkeeper Guillaume Restes, criticised after Marseille, got a public vote of confidence from his coach: 'believe me, he is the most critical of himself.'

Strasbourg came into last Sunday in the relegation zone after four straight league defeats and left it with a 5-1 win over Auxerre, Yassine scoring twice. Hugo Oliveira is expected to start Conrad Harder up front with Gio Reyna behind him, while Fabio Baldé and Joaquín Panichelli remain out.

We lean slightly towards Toulouse at home – their football creates chances, and Strasbourg's away record before last week was poor. But the 5-1 has changed the mood in Alsace, and if Toulouse keep missing their chances, Strasbourg have the attackers to punish them. Goals at both ends would not be a surprise.
