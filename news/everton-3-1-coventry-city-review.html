Match review

Barry's double lifts Everton into the top five as Coventry are beaten 3-1

Thierno Barry turned the game in five minutes after Yann Gboho's opener; Dewsbury-Hall made it safe on David Moyes' landmark night.

Photo: Radarsmum67 (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Everton – Coventry CityPremier League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Gboho 52', Barry 56' and 61', Dewsbury-Hall 81'
  • Everton up to fifth, above Liverpool
  • Moyes equals Catterick's record of 592 games

Everton came from behind to beat Coventry 3-1 and climb to fifth, above Liverpool. Yann Gboho gave Frank Lampard's side the lead seven minutes after the break, his first goal for the club in only his second league appearance.

Thierno Barry needed nine minutes to change everything. He headed the equaliser from Vitaliy Mykolenko's cross on 56 minutes and bundled in a second at the far post five minutes later. It is his third league game in a row with a goal and his tenth of the season; only Erling Haaland has more league goals.

Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall finished it with an angled shot nine minutes from time. Coventry will feel they deserved a penalty in the first half, when James Tarkowski appeared to pull Caleb Yirenkyi's hair, but VAR took no action.

On the night David Moyes equalled Harry Catterick's club record of 592 matches in charge, his side showed again how much they get from limited resources. Jack Grealish, absent after a disciplinary issue, is expected back after the international break.
