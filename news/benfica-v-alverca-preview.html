Benfica v Alverca: Marco Silva's side chase a fifth straight win

Benfica have won four in a row in all competitions, while Alverca coach Sérgio Ferreira says his side must be "very consistent" at the Estádio da Luz.

Key points
  • Benfica: four straight wins
  • Alverca: "We have to be very consistent" - Sérgio Ferreira
  • Bojang expected to replace Baseya in Alverca's defence

Benfica host Alverca on Monday night aiming for a fifth straight win. Since a 3-1 defeat at FC Porto in September, Marco Silva's side have beaten Vitória de Guimarães 2-0, Celtic 4-1, Omonia Nicosia 4-1 and Santa Clara 2-1.

Silva spoke on Sunday about a new option on the right of the attack, the team's mistakes and pressure on the medical department, Record reported. He was also asked again about refereeing and admitted that his aim had been not to comment, but that he could not help it.

Alverca coach Sérgio Ferreira is aware of the difficulty of the trip to the Luz but confident in his team. "We have to be very consistent," he told Record. According to the paper, Gambian centre-back Bojang, 22, is expected to replace Baseya.

Alverca lost 1-0 at home to Arouca last time out, after a 4-1 win at Famalicão. They have won three of their last five league games.
