Aston Villa v Fulham: Jackson and Suzuki carry Villa's feel-good factor

Villa beat the league leaders last weekend; Fulham have won two in a row and climbed out of the relegation zone.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Aston Villa – FulhamPremier League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Villa beat Manchester City 2-1 with two goals from Nicolas Jackson.
  • Emery: 'time and consistency' will make Jackson one of the league's best.
  • Fulham have won two in a row; Oscar Bobb scored the winner at Coventry.

Aston Villa go into Saturday evening's game on a high after handing Manchester City their first league defeat of the season, 2-1, with Nicolas Jackson scoring both goals.

Unai Emery believes 'time and consistency' will turn Jackson into one of the Premier League's best strikers, while admitting there is still a lot of work to do to get there.

“Time and consistency will make him one of the Premier League's best strikers.”Unai Emery on Nicolas Jackson (paraphrased by Sky Sports)

At the other end, Villa fans have taken to Zion Suzuki. The chant about having the world's number one goalkeeper began with Emiliano Martínez and became defiant support for the young Japanese keeper - Sky Sports reports supporters now genuinely believe it.

Fulham, though, travel in form. Oscar Bobb's first Premier League goal, a strike in the 80th minute at Coventry, gave them a 1-0 win, back-to-back victories and a place outside the relegation zone.
