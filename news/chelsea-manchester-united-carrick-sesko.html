Chelsea v Manchester United: Carrick insists the dressing room is not divided

Eight games without a win, Benjamin Sesko out until January and public criticism from senior players: Manchester United travel to Stamford Bridge in turmoil.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Chelsea – Manchester UnitedPremier League31.10 12:30 UTC
Key points
  • United are eight games without a win after a 3-0 defeat against Bournemouth.
  • Benjamin Sesko is out with a shin injury until January 2027.
  • Chelsea were beaten at home by Tottenham.

Manchester United arrive at Stamford Bridge on Saturday lunchtime in their worst run of the season. The 3-0 defeat against Bournemouth stretched their winless sequence to eight matches, and British media report that the club's leadership is already weighing up Michael Carrick's future.

The week has been dominated by words rather than results. Lisandro Martínez criticised the board for assembling what he called too weak a squad, while Bruno Fernandes suggested that Carrick's tactics led to the heavy defeat at Como. Carrick left Fernandes on the bench against Bournemouth, which did not change the outcome.

“The dressing room is not divided.”Michael Carrick, Manchester United head coach

The coach has stood his ground. Asked about the mood, he insisted the dressing room is not divided - and he still has the support of the supporters, who backed him after the Bournemouth defeat.

The injury news does not help. Striker Benjamin Sesko has a shin injury and, according to leading English media, will not play again this year, with a return expected in January 2027. Rather than promoting Joshua Zirkzee, United may go back into the market.

Chelsea have their own wounds to lick: Tottenham recently won at Stamford Bridge for the first time in eight years. A meeting between two sides in need of a reaction should make for an edgy opening game of the weekend.
