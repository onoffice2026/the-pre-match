Colas header in the 90th minute earns Le Mans a 1-1 draw with Monaco

Mamadou Coulibaly put Monaco ahead before half-time; Erwan Colas levelled at the very end.

Key points
  • Coulibaly 40'
  • Colas 90'
  • Le Mans 19 shots

Monaco led from the 40th minute, when Mamadou Coulibaly shot from the centre of the box.

Le Mans kept pressing - 19 shots against five - and were rewarded in the 90th minute, when Erwan Colas scored from very close range after a corner.

Monaco put three of their five attempts on target; the late goal cost them two points.
