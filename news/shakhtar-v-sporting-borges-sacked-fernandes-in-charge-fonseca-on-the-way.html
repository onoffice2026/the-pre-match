Shakhtar v Sporting: Borges sacked, Fernandes in charge, Fonseca on the way

Sporting change coach two days before a Champions League game played in London.

Shakhtar Donetsk – Sporting CPChampions League17:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Rui Borges sacked; Tiago Fernandes interim coach
  • Paulo Fonseca expected as the new coach
  • Game played in London

Sporting have dismissed Rui Borges, and Tiago Fernandes takes temporary charge for the game against Shakhtar Donetsk, played in London. Paulo Fonseca, a former Shakhtar coach, is expected to take over.

Borges did not travel; Portuguese reports say he was unhappy with the way his replacement by Fonseca was handled. Fernandes called up 25 players, with Zalazar back in training.

Shakhtar beat Dynamo Kyiv 3-0 in the Ukrainian Cup last week but have won only one of their last five games in all competitions in our record.

Kick-off is at 17:45 GMT.
