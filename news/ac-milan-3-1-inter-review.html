AC Milan 3-1 Inter: Moreira inspires derby win as Inter's run ends

Diego Moreira scored once and set up two more as Milan raced into an early lead and ended Inter's nine-match unbeaten run in Serie A.

Photo: Vincenzo.togni · CC BY-SA 4.0
AC Milan – InternazionaleSerie A19:45 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Pulisic 4', Rabiot 10', Moreira 43' (Milan); Bastoni 22' (Inter)
  • Diego Moreira: one goal and two assists
  • Inter: 19 shots and 11 corners, Maignan 7 saves

AC Milan won the Derby della Madonnina 3-1 at San Siro, ending Inter's nine-match unbeaten run in Serie A and, according to the ESPN recap, extending their own unbeaten run in the derby to five matches. The result also handed top spot in the table to Lazio.

Milan struck twice in the opening ten minutes. In the 4th minute Diego Moreira worked his way down the right and crossed for Christian Pulisic, who headed into the bottom left corner. Six minutes later Moreira's cleverly worked corner was turned in at the far post by Adrien Rabiot.

Inter responded in the 22nd minute, when Hakan Çalhanoğlu's corner was flicked on by Manuel Akanji and Alessandro Bastoni headed in at the near post. Mike Maignan then had to react quickly to deny Marcus Thuram and Djed Spence as the visitors pushed for an equaliser.

Moreira restored the two-goal cushion in the 43rd minute, floating a left-footed free-kick over the wall into the top right corner.

Inter dominated the ball after the break, finishing with 56.9 per cent possession, 19 shots and 11 corners, but Maignan made seven saves. A late penalty appeal for handball was overturned by VAR, and Milan held on comfortably.
