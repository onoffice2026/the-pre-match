Balogun steps in for injured Fati as Monaco try to steady themselves

Filipe Luís's honeymoon is over, but Nordsjælland arrive in Monaco without a win in seven.

AS Monaco – FC NordsjællandConference League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Fati out for around two months; Balogun added to the UEFA list
  • Monaco: one win in their last four
  • Nordsjælland: seven games without a win

Monaco have made a change to their Conference League squad before Thursday's visit of Nordsjælland: Ansu Fati, who injured a hamstring against Lille on 25 October, has been taken off the list and replaced by Folarin Balogun. Coach Filipe Luís warned this week that Fati's rehabilitation "is going to be very long", and French reports expect the forward to miss around two months.

Balogun is set to start up front, although L'Équipe reports that he is likely to leave in the January window, when Monaco hope a sale will help balance the books; the club has also pushed some player bonuses back to early 2027.

“There are some behaviours that I need to correct.”Filipe Luís, AS Monaco coach

Filipe Luís won his first nine games in charge, but results have turned: a 1-3 defeat at Paris FC on Sunday followed a 1-1 draw with Lille and a 0-1 loss at Lorient, with a 3-0 win over Freiburg in between. "There are some behaviours that I need to correct," he said. Takumi Minamino, speaking before the game, described missing the last World Cup through injury as a very hard moment in his life.

Nordsjælland are struggling for wins rather than goals: they are winless in seven, with draws of 3-3 at Lyngby, 2-2 against CSKA Sofia and 1-1 at Silkeborg before a 2-3 home defeat to Midtjylland. Kick-off in the Principality is at 21:00 local time.
