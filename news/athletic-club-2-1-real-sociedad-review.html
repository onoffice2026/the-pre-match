Athletic Club 2-1 Real Sociedad: Berenguer penalty settles the Basque derby

Iñaki Williams scored, Luka Sucic equalised and Alex Berenguer won it from the spot in first-half stoppage time at San Mamés.

Key points
  • Goals: I. Williams 14', Berenguer 45+2' pen (Athletic); Sucic 30' (Real Sociedad)
  • Real Sociedad: 61.7% possession
  • Five yellow cards in the derby

Athletic Club won the Basque derby 2-1 against Real Sociedad at San Mamés, with all three goals coming in the first half.

The Williams brothers combined for the opener in the 14th minute: Nico Williams played a through ball and Iñaki Williams volleyed it into the centre of the goal.

Real Sociedad hit back in the 30th minute, when Luka Sucic struck a left-footed shot from outside the box into the bottom right corner.

The winner arrived in first-half stoppage time. Alex Berenguer converted a penalty down the middle to put Athletic back in front.

Real Sociedad had more of the ball after the break, finishing with 61.7 per cent possession, 14 shots and eight corners, but Athletic held on. The second half brought four yellow cards, three of them for the visitors, including Mikel Oyarzabal.
