Two long-range goals give Lille the win in Rennes

Soriba Diaoune scored one and made one; Nathan Ngoy's strike settled it 2-1.

Stade Rennais – LilleLigue 114:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Diaoune goal + assist
  • Ngoy winner 68'
  • Rennes 2 shots on target

Lille went ahead in the 24th minute through Soriba Diaoune's left-footed shot from outside the box. Boulaye Dia equalised seven minutes later from close range after a corner.

The winner came in the 68th minute: Diaoune set up Nathan Ngoy, who also struck from distance into the bottom left corner.

Rennes had more of the ball (55%) and 15 shots, but only two on target.
