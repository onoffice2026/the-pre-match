Kasimpasa 2-2 Besiktas: hosts come back from two down

Leandro Trossard and Dusan Vlahovic put Besiktas 2-0 up, but Cláudio Winck and Marcus Rafferty earned Kasimpasa a point.

Photo: Ank Kumar · CC BY-SA 4.0
Kasimpasa – BesiktasSüper Lig16:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Winck 60', Rafferty 87' (Kasimpasa); Trossard 28', Vlahovic 47' (Besiktas)
  • Kasimpasa: 16 shots to Besiktas's 8
  • Besiktas: 58.5% possession

Kasimpasa came back from two goals down to draw 2-2 with Besiktas in Istanbul.

Besiktas took the lead in the 28th minute through Leandro Trossard, and Dusan Vlahovic doubled it two minutes after half-time.

Kasimpasa replied in the 60th minute through Cláudio Winck, and Marcus Rafferty completed the comeback in the 87th minute.

The hosts deserved their point on the numbers: although Besiktas had 58.5 per cent of the ball, Kasimpasa had 16 shots, six of them on target, against eight and four for the visitors.
