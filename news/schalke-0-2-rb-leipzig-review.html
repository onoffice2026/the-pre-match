Schalke 04 0-2 RB Leipzig: Nusa leads the way at the Veltins Arena

Antonio Nusa scored a penalty and set up Rômulo as RB Leipzig overpowered Schalke with ten shots on target.

Photo: DerHans04 · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Schalke 04 – RB LeipzigBundesliga14:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Antonio Nusa 37' (pen), Rômulo 78'
  • Nusa scored one and assisted the other
  • Leipzig 10 shots on target to Schalke's 1

RB Leipzig were comfortable winners at the Veltins Arena, beating Schalke 04 2-0 thanks to a decisive contribution from Antonio Nusa.

Nusa opened the scoring in the 37th minute from the penalty spot, placing a right-footed kick into the bottom left corner.

Leipzig kept up the pressure after the break and sealed the win in the 78th minute. Nusa turned provider, and Rômulo finished with his right foot from the centre of the box into the bottom right corner.

The margin could have been greater. Leipzig had 18 shots, ten of them on target, and only a busy afternoon for the Schalke goalkeeper, who made eight saves, kept the score down.

Schalke rarely threatened, managing six shots and just one on target with 39.7 per cent possession. Timo Becker and Adil Aouchiche were booked for the hosts, while Leipzig finished without a card.
