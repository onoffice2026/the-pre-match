Sassuolo v Fiorentina: two teams without a win in five

Sassuolo conceded six to Lazio, while Fiorentina sit in the relegation zone and have lost Nikola Pongračić.

Photo: Giorgio Galeotti from Reggio Emilia, Italia · CC BY-SA 2.0
Sassuolo – FiorentinaSerie A17:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Sassuolo lost 6-2 to Lazio
  • Fiorentina in the relegation zone
  • Pongračić injured

Monday's game brings together two struggling sides. Sassuolo have not won in five matches and lost 6-2 at home to Lazio on Tuesday.

Fiorentina are also without a win in five and remain in the relegation zone, according to Corriere dello Sport, despite a 2-2 draw with Atalanta in which Franco Mastantuono shone.

Paolo Vanoli will be without defender Nikola Pongračić, who has a hamstring injury.

Sassuolo's last point came in a 1-1 draw with AC Milan in October; since then they have lost to Frosinone, Como and Lazio, conceding 12 goals in three games.
