Sassuolo v Fiorentina: Vanoli's side search for a way out of the drop zone

Fiorentina have lost six of their first eight league games for the first time, and Nikola Pongracic is injured.

Photo: Giorgio Galeotti from Reggio Emilia, Italia · CC BY-SA 2.0
Sassuolo – FiorentinaSerie A17:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Fiorentina: six defeats in their first eight league games
  • Pongracic out with a hamstring injury
  • Fiorentina drew with Atalanta in midweek

Fiorentina visit Sassuolo on Monday still looking for a way out of the bottom zone. Corriere dello Sport noted that the club had never before lost six of their first eight Serie A games.

Coach Paolo Vanoli, who arrived in Florence last November to lead a survival fight, is being asked to do it again, the paper wrote. Defender Nikola Pongracic has a hamstring injury and has been out since the game against Atalanta.

Fiorentina did hold Atalanta to a draw in Florence in midweek, after a dramatic 2-1 defeat at Inter in which Marcus Thuram scored late on.
