PEC Zwolle 1-0 ADO Den Haag: Mbayo goal on the stroke of half-time

Dylan Mbayo scored from Younes Namli's pass in the 45th minute as Zwolle won with only a third of the ball.

Photo: Alonstoter · Public domain
PEC Zwolle – ADO Den HaagEredivisie11:15 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goal: Mbayo 45'
  • Zwolle: 24 shots, 8 corners
  • Attendance: 13,738

PEC Zwolle beat ADO Den Haag 1-0 in the Eredivisie in front of 13,738 supporters at the MAC³PARK Stadion.

The only goal came in the 45th minute. Younes Namli found Dylan Mbayo in the centre of the box and he finished with his left foot into the bottom left corner.

ADO had 65.8 per cent of the possession, but Zwolle created more. The hosts had 24 shots, five of them on target, and won eight corners to two.

ADO managed 12 shots and three on target, and the Zwolle goalkeeper made three saves to protect the lead.

Both sides received three yellow cards. Zwolle committed 15 fouls and ADO nine. ADO completed 439 passes to Zwolle's 175, while Zwolle made 18 tackles and blocked eight shots.
