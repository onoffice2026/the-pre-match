Shakhtar v Sporting: Tiago Fernandes in charge as Rui Borges stays home

Rui Borges will not travel, and Tiago Fernandes will lead Sporting against a Shakhtar side fresh from a 3-0 cup win over Dynamo Kyiv.

Key points
  • Tiago Fernandes leads Sporting
  • Sporting in talks with Paulo Fonseca
  • Shakhtar beat Dynamo Kyiv 3-0 in the cup

Sporting CP go into Tuesday's Champions League game against Shakhtar Donetsk in turmoil on the bench. Record reported that Rui Borges will not travel and that Tiago Fernandes will lead the team, with Borges unhappy at how his replacement by Paulo Fonseca has been handled.

Get French Football News had reported that Sporting held talks with Fonseca, the Lyon coach, about taking over.

Shakhtar beat Dynamo Kyiv 3-0 in midweek to reach the quarter-finals of the Ukrainian Cup.
