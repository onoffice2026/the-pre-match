Barça v Aston Villa: Fermín in flight, five absent and Emery back at his most-feared ground

Hansi Flick is without Koundé, Eric Garcia, Olmo, Espart and Bardghji; the Gol 1957 stand is reopened for the visit of a Villa side with a perfect European record.

Barcelona – Aston VillaChampions League20:00 UTC
PredictionProbably a win for Barcelona.The Teep, masked: no odds, no percentages. For information only; 18+, play responsibly. · Match centre →
Key points
  • Barça: 6-1 v Alavés, but a loss in Paris in the last round
  • Five Barça players injured; Christensen not ready
  • Villa: perfect in Europe, 1-1 v Fulham at the weekend
  • Gol 1957 stand open again

Barcelona go into Tuesday's game needing a win after losing in Paris in the previous Champions League round. They warmed up by beating Alavés 6-1 on Saturday, with Fermín López scoring once and setting up two, and Raphinha and Lamine Yamal both scoring twice.

Flick has a long list of absentees: Jules Koundé, Eric Garcia, Xavi Espart, Dani Olmo and Roony Bardghji are all injured, and Andreas Christensen did not train with the group on Monday.

The club has lifted the closure of the Gol 1957 supporters' stand, shut for the Alavés game after the Clásico incidents, following an agreement with the fan groups. Prince William, a Villa supporter, may be in the directors' box.

Unai Emery returns to the ground where his Paris side lost 6-1 in 2017 and says Barça are always among the favourites. Villa have taken maximum points in Europe so far, but drew 1-1 with Fulham at the weekend after a Zion Suzuki own goal.
