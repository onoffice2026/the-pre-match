Aston Villa 1-1 Fulham: Suzuki fumble and McGinn miss cost Villa

John McGinn hit the bar from the spot and Zion Suzuki dropped a corner over his own line as Fulham took a point at Villa Park.

Photo: Ian Rob · CC BY-SA 2.0
Aston Villa – FulhamPremier League20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Nicolas Jackson 39' (Villa); Zion Suzuki own goal 64' (Fulham)
  • John McGinn hit the bar with a penalty on 16'
  • Attendance: 34,649

Aston Villa were held 1-1 by Fulham at Villa Park, where a missed penalty and a goalkeeping error cost the hosts two points.

Villa should have led early. VAR spotted a handball by Sander Berge, but John McGinn struck the crossbar with his penalty in the 16th minute. Zion Suzuki then made a fine save to keep out Oscar Bobb.

Nicolas Jackson broke the deadlock in the 39th minute, running onto Johan Manzambi's pass and slotting under Bernd Leno for his fifth Premier League goal in five games.

Fulham were the better side after the break and drew level in the 64th minute in unfortunate circumstances for Villa. Alex Iwobi's inswinging corner dropped under the crossbar and Suzuki fumbled the ball behind his own line.

Villa pushed late on, with Pau Torres heading a free-kick wide, but could not find a winner. The point makes it four from two games for Fulham and eases the pressure on their manager Álvaro Arbeloa, while Villa's inconsistent start continues.
