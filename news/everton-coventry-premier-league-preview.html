Preview

Grealish still out as Everton chase a third straight win against Coventry

David Moyes confirms the midfielder remains unavailable after a breach of discipline; promoted Coventry arrive after beating Sunderland.

Everton – Coventry CityPremier League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Grealish unavailable after a breach of discipline
  • Everton: back-to-back wins over Newcastle
  • Coventry beat Sunderland 3-1 last time out

Jack Grealish will miss Everton's Premier League game against Coventry on Friday night, David Moyes has confirmed. The midfielder was left out after what the club called a breach of discipline, following reports of nights out in London and Cheshire. Moyes has said there is still a way back for Grealish at the club, but not when he might return.

Everton have found form without him. They beat Newcastle twice in five days, 3-1 at home in the cup and 1-0 at St James' Park on Monday, a result Moyes described as "not an easy thing". Thierno Barry has already matched last season's tally of eight goals, in only 12 games this time.

Coventry are one of three promoted clubs, with Ipswich and Hull, who have started well; the trio collected 31 points between them in their first nine games and all sit above the relegation zone. Coventry beat Sunderland 3-1 last Saturday after defeats by Fulham and at Tottenham, and earlier thrashed Newcastle 4-1.
