Feyenoord v Inter: two sides looking to bounce back

Feyenoord dropped points at home to Fortuna Sittard, while Inter lost the Milan derby.

Key points
  • Feyenoord drew 1-1 with Fortuna Sittard
  • Ueda still out
  • Inter lost the derby 3-1

Feyenoord and Inter both come into Tuesday's game after setbacks. Feyenoord drew 1-1 at home to Fortuna Sittard on Saturday, conceding late to Shiloh 't Zand, and now lead the Eredivisie by only one point from Ajax.

Giovanni van Bronckhorst made only one substitution and explained that his strongest team could play 90 minutes after a week of rest. Striker Ayase Ueda remains out.

Inter lost the Milan derby 3-1, their first defeat of the Serie A season, after four straight wins in all competitions.
