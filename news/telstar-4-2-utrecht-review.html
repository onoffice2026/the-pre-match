Telstar 4-2 Utrecht: hosts score four in six-goal thriller

Tyrone Owusu, Abdelnour Soualhia, Nökkvi Thórisson and Ar'Jany Martha scored as Telstar beat Utrecht with only 34.7 per cent of the ball.

Photo: Reinjansma · CC BY-SA 3.0
Telstar – FC UtrechtEredivisie13:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Owusu 8', Soualhia 25', Thórisson 44', Martha 59' (Telstar); Alarcón 42', Cathline 46' (Utrecht)
  • Telstar: 7 shots on target from 16
  • Utrecht: 65.3% possession, 20 fouls

Telstar beat Utrecht 4-2 in the Eredivisie at the 711 Stadion, scoring four times despite having just over a third of the possession.

The hosts went ahead in the 8th minute when Tyrone Owusu finished a fast break from the right side of the six-yard box. In the 25th minute Abdelnour Soualhia headed in Ar'Jany Martha's cross from a corner.

Utrecht replied in the 42nd minute through Ángel Alarcón's header from Marius Broholm's cross, but two minutes later Nökkvi Thórisson restored Telstar's two-goal lead after a through ball from Owusu.

Yoann Cathline made it 3-2 straight after the restart, in the 46th minute. Telstar settled the match in the 59th minute, when Martha finished another fast break set up by Thórisson.

Utrecht had 65.3 per cent possession and nine corners, but Telstar had more shots on target, seven to five. Utrecht committed 20 fouls.
