Match review

STVV 1-2 OH Leuven: two missed penalties and a late sucker punch

Dani van den Heuvel saved two first-half penalties at Stayen, and Davis Opoku's late goal handed Sint-Truiden a fifth straight league defeat.

Photo: Kjen373 · CC BY-SA 4.0
Sint-Truidense VV – OH LeuvenBelgian Pro League01.11 15:00 UTC
Key points
  • Penalties missed: Sebaoui (about 22') and Mbuku (before the half hour), both saved by Van den Heuvel
  • Goals: Mbuku 46' (STVV); Mirisola 53', Opoku 81' (OH Leuven)
  • STVV: fifth league defeat in a row

Sint-Truiden had two penalties in the first half against OH Leuven on Sunday, missed both, and still lost 2-1 at the Daio Wasabi Stayen Stadium. It was STVV's fifth defeat in a row in the Jupiler Pro League, and the result lifted OH Leuven above them in the table.

The first penalty came after about 22 minutes. Ryan Merlen's header struck the hand of Ewout Pletinckx, referee Michiel Allaerts pointed to the spot and VAR confirmed the decision. Ilias Sebaoui's kick was too weak, and Van den Heuvel went the right way to save it.

Less than ten minutes later STVV had a second chance from the spot after a foul by Balikwisha on Sissako. Nathanaël Mbuku took this one, and Van den Heuvel dived to the same side and saved again. The OH Leuven goalkeeper made several more saves before half-time to keep the score at 0-0.

Mbuku made up for his miss straight after the restart, finishing a flowing STVV move in the 46th minute. The lead lasted only seven minutes: Robin Mirisola, who was born in Sint-Truiden, headed in a corner for his first goal against the club. Mirisola later hit the post.

With nine minutes left, Davis Opoku broke away, cut inside from the right and beat goalkeeper Kokubo. STVV protested a challenge by Jamie Lawrence on Joedrick Pupe in the build-up, but VAR let the goal stand.

"First time I'm man of the match," Van den Heuvel said afterwards, describing the penalties as a mental battle. STVV's Frederik Soelle Soelle admitted: "We wanted the three points too badly." OH Leuven are now unbeaten in five league games and sit one point above STVV.
