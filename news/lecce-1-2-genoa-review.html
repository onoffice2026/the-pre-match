Lecce 1-2 Genoa: Osmajic and Messias give Genoa the win

Genoa went two up inside half an hour at the Via del Mare and held on after Youssef Maleh's free-kick.

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Osmajic 11', Messias 28' (Genoa); Maleh 40' (Lecce)
  • Messias: one goal and one assist
  • Lecce: 18 shots, 7 on target

Genoa won 2-1 at Lecce, with both of their goals coming in the first half hour.

Milutin Osmajic opened the scoring in the 11th minute after a pass from Junior Messias. Messias then scored himself in the 28th minute, finishing into the bottom left corner after Stephan El Shaarawy's pass.

Lecce pulled one back in the 40th minute, when Youssef Maleh curled a left-footed free-kick into the bottom left corner.

The hosts pushed for an equaliser in the second half and finished with 18 shots and seven on target, but the Genoa goalkeeper made six saves to protect the lead.
