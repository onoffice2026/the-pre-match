Troyes v Le Mans: Le Mans arrive at the centre of the goal-line row

A goal awarded by goal-line technology at Auxerre sparked one of the controversies of the season in France. Le Mans also lose Djibril Sidibé to a broken foot.

Photo: TaraO · CC BY 2.5
Troyes – Le MansLigue 119:45 UTC
Key points
  • Le Mans drew 1-1 at Auxerre after a goal-line technology controversy.
  • French refereeing chiefs confirmed the goal.
  • Djibril Sidibé is out for several weeks with a broken foot.

Le Mans travel to Troyes after a weekend that put them at the centre of a national debate. Their goal at Auxerre, a header by defender Lucas Buades, was awarded by goal-line technology and earned a 1-1 draw.

Auxerre coach Will Still was adamant it should not have counted, claiming the system mistook goalkeeper Paul Nardi's glove for the ball. kicker described it as an 'unprecedented computer failure', and former France striker Djibril Cissé publicly vented his anger.

The referees' directorate nevertheless confirmed the goal.

On the pitch, Le Mans have a blow to absorb: experienced defender Djibril Sidibé has fractured his foot and will be out for several weeks.
