Académico de Viseu 2-1 Rio Ave: Messeguem and Clóvis turn it around

Rio Ave led at half-time, but two goals in eight minutes gave Académico de Viseu the win at the Estádio do Fontelo.

Photo: Vitor Oliveira from Torres Vedras, PORTUGAL · CC BY-SA 2.0
Académico de Viseu – Rio AvePrimeira Liga01.11 18:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Messeguem 69', Clóvis 77' (Viseu); Patrício 38' (Rio Ave)
  • Clóvis: one goal and one assist
  • Ntoi (Rio Ave) sent off on 90+6'

Académico de Viseu came from behind to beat Rio Ave 2-1 at the Estádio do Fontelo.

Rio Ave took the lead in the 38th minute, when Miguel Patrício headed in João Tomé's cross.

Viseu turned the game around in the second half. Soufiane Messeguem equalised in the 69th minute after a pass from André Clóvis, and Clóvis scored the winner in the 77th minute, running onto Alejandro Mestanza's through ball and finishing into the bottom right corner.

Rio Ave had more of the ball, 54.9 per cent, and both sides had five shots on target. Rio Ave finished with ten men after Andreas Ntoi was sent off for violent conduct deep in stoppage time.
