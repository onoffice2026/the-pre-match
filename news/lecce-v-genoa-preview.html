Lecce v Genoa: De Rossi's side ride the Juventus win

Genoa beat Juventus 2-0 in midweek; Lecce have lost four of their last five.

Key points
  • Genoa beat Juventus 2-0 in midweek
  • Lecce lost 5-1 at Frosinone
  • Lecce: four defeats in five

Genoa travel to Lecce in good spirits after a 2-0 win over Juventus on Wednesday, their second straight victory after a 1-0 win over Venezia.

Daniele De Rossi has praised goalkeeper Justin Bijlow, who is set to make his tenth appearance of the season.

Lecce are struggling. They lost 5-1 at newly promoted Frosinone on Thursday after a 2-0 home defeat by Juventus, and have lost four of their last five games.

Their only recent win came at Udinese, 3-0, in the middle of October.
