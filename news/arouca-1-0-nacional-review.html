Match review

Arouca 1-0 Nacional: Esgaio header from a set piece

Tiago Esgaio headed in Naïs Djouahra's set-piece cross in the 64th minute to give Arouca the win.

Photo: JP Ribeiro · CC BY-SA 4.0
Arouca – C.D. NacionalPrimeira Liga15:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goal: Esgaio 64'
  • Arouca: 8 corners to 2
  • Nacional: 1 shot on target

Arouca beat Nacional 1-0 in the Portuguese Primeira Liga at the Estádio Municipal de Arouca, with a second-half header deciding the match.

The first half ended goalless, although Arouca had most of the ball and finished with 61.6 per cent of the possession.

The goal came in the 64th minute from a set piece. Naïs Djouahra crossed and Tiago Esgaio headed into the top of the goal from the centre of the box.

Arouca had 12 shots, five of them on target, and won eight corners to Nacional's two. Nacional had five shots and one on target, and their goalkeeper made four saves.

Each side received two yellow cards. Arouca committed only six fouls in the match, Nacional ten. Arouca completed 486 passes to Nacional's 271.
