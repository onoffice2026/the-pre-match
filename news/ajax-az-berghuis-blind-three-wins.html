Ajax v AZ: Berghuis expected back as Ajax chase a fourth straight win

Ajax have won three in a row and Míchel expects Steven Berghuis to return for the visit of AZ, with Daley Blind back after 1,433 days.

Photo: Alf van Beem · CC0
Ajax Amsterdam – AZ AlkmaarEredivisie31.10 20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Ajax have won three in a row.
  • Steven Berghuis is expected back against AZ; Caio Henrique and Gaaei only after the international break.
  • Daley Blind returned to the Eredivisie after 1,433 days.

Ajax welcome AZ on Saturday evening on the back of three consecutive victories. The most recent came away at Sparta, where pundits on the Pak Schaal podcast praised an excellent performance from goalkeeper Marc ter Stegen and discussed the club's title ambitions.

Coach Míchel Sánchez gave a medical update after that game. Steven Berghuis is expected to be available against AZ, while Caio Henrique and Anton Gaaei are only likely to return after the international break. There is no timeline yet for Simon Adingra.

“Of course it has been frustrating.”Daley Blind on his return

The Sparta game also marked a personal milestone. After 1,433 days, Daley Blind made his return to the Eredivisie, coming on late to help see out a hard-fought win. 'Of course it has been frustrating,' he said, adding that he had given everything to get back.

AZ will be a sterner test, and Míchel's selection choices in defence - a recurring topic around the club - will be closely watched.
