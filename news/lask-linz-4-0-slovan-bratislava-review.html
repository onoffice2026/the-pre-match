LASK 4-0 Slovan Bratislava: Jørgensen double and 29 shots

Kasper Jørgensen scored a header and a free-kick as LASK were 4-0 up three minutes after half-time.

Photo: funky1opti (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
LASK Linz – Slovan BratislavaChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Jørgensen: header and free-kick
  • 4-0 after 48 minutes
  • LASK 29 shots, 10 on target

Kasper Jørgensen headed LASK in front in the 19th minute from Moses Usor's cross, and added a second from a free-kick in the 37th.

Robert Ljubicic made it 3-0 just before half-time after a corner, and Samuel Adeniran scored the fourth three minutes after the break.

LASK dominated the numbers: 29 shots, 10 on target, eight corners and 60% of the ball. Slovan's goalkeeper made five saves and his defence 31 clearances.
