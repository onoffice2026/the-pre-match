Injury tracker: who misses the Champions League week

Kane plays, Saibari and Gnabry do not; PSG without three regulars; Barcelona's list grows to five.

Bayern Munich: Ismael Saibari (ankle surgery, three to four months) and Serge Gnabry are out for the year; Michael Olise is fit.

Barcelona: Jules Koundé, Eric Garcia, Xavi Espart, Dani Olmo and Roony Bardghji injured; Andreas Christensen not ready.

Paris Saint-Germain: Ousmane Dembélé, Marquinhos and Nuno Mendes miss the trip to Villarreal.

Lille: Nabil Bentaleb (ill) and Ethan Mbappé (suspended). Feyenoord: Jeremiah St. Juste out; Van Persie and Nelson back.

Real Betis: Cucho Hernández, Junior Firpo and Iker Losada out. Fenerbahçe: Talisca out. Napoli: two starters out, Højlund doubtful.
