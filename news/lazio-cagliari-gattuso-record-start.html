Lazio v Cagliari: Gattuso's leaders chase more history

Twenty-two points from a possible 24 is the best start in Lazio's Serie A history. Gennaro Gattuso is still asking for calm.

Photo: Messapi, cropped by Blackcat · CC BY-SA 4.0
Lazio – CagliariSerie A14:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • 22 points from 24: Lazio's best Serie A start ever.
  • Lazio won 6-2 at Sassuolo despite missing two penalties.
  • Gattuso is preaching calm: 'The table gives you a headache.'

Lazio host Cagliari as leaders of Serie A and as one of the stories of the season. With 22 points from 24, Gennaro Gattuso has delivered the best start in the club's Serie A history, according to Foot Mercato - and it has come despite a boycott by the club's supporters.

Their latest statement was an extraordinary 6-2 win at Sassuolo on Tuesday, in a game that also featured a red card and two missed Lazio penalties. Dutch duo Kenneth Taylor and Tijjani Noslin both scored and were singled out by Gattuso, who described his team as an orchestra.

“The table gives you a headache.”Gennaro Gattuso, Lazio coach

Gattuso refuses to get carried away. 'The table gives you a headache,' he said after the Sassuolo win, insisting there is still a team to chase and warning that a hard knock is still to come.

Another name attracting attention in Rome is the young Romano Floriani Mussolini, described by Foot Mercato as one of the surprises of Lazio's season.
