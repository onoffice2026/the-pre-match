Revived Strasbourg bring Yassine's form to Toulouse

A 5-1 win over Auxerre ended four straight defeats; Toulouse played well in Marseille but came away with nothing.

Photo: André Cros · CC BY-SA 4.0
Toulouse – StrasbourgLigue 106.11 19:45 UTC
Key points
  • Strasbourg ended four straight defeats with a 5-1 win over Auxerre
  • Yassine: two goals and an assist
  • Toulouse lost 2-0 at Marseille but created plenty

Strasbourg arrive in Toulouse on Friday in a much better mood than a week ago. A run of four straight Ligue 1 defeats, against Nice, Paris Saint-Germain and Rennes among others, had pushed them into the relegation places before Sunday's 5-1 home win over Auxerre lifted them above their opponents.

Moroccan attacking midfielder Gessime Yassine was the star of that win, setting up Martial Godo, who was making his first start of the season, and then scoring twice, the first a header, the first of his life. "When I head the ball, I have to close my eyes," he said afterwards with a smile.

“When I head the ball, I have to close my eyes!”Gessime Yassine, Strasbourg

Toulouse lost 2-0 at Marseille on Sunday, but L'Équipe described them as bold and dangerous: they conceded twice in the first half and then forced Marseille goalkeeper Jeffrey De Lange into a string of saves. Before that they drew 0-0 with Troyes and 2-2 at Monaco.
