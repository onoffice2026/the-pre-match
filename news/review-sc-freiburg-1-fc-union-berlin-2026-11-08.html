Freiburg beat Union Berlin 2-0 without allowing a shot on target

An own goal and a Yuito Suzuki finish gave Freiburg a comfortable win.

Photo: Judith Zimmermann (via Openverse) · CC0
SC Freiburg – 1. FC Union BerlinBundesliga16:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Khedira own goal 55'
  • Suzuki 67'
  • Grifo penalty saved 84'

The first goal came in the 55th minute, an own goal by Union Berlin's Rani Khedira. Yuito Suzuki made it 2-0 in the 67th, set up by Derry Scherhant.

Vincenzo Grifo could have added a third, but his penalty in the 84th minute was saved by Frederik Rønnow.

Freiburg had 20 shots to Union's four; the visitors did not put a single one on target.
