Marseille v Toulouse: Genesio wants answers after the 4-3 scare against Le Havre

Four goals up with half an hour left, Marseille almost threw it all away against bottom side Le Havre. Bruno Genesio has explained why he spared Angel Gomes from criticism.

Photo: Sophie P. · CC BY-SA 4.0
Marseille – ToulouseLigue 119:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • OM led 4-0 against Le Havre and won only 4-3.
  • Genesio criticised the young substitutes, including Keyliane Abdallah.
  • Adidas' return as kit supplier is being brought forward.

Marseille won their last league game 4-3 against Le Havre, but nobody in the camp treated it as a normal victory. Leading 4-0 with half an hour to go, OM collapsed in the closing stages and nearly let bottom-placed Le Havre, still without a league win, back into the match.

Bruno Genesio was furious afterwards and singled out the young substitutes. 'I am very disappointed with the youngsters who came on,' he said, with Keyliane Abdallah among those criticised.

“I am very disappointed with the youngsters who came on.”Bruno Genesio, Marseille coach

In his press conference before Toulouse, the coach returned to that outburst and explained why Angel Gomes was not part of it. Amine Harit, meanwhile, issued a reminder to the squad about the complicated ending.

Off the pitch, Marseille are preparing a commercial boost: Adidas are set to return as kit supplier earlier than the 2028-29 date originally planned.
