Bayer Leverkusen 2-4 VfB Stuttgart: Demirovic double stuns the BayArena

Stuttgart soaked up pressure and struck four times on the break and from set pieces to win a six-goal contest in Leverkusen.

Bayer Leverkusen – VfB StuttgartBundesliga14:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Demirovic 14', 65'; Leweling 22'; El Khannouss 51' (Stuttgart); Schick 28', Maza 79' (Leverkusen)
  • Eight yellow cards, four of them in the 40th minute
  • Stuttgart scored four from 7 shots on target with 36.8% possession

VfB Stuttgart produced a ruthless away performance at the BayArena, beating Bayer Leverkusen 4-2 despite having little more than a third of the ball.

Ermedin Demirovic opened the scoring in the 14th minute with a left-footed finish from the centre of the box, and Jamie Leweling doubled the lead in the 22nd minute, prodding home from close range after a corner. Leverkusen hit back in the 28th minute when Patrik Schick headed in Aleix García's corner.

The temperature rose before the break, with four players booked in the 40th minute alone: Facundo Medina and García for Leverkusen, Jeff Chabot and Luca Jaquez for Stuttgart. The referee showed eight yellow cards in all, four to each side.

Stuttgart took control after the interval. Bilal El Khannouss struck a right-footed shot from outside the box into the bottom right corner in the 51st minute, set up by Chris Führich, and Demirovic added his second in the 65th minute, finishing a fast break created by Deniz Undav. The goal stood after a VAR check.

Ibrahim Maza headed a consolation for the hosts in the 79th minute from Miguel Gutiérrez's cross. Leverkusen finished with 63.2 per cent possession, 24 shots and 12 corners, but Stuttgart turned seven efforts on target into four goals.
