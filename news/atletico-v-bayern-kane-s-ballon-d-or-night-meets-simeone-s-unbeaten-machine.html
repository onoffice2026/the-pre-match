Atlético v Bayern: Kane's Ballon d'Or night meets Simeone's unbeaten machine

The new Ballon d'Or winner leads Bayern into the Metropolitano with two attacking players lost for months and Michael Olise passed fit.

Atlético Madrid – Bayern MunichChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Kane plays his first Champions League game as Ballon d'Or winner
  • Saibari (ankle surgery) and Gnabry are out for the rest of the year
  • Olise is in the squad after a knee scare
  • Atlético: 9 unbeaten; Bayern: 12 unbeaten

Harry Kane arrives in Madrid as the reigning Ballon d'Or winner, having edged Kylian Mbappé for the award a week ago. The 33-year-old striker has also addressed the question of his contract with Bayern, which runs out next summer, on the eve of the game.

Bayern's good news is Michael Olise. The winger left Saturday's 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund with a right-knee problem, but he travelled with the squad and is available for the Champions League tie.

The bad news came from sporting director Max Eberl on Monday: Ismael Saibari and Serge Gnabry were both injured against Dortmund and will not play again this year. Saibari had surgery on torn ankle ligaments and is expected to be out for three to four months.

Atlético come into the game in form. Diego Simeone's side won 2-0 at Levante at the weekend, have won four of their last five and are unbeaten in nine. Bayern are unbeaten in twelve but have drawn two of their last five, both at home.
