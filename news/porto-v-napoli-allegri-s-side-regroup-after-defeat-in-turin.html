Porto v Napoli: Allegri's side regroup after defeat in Turin

Napoli lost 2-0 at Juventus with ten men and travel to Porto with two starters missing.

Photo: Clément Bucco-Lechat · CC BY-SA 3.0
FC Porto – NapoliChampions League04.11 20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Napoli lost 0-2 at Juventus
  • Two Napoli starters out; Højlund trained apart
  • Porto 4-0 Estoril

Napoli were beaten 2-0 by Juventus on Sunday, with Kerim Alajbegović and Jérémie Boga scoring in the second half against ten men. Massimiliano Allegri was suspended, with Landucci on the bench.

Two regular starters will miss the Porto game, and Rasmus Højlund trained separately on Monday.

Porto beat Estoril 4-0 at the weekend but lost their last Champions League game, 1-0 at home to PSV.
