Leverkusen v Marseille: Medina meets his old club as OM rotate

Marseille arrive without Greenwood and with Emerson nursing a knee; Leverkusen have slipped to sixth after losing 4-2 at home to Stuttgart.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Bayer Leverkusen – MarseilleEuropa League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Medina faces his former club
  • Leverkusen lost 4-2 at home to Stuttgart
  • Greenwood out, Emerson doubtful for OM

Facundo Medina faces Marseille for the first time since leaving for Leverkusen in the summer. The Argentine defender, a World Cup finalist this year, told L'Equipe he has learned a lot in the past twelve months.

Leverkusen's form is patchy. They lost 4-2 at home to Stuttgart on Saturday, dropping to sixth in the Bundesliga, and were also beaten 3-0 by Hoffenheim in October. Summer signing Afonso Moreira is still waiting for his first goal, and kicker reports the pressure is starting to tell.

Marseille won their last two league games, 4-3 against Le Havre and 2-0 against Toulouse, with Amine Harit scoring in the first minute and Amine Gouiri adding the second. In Europe, though, they lost 1-0 to both Sturm Graz and Olympiacos.

Bruno Genesio is expected to rotate his squad. Emerson trained apart after a knee knock against Toulouse, and Mason Greenwood, with 18 goals in the last year, has been out since late October.
