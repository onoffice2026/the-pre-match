Crystal Palace v Hoffenheim: Sage under pressure as Palace drop into the relegation zone

Palace lost 2-0 at Tottenham with ten men and are 18th in the Premier League; Hoffenheim beat Leverkusen and Lyon 3-0 in October.

Key points
  • Palace 18th after a third straight league defeat
  • Mateta red card under appeal, Sarr out
  • Hoffenheim beat Lyon 3-0 in the Europa League

Pierre Sage needs a result. Palace lost 2-0 at Tottenham on Saturday after Jean-Philippe Mateta was sent off, their third straight league defeat, and dropped into the Premier League relegation zone. The club is appealing against Mateta's red card.

Wednesday's gossip columns already name England assistant Anthony Barry as a possible replacement if Sage is sacked.

Palace are also without Ismaïla Sarr, who has 15 goals in the last year and has been out since the end of October.

Hoffenheim had a strong October, beating Bayer Leverkusen 3-0, Lyon 3-0 in this competition and Borussia Mönchengladbach 2-1, but their last two games brought a 1-1 draw with Holstein Kiel and a 2-0 defeat at Werder Bremen.
