Getafe 3-1 Sevilla: Ünal and Satriano turn it round late

Enes Ünal and Martín Satriano set each other up in five minutes after Kike Salas had given Sevilla the lead, and Orel Mangala added a third in stoppage time.

Key points
  • Goals: Ünal 68', Satriano 73', Mangala 90+4' (Getafe); Salas 19' (Sevilla)
  • Sevilla: Manu Bueno sent off in the 91st minute
  • 36 fouls in the match

Getafe came from behind to beat Sevilla 3-1 at the Coliseum, scoring three times in the last 25 minutes of a match that ended with Sevilla down to ten men.

Sevilla took the lead in the 19th minute when Kike Salas headed Félix Correia's corner into the top right corner. The visitors held that advantage until midway through the second half, with the two sides level on shots and corners.

Getafe's strikers then combined twice in five minutes. In the 68th minute Martín Satriano crossed for Enes Ünal to equalise from the centre of the box, and in the 73rd minute Ünal returned the favour for Satriano, whose goal was confirmed after a VAR review.

Sevilla's Manu Bueno was sent off in the first minute of stoppage time, and four minutes into added time Orel Mangala finished from Andrés García's pass to make it 3-1.

The match was physical, with 36 fouls between the two sides and four yellow cards for Sevilla. Getafe had 47 per cent possession but five shots on target to Sevilla's four.
