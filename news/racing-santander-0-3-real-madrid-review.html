Match review

Racing Santander 0-3 Real Madrid: Mbappé, Bellingham and Diomande seal it early

Real Madrid were three goals up by the 36th minute at El Sardinero and moved back within three points of leaders Barcelona.

Photo: Maria Victoria Gomez Fernandez · CC BY 2.5
Racing Santander – Real MadridLaLiga15:15 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Mbappé 11' (pen), Bellingham 21', Diomande 36'
  • Bellingham's 50th goal for Real Madrid
  • Real Madrid: 22 shots, 7 on target

Real Madrid made it four La Liga wins in a row with a comfortable 3-0 victory at Racing Santander, a week after beating Barcelona in El Clásico. José Mourinho's side settled the match before half-time and ended Racing's unbeaten home run.

Julen Agirrezabala kept Racing level early with a point-blank save from Jude Bellingham's header, but in the 11th minute Kylian Mbappé was tripped in the box by Álvaro Mantilla and converted the penalty into the bottom left corner. It was his fifth goal in three games.

Thibaut Courtois turned away a low shot from Iker Luque before Madrid doubled the lead in the 21st minute. Arda Güler's through ball sent Bellingham clear, and he rounded the goalkeeper for his 50th goal for the club and his first since September.

The third goal arrived in the 36th minute: after a corner, the ball dropped for Yan Diomande at the edge of the area and he struck a first-time volley into the top left corner, his third goal in four matches.

Racing improved after the break, but Yassir Zabiri missed the target twice, Mbappé hit the post, and a penalty for handball against Aurélien Tchouaméni was overturned after a VAR check. Madrid finished with 22 shots to Racing's 10.
