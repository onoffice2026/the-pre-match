Auxerre v Paris FC moved to 19:00 – and Auxerre must recover from 1-5

The prefecture asked for a later kick-off because of Saturday's demonstrations; Auxerre were beaten 5-1 at Strasbourg last weekend.

Photo: YoyoAJA89 (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 4.0
AJ Auxerre – Paris FCLigue 118:00 UTC

Key points
  • Kick-off moved from 17:15 to 19:00 at the prefecture's request
  • Auxerre lost 1-5 at Strasbourg with ten men
  • Paris FC beat Monaco 3-1 last weekend

Auxerre against Paris FC has been pushed back to 19:00 on Saturday at the request of the Yonne prefecture, with the agreement of both clubs, because of the demonstrations planned that day. The knock-on effect: PSG v Troyes now starts 20 minutes later than planned.

For Auxerre the timing is the smaller issue. Will Still's team were thrashed 5-1 at Strasbourg after going down to ten men, a heavy blow after an encouraging October that included a 3-1 win at Le Havre.

Paris FC come in with an up-and-down run – wins over Monaco (3-1) and Rennes (4-3), defeats at Lens and Lorient – but they score goals and travel with confidence after beating Monaco last weekend.

Our take: Auxerre at home will want a reaction, and the Abbé-Deschamps can be a difficult place. Paris FC's attacking form makes them dangerous, though, and with both defences leaking goals recently, an open game looks more likely than a cagey one.
