Match review

Feyenoord 1-1 Fortuna Sittard: Late 't Zand leveller silences De Kuip

Shiloh 't Zand struck four minutes from time to cancel out Luciano Valente's opener and deny Feyenoord victory.

Photo: Author and original uploader was ART at nl.wikipedia · Public domain
Feyenoord Rotterdam – Fortuna SittardEredivisie15:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Luciano Valente 59' (Feyenoord); Shiloh 't Zand 86' (Fortuna)
  • One yellow card: Syb van Ottele 75'
  • Feyenoord had 69.3% possession and 15 shots

Feyenoord dominated the ball at De Kuip but had to settle for a 1-1 draw after Fortuna Sittard snatched a late equaliser.

The hosts controlled the first half without making it count, and the deadlock did not break until the 59th minute. Anis Hadj Moussa set up Luciano Valente, who finished with his left foot from the centre of the box into the bottom left corner.

Fortuna stayed in the contest and were rewarded in the 86th minute. Sven Simons provided the pass and Shiloh 't Zand slotted a left-footed shot into the same bottom left corner to level the scores.

Feyenoord finished with 69.3 per cent possession, 15 shots and six on target, while the Fortuna goalkeeper made five saves. The visitors managed 11 shots, three on target.

The only booking of the afternoon went to Fortuna's Syb van Ottele in the 75th minute; Feyenoord committed just six fouls all game.
