Match review

Como 1-0 Venezia: Da Cunha penalty decides it

Lucas da Cunha's penalty just before half-time was the only goal as Como dominated the ball and the shots at the Sinigaglia.

Photo: Rehman Abubakr · CC BY-SA 4.0
Como – VeneziaSerie A11:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goal: Da Cunha 43' (pen)
  • Como: 19 shots, 8 corners
  • Venezia: 1 shot on target

Como beat Venezia 1-0 at the Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia, with a first-half penalty from Lucas da Cunha deciding a one-sided match.

The hosts had 65.7 per cent possession and 19 shots, against seven for Venezia, but found it hard to turn their pressure into clear chances. Only four of Como's shots were on target, and eight were blocked.

Venezia picked up two yellow cards before half-time, for Simon Sohm in the 39th minute and Juan Jesus in the 41st minute. Two minutes later Como were awarded a penalty, and Da Cunha converted it with his left foot into the top left corner.

Venezia rarely threatened in the second half, with one shot on target all game and a single corner, against eight for Como.

Como completed 656 passes to Venezia's 310 and committed only five fouls. Kaiki was booked late on.
