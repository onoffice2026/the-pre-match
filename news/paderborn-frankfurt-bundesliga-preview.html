The third 'Götze summit' as in-form Frankfurt visit goal-shy Paderborn

Two wins over Hamburg in five days lifted Eintracht from 14th to 8th; Paderborn's problem is scoring.

Photo: Sviraman · CC BY-SA 4.0
SC Paderborn 07 – Eintracht FrankfurtBundesliga07.11 14:30 UTC
Key points
  • Mario v Felix Götze for the third time
  • Frankfurt up from 14th to 8th after two wins over Hamburg
  • Paderborn: one goal in their last three games

Saturday's game in Paderborn brings a family reunion: Felix Götze of Paderborn against his older brother Mario, the World Cup winner, of Eintracht Frankfurt. Both made it to the Bundesliga, but this will be only the third time they have faced each other directly.

Frankfurt arrive in good shape after beating Hamburg twice in five days, 3-1 in the DFB-Pokal and 3-1 again in the league, a run that lifted them from 14th to 8th in the table. The club has also had to manage the excitement around Niko Ilicevic, who made his Bundesliga debut the day after his 16th birthday to equal a record, and Spanish reports say Eintracht have made first contact over Elche full-back Buba Sangaré.

“Best not to talk about it too much.”Ralf Kettemann, Paderborn coach, on his side's lack of goals

Paderborn have kept things tight but struggle at the other end: 1-0 against Hamburg, 0-1 against Stuttgart and a goalless draw at Gladbach last weekend. Asked about the lack of goals, coach Ralf Kettemann said it was "best not to talk about it too much".
