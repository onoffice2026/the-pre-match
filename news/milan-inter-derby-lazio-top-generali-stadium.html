Milan derby with the title race tightening: Inter no longer on top

Inter go into Saturday's Derby della Madonnina after Lazio overtook them at the top of Serie A, while the two Milan clubs have just revealed the name of their new stadium.

Photo: POAN68 · CC BY-SA 4.0
AC Milan – InternazionaleSerie A19:45 UTC
Key points
  • Lazio overtook Inter at the top of Serie A in midweek.
  • Luka Modric, 41, remains central to Milan's midfield.
  • Milan and Inter named the San Siro successor: the Generali Stadium, due for Euro 2032.

The Milan derby comes at a moment of movement at the top of Serie A. Lazio became the new leaders in midweek with an emphatic win at Sassuolo, helped by goals from Kenneth Taylor and Tijjani Noslin, and in doing so moved past Internazionale in the table.

That raises the stakes for Inter on Saturday night: a derby defeat would not only hurt local pride but risk losing ground in the title race.

AC Milan can rely on a remarkable veteran in midfield. Luka Modric, now 41, is still showing his class in Serie A, and Dutch analysts at Voetbal International devoted a segment this week to why he continues to make the difference at his age.

Off the pitch, the two clubs are working together. On Thursday they revealed the design and name of the successor to San Siro: the Generali Stadium, which is due to be completed in time for Euro 2032.

Not everything around Milan has been positive. The mural dedicated to club legend Franco Baresi outside Casa Milan has been vandalised again, this time with black paint covering several elements of the artwork.
