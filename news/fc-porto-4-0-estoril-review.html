FC Porto 4-0 Estoril: three goals in nine minutes keep the run going

Porto scored early through Victor Froholdt and then struck three times in quick succession after the hour at the Estádio do Dragão.

Key points
  • Goals: Froholdt 4', Sainz 64', André Silva 70', Gabri Veiga 73'
  • Porto: 24 shots, 11 on target
  • Estoril: no shots on target

FC Porto beat Estoril 4-0 at the Estádio do Dragão, keeping up their perfect record in the Portuguese league this season.

Victor Froholdt gave the hosts the lead in the 4th minute, finishing from very close range after a pass from Pepê.

Porto dominated without adding to their lead until the second half. Borja Sainz curled a right-footed shot into the top right corner in the 64th minute after a pass from Gabri Veiga.

André Silva made it 3-0 in the 70th minute from close range, and Veiga completed the scoring in the 73rd minute, set up by Sainz.

Porto finished with 24 shots, 11 of them on target, and 65.5 per cent of the ball. Estoril failed to register a single shot on target.
