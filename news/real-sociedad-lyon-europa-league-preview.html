Real Sociedad look to Europe after derby defeat as Lyon arrive short of two starters

A high-risk night at Anoeta: the Basques want a step towards the top eight, Lyon are without Niakhaté and Nuamah.

Photo: Txuriurdin · CC BY-SA 3.0
Real Sociedad – LyonEuropa League17:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Lyon: Niakhaté and Nuamah out with muscle strains
  • Real Sociedad lost the Basque derby 2-1 and dispute the decisive penalty
  • Four previous meetings, all in the Champions League: two wins each

Real Sociedad host Lyon on matchday four of the Europa League looking for a response to Sunday's 2-1 derby defeat at Athletic Club. Iñaki Williams scored early, Luka Sučić equalised on the half-hour and Álex Berenguer's penalty just before the break decided it; the club has formally disputed that penalty. The result ended a run of four straight league wins.

Before the derby the Basques had been flying, with wins of 6-0 at Lillestrøm, 5-2 at Málaga and 2-1 against Levante, and a win here would keep them on course for a top-eight finish in the league phase.

“They've suffered muscle strains. We hope to have them back for Sunday's match in Brest.”Jorge Maciel, Lyon interim coach

Lyon will be without centre-back Moussa Niakhaté and winger Ernest Nuamah, who both have muscle strains. Interim coach Jorge Maciel said the club hoped to have them back for Sunday's trip to Brest but could not yet say whether they would be fit. Lyon's recent form is mixed: wins over Nice and Angers either side of defeats at Hoffenheim (0-3) and Paris Saint-Germain (0-2).

Off the pitch, the authorities have classed the game as high risk. Basque police identified and fined six Real Sociedad ultras near Anoeta on Wednesday, finding extendable batons, knives, flares and drugs among a group of about a hundred people. The clubs have met four times before, all in the Champions League, with two wins each, including the 2013 qualifier won by Real. Real defender Mamadou Sarr, on loan from Chelsea and a Lyon academy product, faces his former club.
