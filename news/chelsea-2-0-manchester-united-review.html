Chelsea 2-0 Manchester United: Martinez sees red as Carrick's pain deepens

Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer struck after the break as ten-man Manchester United fell apart at Stamford Bridge.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Chelsea – Manchester UnitedPremier League12:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Morgan Rogers 50', Cole Palmer 64' (pen)
  • Lisandro Martínez sent off for a second yellow card on 63'
  • Manchester United committed 20 fouls to Chelsea's 8

Manchester United dominated the first half at Stamford Bridge and left with nothing. A 2-0 defeat to Chelsea was their third in a row and, according to the ESPN recap, stretched their run without a win in all competitions to nine matches for the first time since 1988-89.

The visitors should have been ahead at the interval. Matheus Cunha had the ball in the net on the half-hour, only for VAR to rule him offside, while Bruno Fernandes, restored as captain, skied a glorious chance and Mike Penders kept out Amad Diallo.

Chelsea struck through a moment of individual quality in the 50th minute. Morgan Rogers collected the ball deep, drove through the United defence and finished with his left foot from the centre of the box, with Roméo Lavia credited with the assist.

The game then turned on Lisandro Martínez. Booked for a bad foul in the 59th minute, the defender collected a second yellow card in the 63rd minute for a raised arm on Wesley Fofana, which also handed Chelsea a penalty. Cole Palmer converted it a minute later, steering a left-footed kick into the bottom right corner.

United lost their discipline entirely after that, finishing with a red card and five bookings in total, and committing 20 fouls to Chelsea's eight. The hosts ended with 18 shots, nine on target, and home supporters serenaded Michael Carrick with chants of "you're getting sacked in the morning". Estêvão even had a late third ruled out for offside.
