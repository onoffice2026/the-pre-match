Match review

Brentford 2-0 Nottingham Forest: Milenkovic own goal sets up Bees win

A freak own goal and a deflected Igor Thiago finish ended Brentford's winless run and left Forest in the bottom three.

Photo: AndyScott · CC BY-SA 4.0
Brentford – Nottingham ForestPremier League15:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Milenkovic own goal 30', Igor Thiago 76'
  • Mathias Jensen hit the crossbar with a free-kick
  • Forest: 15 shots, 1 on target

Brentford ended a four-match winless run with a 2-0 victory over Nottingham Forest, who again paid for poor finishing despite plenty of the ball.

Forest had the better early chances. Liam Delap hooked over after turning in the box, and James McAtee curled wide of the far post when well placed.

Brentford took the lead against the run of play in the 30th minute. Dango Ouattara skipped past Murillo and his attempted pass across goal struck Nikola Milenkovic, looping over Matz Sels for an own goal. Mathias Jensen later struck the crossbar with a free-kick.

Ryan Yates headed over a good chance to equalise early in the second half, and Brentford made Forest pay in the 76th minute. Substitute Jaidon Anthony crossed on a fast break and Igor Thiago finished, via another deflection off Milenkovic.

Forest held 58.3 per cent of possession and had 15 shots, yet only one was on target. Brentford needed just seven attempts. Oliver Glasner's side remain in the bottom three with only six league goals this season.
