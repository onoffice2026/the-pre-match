Liverpool v Arsenal: Iraola backs Gravenberch as Arteta's touchline row rumbles on

Andoni Iraola has defended Ryan Gravenberch ahead of Sunday's meeting with Arsenal, while Mikel Arteta arrives at Anfield still unhappy about how the technical-area rule is applied.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Liverpool – ArsenalPremier League16:30 UTC
Key points
  • Liverpool won four of their last five, including a 4-3 comeback against Villarreal.
  • Iraola publicly backed Ryan Gravenberch after recent criticism.
  • Arteta, booked again against Everton, says he is compiling footage of other managers leaving their technical areas.

Liverpool welcome Arsenal on Sunday with momentum behind them. The Reds took four wins from five matches, including a wild 4-3 Champions League comeback against Villarreal from 0-2 down and a 2-0 league win over Brighton at Anfield, after which Iraola said he had seen a team 'you can rely on'.

Ahead of the visit of Arsenal, the Liverpool coach went out of his way to protect Ryan Gravenberch. The Dutch midfielder has been given more of a role in recent weeks but has also faced criticism, and his manager answered it directly: 'We trust him.'

“We trust him.”Andoni Iraola on Ryan Gravenberch

Arsenal come into the fixture after a 2-0 win over Everton, a game that left Mikel Arteta relieved that his players came through it unscathed and full of praise for Gabriel Magalhães, whom he called one of the best in the world.

The same afternoon brought Arteta his second yellow card of the season for leaving his technical area. He responded by saying he is collecting footage of rival managers doing the same, arguing that others are rarely punished. A tense afternoon at Anfield will put that theme back in the spotlight.

Arteta also had to calm talk around goalkeeper David Raya, who spoke this week about an ambition to play in La Liga one day. The manager answered with a joke - in Spain, he said, Raya can play on the beach - and made clear he hopes to keep him for many years.
