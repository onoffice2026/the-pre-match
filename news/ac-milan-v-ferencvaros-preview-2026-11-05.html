Milan v Ferencvaros: derby winners host the Hungarian surprise

Milan beat leaders Inter 3-1 on Saturday; Ferencvaros have won all three of their recent European games.

Photo: kyle simourd (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
AC Milan – FerencvarosEuropa League17:45 UTC
Key points
  • Milan beat Inter 3-1 on Saturday
  • Milan lost 3-0 to Bournemouth in their last European game
  • Ferencvaros: three European wins in a row

Milan come into Thursday's game on a high. On Saturday they beat Inter 3-1 at San Siro, the leaders' first league defeat of the season, with Diego Moreira scoring a superb free-kick and Adrien Rabiot finishing a clever short corner.

Their form is uneven, though: a few days before the derby they lost 4-2 at Bologna, and their last European game ended in a 3-0 defeat to Bournemouth.

Rúben Amorim is managing an injury crisis. Midfielder Diawara, signed for 3 million euros as cover, started the derby and impressed, according to Corriere dello Sport.

Ferencvaros travel with confidence. In Europe they beat Celtic 3-1, Viktoria Plzen 2-1 and Torreense 4-1.
