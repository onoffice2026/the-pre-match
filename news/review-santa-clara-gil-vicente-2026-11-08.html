Gil Vicente come from behind as Santa Clara lose a man

An own goal put Santa Clara ahead; Walace França and a Murilo free kick turned it around after Pedro Pacheco's red card.

Photo: Bill Ward's Brickpile (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Santa Clara – Gil VicentePrimeira Liga08.11 15:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Pacheco red card 62'
  • Murilo free kick 81'
  • Santa Clara 0 shots on target

Santa Clara led in the 29th minute through an Antonio Espigares own goal - the only time the home side beat the goalkeeper, as none of their nine shots was on target.

Gil Vicente equalised seven minutes into the second half with a Walace França header from a corner. Pedro Pacheco was sent off in the 62nd minute, and Murilo won it in the 81st with a left-footed free kick.

The visitors finished with 20 shots and nine corners.
