Napoli and Lazio share the points after Pedraza own goal

Lazio scored first in the 28th minute: Tijjani Noslin led a fast break and Davide Frattesi finished low into the bottom left corner.

Napoli were level three minutes after half-time, when Alfonso Pedraza turned the ball into his own net.

The statistics describe an even match: 15 shots each, six on target each, six corners each and possession almost split down the middle.
