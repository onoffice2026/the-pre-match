Union Berlin v Cologne: a second meeting in five days

Cologne beat Union 5-0 in midweek; now the two sides meet again in the Bundesliga in Berlin.

Photo: Lear 21 · CC BY-SA 3.0
1. FC Union Berlin – FC CologneBundesliga14:30 UTC
Key points
  • Cologne beat Union 5-0 on Tuesday
  • Union: one win in five
  • Cologne drew 0-0 with Schalke

Union Berlin get a quick chance at revenge. Cologne beat them 5-0 on Tuesday, and the two teams meet again on Sunday in Berlin.

Union have won only one of their last five matches, with draws against Augsburg and Borussia Dortmund.

Cologne's form is mixed: the 5-0 win followed a goalless draw with Schalke and a 1-0 defeat at Eintracht Frankfurt.

Union's last home league game was a goalless draw with Borussia Dortmund, and in September they lost 7-0 away at Bayern Munich.
