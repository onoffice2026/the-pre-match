Ten in a row: PSG rest Hakimi before Troyes visit the Parc

Paris Saint-Germain have won ten straight and set a Champions League record in midweek; Troyes arrive after beating Le Mans 2-0.

Photo: *_* (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Paris Saint-Germain – TroyesLigue 120:05 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • PSG: ten straight wins, fastest to four goals in Champions League history
  • Hakimi rested before the international break
  • Kick-off delayed by 20 minutes to 21:05

Paris Saint-Germain are flying. They have won ten games in a row, and on Tuesday they became the fastest side in Champions League history to score four goals, 4-0 up against Villarreal after 16 minutes on the way to a 6-0 win.

The club announced on Friday that Achraf Hakimi will be rested for Troyes, though he remains available for Morocco during the international break. Rotation is likely elsewhere too, with the break approaching.

Troyes come into the weekend with a 2-0 home win over Le Mans, Diop opening the scoring, after a goalless draw at Toulouse. Before that they had lost three in a row, including 0-2 against Marseille and 1-3 against Lens.

The verdict: PSG at home are overwhelming favourites, even with changes. Troyes' best hope is to stay compact and keep the score respectable; their recent defensive record against the top sides suggests that will be hard.
