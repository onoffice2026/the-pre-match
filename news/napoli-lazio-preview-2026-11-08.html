Napoli v Lazio: crisis at the Maradona against Gattuso's leaders

Seventh defeat of the season in Porto, a dressing-room row and a retreat ordered by the club: Allegri's Napoli face the Serie A leaders.

Photo: Joris (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 4.0
Napoli – LazioSerie A11:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Napoli eighth, seven defeats this season
  • Lazio top of Serie A
  • No away fans allowed

Napoli host Lazio at the Maradona in a tense week. After the Champions League defeat in Porto, Italian press reported a row between Lucca and De Bruyne, sharp words from Politano and Højlund, and a visit from Aurelio De Laurentiis to the training ground, where the team was sent into retreat.

Massimiliano Allegri spoke bluntly after a seventh defeat of the season, pointing at the transfer market. Napoli are eighth with 14 points.

“I know Allegri, I don't trust him.”Gennaro Gattuso

Lazio arrive as leaders under former Napoli midfielder Gennaro Gattuso. "I know Allegri, I don't trust him," Gattuso said, confirming that Zaccagni will travel. Lazio fans are banned from the away end and planned a send-off at the team's departure instead.
