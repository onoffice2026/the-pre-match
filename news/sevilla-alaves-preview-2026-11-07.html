Sevilla sixth but unconvinced: García Plaza wants more from his good spells

Sevilla host Alavés on Friday night in a game also marked by the tribute to Jesús de Miguel; Alavés arrive seven matches without a win.

Photo: Niels98 (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 4.0
Sevilla – AlavésLaLiga20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Tribute to Jesús de Miguel before kick-off
  • García Plaza: 'sixth place does not reflect reality'
  • Alavés: seven games without a win, 1-6 at Barcelona

The Sánchez-Pizjuán opens the twelfth round on an emotional night, with the tribute to Jesús de Miguel, the Tenerife striker who died after an on-pitch collision last weekend. On the pitch, Sevilla want to put Getafe behind them: Luis García Plaza called the second half there 'the worst we have done'.

The coach is not hiding from the paradox of sixth place. 'A team that is sixth cannot say it is doing things badly,' he said, but he wants his side to 'make more of the moments when we are superior' – and he warned fans that the season will be long, suggesting two or three teams below Sevilla will finish above them.

The selection question is up front: Isaac Romero, the squad's best scorer, or continuity for Stassin, and whether to play two strikers. García Plaza confirmed Fofana and Isaac are fit. Alavés arrive in poor shape – seven games without a win, the last a 6-1 defeat in Barcelona – and Quique admits his first job is to rebuild the players' morale.

Our take: Sevilla at home against a side short of confidence should be the favourites, but their habit of fading after the break keeps the door open. A home win, with Alavés' best hope a low-scoring game.
