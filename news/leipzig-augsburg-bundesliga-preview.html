Leipzig regroup after City defeat as Augsburg search for a first win in six

Twice level against Manchester City, Leipzig still lost; Augsburg keep throwing away leads late on.

Photo: Eurobettingtips (via Openverse) · Public domain
RB Leipzig – FC AugsburgBundesliga07.11 14:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Leipzig lost 3-2 to Man City after equalising twice
  • Augsburg: six league games without a win
  • Augsburg keep conceding late

RB Leipzig came back twice against Manchester City on Wednesday but still lost 3-2, Erling Haaland settling it in the 78th minute. "It's not enough to be close," said Christoph Baumgartner afterwards, and Ridle Baku agreed that small details decide games at that level. In the league, Leipzig had won 2-0 at Schalke last weekend.

Augsburg arrive with their bright start to the season fading. They have gone six Bundesliga games without a win, and kicker points to a recurring problem: leads lost to late goals. Last weekend they were beaten 2-1 at home by Freiburg, after a 1-1 draw with Union Berlin; their only recent win was 4-2 in the cup at Sonnenhof Großaspach.

“It's not enough to be close.”Christoph Baumgartner, RB Leipzig

Saturday's game in Leipzig kicks off at 15:30 local time.
