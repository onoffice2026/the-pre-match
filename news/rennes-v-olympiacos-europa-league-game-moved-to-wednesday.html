Rennes v Olympiacos: Europa League game moved to Wednesday

Two unbeaten sides in the Europa League; Rennes come in after a 5-2 defeat at Nice.

Stade Rennais – OlympiacosEuropa League15:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Moved to Wednesday
  • Olympiacos: 8 straight wins
  • Rennes lost 2-5 at Nice

The Europa League game between Stade Rennais and Olympiacos has been brought forward from Thursday to Wednesday, 4 November.

Both teams are unbeaten in the competition. Olympiacos have won eight in a row. Rennes, however, were beaten 5-2 at Nice on Sunday, with Elye Wahi scoring a hat-trick.
