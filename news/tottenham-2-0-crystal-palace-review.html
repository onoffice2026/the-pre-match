Tottenham 2-0 Crystal Palace: Spurs win three in a row against ten men

An early red card for Jean-Philippe Mateta left Palace chasing shadows as Archie Gray and Jan Paul van Hecke headed Tottenham to victory.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Tottenham Hotspur – Crystal PalacePremier League17:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Archie Gray 25', Jan Paul van Hecke 73'
  • Jean-Philippe Mateta sent off on 13' after a VAR review
  • Tottenham: 34 shots and 19 corners; Palace: 2 shots

Tottenham Hotspur won three Premier League matches in a row for the first time in 20 months, beating ten-man Crystal Palace 2-0 at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

The match turned in the 13th minute. Jean-Philippe Mateta caught Micky van de Ven on the Achilles with a studs-up challenge, and referee Michael Oliver upgraded his yellow card to a red after a VAR review.

Spurs went ahead in the 25th minute. Mohammed Kudus swung a corner to the back post and Archie Gray headed in his third goal for the club.

Palace goalkeeper Dean Henderson kept the score down with a string of saves, but he could not stop the second in the 73rd minute. Jan Paul van Hecke's header from a corner was tipped onto the crossbar and the defender bundled in the rebound.

Tottenham finished with 79.4 per cent of the ball, 34 shots and 19 corners, while Palace managed only two attempts. Roberto De Zerbi also handed a league debut to teenager Luca Williams-Barnett, and the defeat will add to the pressure on Palace boss Pierre Sage.
