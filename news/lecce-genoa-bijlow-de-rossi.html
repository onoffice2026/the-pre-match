Lecce v Genoa: De Rossi's Genoa ride a Bijlow wave

Genoa beat Juventus 2-0 in midweek, with goalkeeper Justin Bijlow praised by Daniele De Rossi. Lecce are trying to recover from a 5-1 defeat.

Key points
  • Genoa beat Juventus 2-0 in midweek.
  • Justin Bijlow made five saves in the 1-0 win over Venezia.
  • Lecce lost 5-1 at Frosinone.

Genoa travel to Lecce full of confidence after beating Juventus 2-0 in midweek, a result that deepened the crisis in Turin.

Goalkeeper Justin Bijlow is at the heart of their good run. He made five saves in a 1-0 win over Venezia despite concerns about back problems, and coach Daniele De Rossi called him 'very important'. If nothing unusual happens, the Dutchman will make his tenth appearance of the season this weekend.

“Very important.”Daniele De Rossi on goalkeeper Justin Bijlow

Lecce are looking for a reaction after a heavy 5-1 defeat at promoted Frosinone, in which Swiss international Joel Monteiro scored his first Serie A goal - a goal he could barely celebrate.

With confidence on one side and repairs needed on the other, Genoa will start as the team in form.
