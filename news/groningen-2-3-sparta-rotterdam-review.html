Groningen 2-3 Sparta Rotterdam: Kostic long-range winner

Andrej Kostic struck from outside the box in the 85th minute as Sparta won a five-goal game at the Euroborg despite having eight shots.

FC Groningen – Sparta RotterdamEredivisie15:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Ondrejka 1', Van Bergen 29' (Groningen); Hatenboer 15', Zonneveld 25', Kostic 85' (Sparta)
  • Sparta goalkeeper: 8 saves
  • Groningen: 20 shots, 10 on target

Sparta Rotterdam won 3-2 at FC Groningen in the Eredivisie, scoring a late winner after a lively first half that produced four goals.

Groningen led inside the first minute when Jacob Ondrejka finished into the top right corner after a pass from Brynjólfur Willumsson. Sparta levelled in the 15th minute through Cedric Hatenboer, set up by Marvin Young's through ball.

Milan Zonneveld put Sparta ahead in the 25th minute, but four minutes later Thom van Bergen equalised for Groningen after a through ball from Hans Hateboer.

The second half was goalless until the 85th minute, when Andrej Kostic shot from outside the box into the centre of the goal to win it for Sparta.

Groningen had 20 shots and ten on target, but the Sparta goalkeeper made eight saves. Sparta scored three goals from four shots on target.
