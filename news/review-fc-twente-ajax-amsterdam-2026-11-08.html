Weghorst rescues Twente in the 95th minute against Ajax

Ajax led 2-0 with Oscar Gloukh assisting both goals; an own goal and a stoppage-time penalty earned Twente a 2-2 draw.

Photo: tpower1978 (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
FC Twente – Ajax AmsterdamEredivisie13:30 UTC

Key points
  • Gloukh two assists
  • Twente 22 shots vs 6
  • Weghorst penalty 90+5'

Ajax struck after three minutes: Oscar Gloukh crossed and Tolu Arokodare headed in. Rayane Bounida doubled the lead from outside the box in the 66th minute, again set up by Gloukh.

Twente replied within two minutes through a Dies Janse own goal, and kept pressing. Their pressure was relentless - 22 shots and nine corners against six shots for Ajax.

It paid off in the fifth minute of stoppage time, when Wout Weghorst converted a penalty to share the points.
