AEK v Real Madrid: Mourinho's winning machine arrives in Athens

Real Madrid have won five in a row and come to Greece without fear; AEK lose João Mário to pneumonia.

Key points
  • Real Madrid: five wins in a row, 17 goals scored
  • AEK: beaten 5-1 at Man City and 3-1 at Shakhtar
  • João Mário out for AEK with pneumonia

Real Madrid travel to Athens on a run of five straight wins in all competitions, including a 2-1 Clásico victory away to Barcelona and a 4-1 home win over RB Leipzig in the Champions League. José Mourinho's side scored 17 goals in those five games.

AEK's form is uneven. They beat Panetolikos 5-0 and OFI Crete 3-0 at home, but lost 2-0 at Panathinaikos on Sunday and were beaten 5-1 at Manchester City and 3-1 at Shakhtar in Europe.

The hosts will be without midfielder João Mário, who was taken to hospital with pneumonia; the AEK coach confirmed the absence on the eve of the game.

For Madrid, Eduardo Camavinga spoke before the trip about a difficult summer of exit rumours, saying he had reflected a lot and that Mourinho had given him great confidence.
