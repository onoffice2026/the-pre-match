Gil Vicente v Vitória de Guimarães: Luís Pinto faces his former club

Gil Vicente coach Luís Pinto meets the side he won the Allianz Cup with, and two key players could return to his team.

Photo: JP Ribeiro · CC BY-SA 4.0
Gil Vicente – Vitória de GuimaraesPrimeira Liga20:15 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Luís Pinto meets the club he won the Allianz Cup with
  • Two key players close to a return for Gil Vicente
  • Tickets: 40-60 euros

Gil Vicente host Vitória de Guimarães on Monday night, a reunion for coach Luís Pinto, who won the Allianz Cup with Vitória, Record reported.

Pinto has more options for the game: according to Record, two important players are close to a return to the starting line-up.

Left-back David Moreira has also earned a place in Cape Verde's preliminary squad after impressing for Gil Vicente.

Tickets for the game went on sale at prices between 40 and 60 euros.
