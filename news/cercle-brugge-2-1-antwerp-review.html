Match review

Cercle Brugge 2-1 Antwerp: Akinsola double after the break

Babatunde Akinsola scored twice in the second half and Cercle held on after Modibo Fofana's late goal for Antwerp.

Photo: Валерий Дед · CC BY 3.0
Cercle Brugge KSV – AntwerpBelgian Pro League12:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Akinsola 48', 61' (Cercle); Fofana 81' (Antwerp)
  • Antwerp: 60% possession, 5 yellow cards
  • Cercle: 5 shots on target from 10

Cercle Brugge beat Antwerp 2-1 at the Jan Breydel Stadium in the Belgian Pro League, with Babatunde Akinsola scoring both goals.

The first half ended goalless. Three minutes after the restart Akinsola gave Cercle the lead, in the 48th minute, and he doubled it in the 61st minute.

Antwerp, who had 60 per cent of the possession, pulled one back through Modibo Fofana in the 81st minute, but could not find an equaliser.

Cercle had ten shots, five of them on target, while Antwerp had 15 shots with four on target. Both sides won four corners.

Antwerp received five yellow cards and committed 15 fouls, against eight for Cercle, who were not booked. The visitors completed 383 passes to Cercle's 219, while Cercle made 19 tackles to defend their lead.
