Villarreal v Paris Saint-Germain: the champions on nine straight wins

PSG arrive after a 5-1 win at Le Havre; Felix Zwayer will referee the game.

Key points
  • PSG: nine wins in a row
  • Hakimi scored a hat-trick at Le Havre
  • Referee: Felix Zwayer

Paris Saint-Germain travel to Villarreal on a run of nine straight wins. They beat Le Havre 5-1 on Friday, with Achraf Hakimi scoring a hat-trick and adding two assists, and Luis Enrique is now the coach with the most wins in the club's history.

The PSG players rested on Sunday after a Halloween dinner, L'Équipe reports. German referee Felix Zwayer has been appointed for the match.

Villarreal lost 1-0 at home to Espanyol on Friday. Midfielder Pape Gueye has said he wants to win the African Ballon d'Or.
