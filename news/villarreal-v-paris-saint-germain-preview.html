Villarreal v PSG: Luis Enrique expected to bring back his regulars

PSG rested several starters in a 5-1 win at Le Havre, and Felix Zwayer will referee Tuesday's game at La Cerámica.

Photo: Oldelpaso · CC BY-SA 3.0
Villarreal – Paris Saint-GermainChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • PSG won 5-1 at Le Havre with several regulars rested
  • Hakimi scored a hat-trick
  • Referee: Felix Zwayer (Germany)

Paris Saint-Germain head to Villarreal for the fourth round of the Champions League league phase on Tuesday night. Luis Enrique left several regular starters on the bench in Friday's 5-1 win at Le Havre, but L'Equipe expects him to field his leading players in Spain, with Désiré Doué in attack.

Achraf Hakimi scored a hat-trick in Normandy, Voetbal International reported. The defeat cost Le Havre coach Didier Digard his job the next day.

Villarreal lost at home to Espanyol, and defender Pau Navarro later apologised for comments after that defeat, saying he would like to see the stadium always full.

UEFA has appointed the experienced German referee Felix Zwayer, 45, for the match.
