Willem II face PEC with a 42-year-old record looming

The promoted side are bottom with two points from eleven games and have gone 28 Eredivisie matches without a win; Helmond Sport's record is 30.

Photo: Maarten van Esch · Public domain
Willem II – PEC ZwolleEredivisie06.11 19:00 UTC
Key points
  • Willem II: 28 Eredivisie games without a win; record is 30
  • 1-6 v Cambuur, 0-7 at PSV in the last two
  • PEC beat ADO 1-0 but are without Kostons

Willem II's return to the Eredivisie has turned into a fight with history. The bottom club have two points from eleven matches, and they have now gone 28 Eredivisie games without a win – 22 defeats and six draws since January 2025. The record, held by Helmond Sport since 1983-84, is 30.

The slide has already cost John Stegeman his job, and the last two results were brutal: 1-6 at home to Cambuur and 0-7 at PSV. Streppel, speaking after the PSV game, criticised his defence for standing too high and failing at set pieces – and answered a question about solutions with a sarcastic 'paintball and stone grilling'.

PEC Zwolle are not in great shape either – they too changed coach this autumn and went out of the cup to TOP Oss – but they beat ADO Den Haag 1-0 last weekend. They will be without attacker Koen Kostons, out for a longer period.

Our take: PEC are the stronger side on form, and Willem II's confidence is at zero. But the home side know a win would stop the countdown, and PEC without Kostons lack a cutting edge. Expect a nervous game – PEC slight favourites.
