Erzurum BB 1-3 Çorum FK: Ramírez double earns away win

Jesús Ramírez scored twice, once from the penalty spot, as Çorum FK won in Erzurum.

Photo: Prime Minister's Office from Greece · CC BY-SA 2.0

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Söyüncü 20', Ramírez 71', 79' pen (Çorum); Diabate 74' (Erzurum)
  • Erzurum: 8 corners, Çorum: 0
  • 32 fouls in total

Çorum FK won 3-1 away at Erzurum BB in the Turkish top flight, in a match the forecasts had seen as close to even.

Çağlar Söyüncü gave the visitors the lead in the 20th minute.

Jesús Ramírez doubled it in the 71st minute. Ibrahim Diabate pulled one back for Erzurum three minutes later, but Ramírez restored the two-goal margin from the penalty spot in the 79th minute.

Erzurum had 52.4 per cent of the ball, 13 shots and eight corners, but managed only three shots on target. Çorum took seven shots, three on target, and won no corners at all.

It was a physical game, with 32 fouls in total.
