Pressure grows at La Rosaleda as Málaga face Racing without Andrés Martín

Málaga are on the second-worst start in their history; Racing lose their top scorer after a 3-0 defeat by Real Madrid.

Málaga – Racing SantanderLaLiga13:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Málaga: second-worst start in club history
  • Racing without Andrés Martín (18 goals in a year)
  • Racing lost 3-0 to Real Madrid last time out

The numbers are weighing on Málaga. According to Marca, Funes's side are on the second-worst start to a season in the club's history, and graffiti appeared at La Rosaleda last weekend in protest at the situation. Their latest defeat came at Real Betis, 2-1, after which Betis coach Manuel Pellegrini said the newly promoted club should have bet more on experience to avoid suffering so much in the top flight, although he also believes they will stay up.

Racing Santander, also back in LaLiga this season, have had a better start, with a 2-1 home win over Espanyol among their results. But they lost 3-0 to Real Madrid last Sunday and will now be without Andrés Martín, who is out long-term. He has scored 18 goals in the last year, more than a fifth of the team's total.

This is a meeting of two promoted sides at 14:00 local time on Saturday, and for Málaga it already looks like a game they cannot afford to lose.
