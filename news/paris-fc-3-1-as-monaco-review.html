Paris FC 3-1 Monaco: Sinayoko scores twice and sets up the third

Lassine Sinayoko struck twice before half-time and assisted Pablo Pagis as Paris FC beat Monaco at the Stade Jean Bouin.

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Sinayoko 38', 44', Pagis 74' (Paris FC); Balogun 65' (Monaco)
  • Sinayoko: two goals and one assist
  • Paris FC: 10 shots on target

Paris FC beat AS Monaco 3-1 at the Stade Jean Bouin, with Lassine Sinayoko involved in all three home goals.

Sinayoko opened the scoring in the 38th minute, finishing into the bottom left corner after a pass from Maxime Lopez. Six minutes later he added a second, running onto Moses Simon's through ball and finishing into the bottom right corner.

Monaco pulled one back in the 65th minute, when Folarin Balogun finished from very close range after a cross from Vanderson.

Paris FC restored the two-goal margin in the 74th minute: Sinayoko turned provider and Pablo Pagis scored from the centre of the box.

Monaco had 55.6 per cent of the ball, but Paris FC were far more dangerous, with 19 shots and ten on target against Monaco's nine and two. Monaco's goalkeeper made seven saves.
