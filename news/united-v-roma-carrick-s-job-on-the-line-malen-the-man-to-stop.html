United v Roma: Carrick's job on the line, Malen the man to stop

Manchester United have gone nine games without a win, their worst run in 38 years; Roma arrive as favourites at Old Trafford for the first time.

Manchester United – AS RomaChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • United: 9 without a win, 3 straight defeats
  • Martínez expected to start after his red card at Chelsea
  • Malen: top scorer in Europe's big five leagues in 2026
  • Roma have lost 4 out of 4 at Old Trafford

Michael Carrick says he is not afraid of losing his job, even after the 2-0 defeat at Chelsea extended United's winless run to nine games. The club had earlier given him more time, but the pressure has only grown before Roma's visit.

Lisandro Martínez is expected to keep his place despite his red card at Stamford Bridge and his public criticism of football director Jason Wilcox, for which he was fined. Carrick says the defender's commitment is not in question.

Roma's danger man is Donyell Malen, the top scorer in Europe's major leagues in 2026, who returns to England transformed under Gian Piero Gasperini. Gasperini, who admits Roma are short in midfield, is playing the game down.

History favours United: Roma have lost all four of their visits to Old Trafford, including the 7-1 in 2007. Form does not: Roma are unbeaten in five, United have lost their last three.
