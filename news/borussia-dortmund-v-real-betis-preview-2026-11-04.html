Dortmund v Betis: Pellegrini chases a fourth Champions League win

Betis have won all three Champions League games; 6,300 fans travel to the Signal Iduna Park.

Photo: Arne Müseler / www.arne-mueseler.com (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 3.0
Borussia Dortmund – Real BetisChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Betis: three wins from three in the Champions League
  • Dortmund: unbeaten in five, 2-2 at Bayern
  • 6,300 Betis fans travelling

Real Betis can come close to sealing qualification with a fourth straight Champions League win. Manuel Pellegrini said his side has 'the personality to go out and win in Dortmund', and centre-back Diego Llorente believes the squad can compete with anyone.

Around 6,300 Betis supporters are expected in Dortmund, one of the biggest away followings in the club's history outside a final. Dani Ceballos and Antony, both with past wins over Dortmund, are seen as lucky charms.

“We have the personality to go out and win in Dortmund.”Manuel Pellegrini

Dortmund are unbeaten in five, including a 2-2 draw at Bayern and a 3-2 win over Werder Bremen. Niko Kovač's side face two games before the international break. Former Dortmund defender Marc Bartra returns with Betis.
