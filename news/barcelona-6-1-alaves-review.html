Match review

Barcelona 6-1 Alavés: Raphinha and Lamine Yamal both score twice

The league leaders bounced back from their defeat to Real Madrid in style, scoring four times before half-time at Camp Nou.

Photo: Paolo Costa Baldi · CC BY-SA 3.0
Barcelona – AlavésLaLiga20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Raphinha 8', 41'; Fermín López 23'; Yamal 45+1', 64'; Gordon 81' (Barcelona); Mariano Díaz 84' (Alavés)
  • Fermín López: one goal and two assists
  • Barcelona: 77.1% possession

Barcelona responded to their first dropped points of the season, a 2-1 defeat to Real Madrid, with a 6-1 win over Alavés in front of 53,523 at Camp Nou.

Fermín López was the driving force early on. He set up Raphinha for the opener in the 8th minute and then scored himself in the 23rd minute, rifling a shot from a tight angle on the right into the top left corner after a pass from Lamine Yamal.

López also supplied the through ball for Raphinha's second in the 41st minute, and Yamal made it 4-0 in first-half stoppage time with a first-time strike from outside the box, set up by João Cancelo.

Yamal added his second in the 64th minute, a delicate finish from Gabriel Jesus's pass that was first flagged offside and then awarded after a VAR review. Substitute Anthony Gordon scored his first league goal for the club in the 81st minute, running onto a through ball from Rodri.

Mariano Díaz pulled one back for Alavés in the 84th minute. Barcelona finished with 77.1 per cent of the ball despite missing several first-team players, among them Jules Koundé, Dani Olmo and Andreas Christensen.
