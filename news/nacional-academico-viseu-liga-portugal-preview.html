Académico de Viseu take a six-game winning run to Madeira

The Viriatos came from behind to beat Rio Ave for their sixth straight win; Nacional lost narrowly at Arouca.

Photo: Not stated · Public domain
C.D. Nacional – Académico de ViseuPrimeira Liga07.11 15:30 UTC
Key points
  • Académico de Viseu: six straight wins
  • They won 2-1 at Sporting CP during the run
  • Nacional lost 1-0 at Arouca last time out

Académico de Viseu are one of the stories of the Portuguese season. Last weekend's 2-1 home win over Rio Ave was their sixth in a row: Rio Ave led at half-time, but Messeguem and Clóvis turned the game around after the break. The run includes a 2-1 win at Sporting CP and identical 2-1 wins over Estoril and Rio Ave.

Nacional have been less consistent. They beat Braga 1-0 at home two weeks ago but lost 1-0 at Arouca last Sunday, after earlier defeats at Rio Ave (2-3) and at home to Famalicão (0-4). Saturday's game in Madeira kicks off at 15:30 local time.

The build-up in Portugal this week has been dominated by Cristiano Ronaldo, 41, who announced the end of his international career after 146 goals for Portugal; national coach Jorge Jesus was among those to pay tribute.
