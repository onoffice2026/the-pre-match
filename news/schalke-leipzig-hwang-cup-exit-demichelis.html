Schalke v Leipzig: Hwang outburst overshadows Schalke's cup exit

Schalke were knocked out of the cup by second-tier Dynamo Dresden. Leipzig, under pressure Martin Demichelis, arrive after a hard-fought cup win at Essen.

Photo: Q1q2q3qwertz · CC BY-SA 4.0
Schalke 04 – RB LeipzigBundesliga14:30 UTC
Key points
  • Schalke lost 2-0 to Dynamo Dresden in the DFB-Pokal.
  • Hwang Hee-chan pushed the fourth official and a physio; the club will deal with it internally.
  • Marco Rose has defended Leipzig coach Martin Demichelis.

Schalke go into Saturday's game against RB Leipzig after an embarrassing week. They were knocked out in the second round of the DFB-Pokal, losing 2-0 away to second-division Dynamo Dresden.

The defeat was overshadowed by forward Hwang Hee-chan, who lost his temper before half-time after a challenge went unpunished, pushing the fourth official and the club's own physio. He was only booked, but coach Miron Muslič and the club say they will deal with the incident internally.

Leipzig reached the next round of the cup after a tough game at Rot-Weiss Essen, where the hosts could be proud despite the defeat. In the league, Leipzig had earlier given promoted Elversberg their first heavy beating of the season.

There is debate around Leipzig coach Martin Demichelis, and former RB coach Marco Rose, now at Bournemouth, has come to his defence.
