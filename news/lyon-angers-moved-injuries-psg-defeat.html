Lyon v Angers: moved for the national day of protest, with injuries piling up

The fixture was rescheduled at the request of the authorities. Lyon return to action after a 2-0 defeat at PSG and a growing injury list.

Lyon – AngersLigue 116:15 UTC
Key points
  • The match was moved because of the national day of protest on 31 October.
  • Lyon lost 2-0 to PSG; substitutes Mayulu and Godts scored.
  • Openda and Sulc are injured; Tolisso has a minor problem.

Lyon's home game against Angers is one of several Ligue 1 fixtures moved at the request of the authorities, because of the third national day of social mobilisation planned across France on Saturday 31 October.

Lyon come into it after losing 2-0 to Paris Saint-Germain in a rescheduled top-of-the-table game, decided by two substitutes: Senny Mayulu opened the scoring and Mika Godts, who also cleared off the line, added the second. The win took PSG top.

The injury news keeps coming for the hosts. After Loïs Openda was hurt against Lens earlier in the month, the club announced that Pavel Sulc is also injured, and Corentin Tolisso has picked up a minor injury.

Angers will hope to take advantage of a Lyon side with fewer options than usual.
