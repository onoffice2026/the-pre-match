Óskarsson goal is enough for Real Sociedad

Ander Barrenetxea's through ball, Orri Óskarsson's finish: 1-0 at the Reale Arena.

Photo: vecinodelquinto! (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Real Sociedad – Rayo VallecanoLaLiga08.11 15:15 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Óskarsson 67'
  • 3 shots on target each

A balanced match was decided in the 67th minute, when Orri Óskarsson ran onto Ander Barrenetxea's through ball and finished into the bottom right corner.

Real Sociedad had 17 shots to Rayo's 11, but only three on target each; Rayo could not find a way back.
