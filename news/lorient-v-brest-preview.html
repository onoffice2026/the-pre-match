Match preview

Lorient v Brest: the postponed Breton derby

The derby moved because of last weekend's protests now goes ahead on Monday, with Lorient unbeaten in three.

Lorient – BrestLigue 119:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Derby postponed from last weekend
  • Lorient unbeaten in three
  • Lorient without Bamba Dieng

The Breton derby between Lorient and Brest, postponed last weekend because of nationwide protests, takes place on Monday.

Lorient are unbeaten in three, with 1-0 wins over Monaco and Paris FC and a goalless draw at Angers, although they are still without striker Bamba Dieng, who has 15 goals in the last 365 days.

Brest lost 2-1 at home to Nice in their last game. They took the lead through a goalkeeping error by Yehvann Diouf, but Nice came back and Brest missed a penalty in stoppage time.
