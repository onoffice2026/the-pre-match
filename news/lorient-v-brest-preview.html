Match preview

Lorient v Brest: the postponed Breton derby

The derby was postponed during the eighth round of Ligue 1, and Brest come in after a home defeat to Nice.

Lorient – BrestLigue 119:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Derby postponed from the eighth round
  • Brest lost 2-1 at home to Nice
  • Lala missed a stoppage-time penalty for Brest

Lorient and Brest meet in the Breton derby on Monday night. The game was postponed during the eighth round of Ligue 1, when matches were moved because of the mobilisations in France, Foot Mercato reported.

Brest lost 2-1 at home to Nice in their last league game on 23 October. They took the lead through a big mistake by Nice goalkeeper Yehvann Diouf, but Sofiane Diop won it for Nice in the 83rd minute and Brest's Lala missed a penalty in stoppage time.

Lorient have been without forward Bamba Dieng since August. He scored 15 goals in the last year, more than a quarter of the team's total.
