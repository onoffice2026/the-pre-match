Match review

Borussia Mönchengladbach 0-0 SC Paderborn: Stalemate at Borussia-Park

Neither side found a way through in an evenly matched goalless draw in Mönchengladbach.

Photo: Marcel Meier · CC BY-SA 2.0
Borussia Mönchengladbach – SC Paderborn 07Bundesliga14:30 UTC
Key points
  • No goals; four shots on target each
  • Five yellow cards, three of them for Paderborn
  • Paderborn had 57% possession and 7 corners

Borussia Mönchengladbach and SC Paderborn shared a goalless draw at Borussia-Park in a contest that offered few clear openings.

The visitors had more of the ball, with 57 per cent possession, and edged the shot count 13 to 12. Each side managed four shots on target and each goalkeeper made four saves.

Paderborn also won more corners, seven to Gladbach's four, but could not turn their territorial advantage into a goal. Gladbach, for their part, were more economical with the ball and matched the visitors for efforts on target without ever finding the decisive touch.

The game was competitive rather than spectacular, with 19 fouls and five yellow cards. Tom Baack was booked for Paderborn in the first half, followed by Daiki Hashioka and Fabio Chiarodia for the hosts, before Tjark Scheller and Laurin Ulrich were cautioned late on.
