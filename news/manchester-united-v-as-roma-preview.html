Manchester United v Roma: Carrick's crisis meets Gasperini's unbeaten side

United are winless in nine and have lost three in a row; Roma are unbeaten in Serie A.

Photo: Arne Müseler · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Manchester United – AS RomaChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • United winless in nine
  • Roma unbeaten in Serie A
  • Roma: 18 goals in five games

Manchester United host Roma in deep trouble. They are without a win in nine matches in all competitions and lost 2-0 at Chelsea on Saturday, their third defeat in a row. The Guardian describes Michael Carrick as the latest manager to suffer at the club.

Roma are flying. Gian Piero Gasperini's side won 5-2 at Udinese after leading 4-0 at half-time and remain unbeaten after ten Serie A rounds, L'Équipe reports.

Roma have scored 18 goals in their last five matches.
