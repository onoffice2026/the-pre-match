Match preview

Feyenoord v Inter: Chivu back in De Kuip after the derby defeat

Inter lost the Milan derby 3-1 and arrive with injuries; Feyenoord get Van Persie and Nelson back but lose St. Juste.

Photo: huphtur (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 2.0
Feyenoord Rotterdam – InternazionaleChampions League20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Inter lost the derby 1-3 after four straight wins
  • Feyenoord: Van Persie and Nelson back, St. Juste out
  • Referee: Glenn Nyberg (Sweden)

Cristian Chivu returns to a ground he knows from his Ajax days, and was asked more about Ajax than about Feyenoord on Monday. His focus, he said, is only on Tuesday: Inter are still in the Champions League race and he has full confidence in his players.

The reigning Italian champions come in after a 3-1 derby defeat by AC Milan, which Italian media say exposed injuries and individual interests in the squad. Before the derby they had won four in a row.

Giovanni van Bronckhorst has mixed news: Shaqueel van Persie and Reiss Nelson return, but Jeremiah St. Juste will be out for some time. The coach warned that Feyenoord's earlier home win over Como guarantees nothing against Inter.

Swedish referee Glenn Nyberg takes charge; Dutch sides usually do well when he officiates. Feyenoord have drawn their last two league games.
