Manchester City 0-1 Brighton: Ayari strike sinks the leaders at the Etihad

Yasin Ayari's early finish gave Brighton revenge for their midweek cup exit and handed Manchester City a second straight league defeat.

Photo: Christian David · CC BY-SA 4.0
Manchester City – Brighton & Hove AlbionPremier League15:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goal: Yasin Ayari 11'
  • Manchester City had 21 shots, only 6 on target
  • City's second straight league defeat after seven opening wins

Brighton took swift revenge for their Carabao Cup shoot-out defeat, beating Premier League leaders Manchester City 1-0 at the Etihad Stadium only days after losing at the same venue.

The decisive moment came in the 11th minute. Lewis Dunk released Diego Gómez down the right from a free-kick, Matheus Nunes failed to cut out the low cross, and the ball ran off Maxim De Cuyper for Yasin Ayari to finish with his left foot. It was a neat piece of redemption for Ayari, who had missed a penalty in midweek.

City responded with long spells of pressure and finished with 62.4 per cent of the ball, 21 shots and eight corners, yet only six of those efforts were on target. Iliman Ndiaye was their most dangerous outlet: he headed one chance wide, saw Bart Verbruggen tip a powerful drive onto the post and later found the side-netting.

Brighton defended deep and with conviction, picking up three yellow cards as the pressure built. Late efforts from Nico O'Reilly and Rayan Cherki went close, but Verbruggen and his back line held firm.

The result is City's second successive Premier League defeat after a perfect start of seven wins, and it leaves Fabian Hürzeler's side just two points off the top. Arsenal now have the chance to move above City when they visit Liverpool on Sunday.
