Deportivo v Osasuna: Arrasate promises changes

Jagoba Arrasate expects an Osasuna side "faithful to its identity" at Riazor on Monday, after a 2-0 defeat at Sevilla.

Key points
  • Arrasate: "There will be changes"
  • Osasuna lost 2-0 at Sevilla last time out in the league
  • Deportivo: one point from their last three league games

Osasuna travel to A Coruña for Monday night's game at Riazor, and coach Jagoba Arrasate has warned that there will be changes. He told Mundo Deportivo on Sunday that he expects to see "a team faithful to its signs of identity", the qualities seen in his first match in charge against Racing Santander.

That first game ended in a 2-0 home win over Racing, but Osasuna then lost 2-0 at Sevilla before winning 6-0 at Atlético Calatayud in midweek.

Deportivo have also struggled for league wins. Their last three league games brought a 3-2 defeat at Real Sociedad, a 1-1 draw with Levante and a 1-0 loss at Atlético Madrid, before a 3-0 win at Auriense in midweek.
