Match review

Hull City 2-2 Ipswich Town: Enciso salvages a point at the MKM

Hull fought back from a goal down to lead through Oliver McBurnie's free-kick, only for Julio Enciso to equalise six minutes later.

Photo: NONONSENSEDEFENDING · CC BY-SA 4.0
Hull City – Ipswich TownPremier League15:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Iroegbunam 58', McBurnie 68' (free-kick) (Hull); Florentino 22', Enciso 74' (Ipswich)
  • Enciso's first-half header disallowed for a foul
  • Three shots on target each; Ipswich 61.5% possession

Hull City kept their unbeaten home record but extended their winless run after a see-saw 2-2 draw with Ipswich Town at the MKM Stadium.

Ipswich had the better of the first half and went ahead in the 22nd minute, when Abdoul Ouattara's through ball sent Florentino clear on the right and he finished from a tight angle into the top right corner. Enciso thought he had doubled the lead with a header from a corner, but it was ruled out for a foul on McBurnie.

Hull improved after the break. Tim Iroegbunam raced down the right and fired in the equaliser in the 58th minute, with McBurnie supplying the assist.

Ten minutes later McBurnie turned scorer, driving a free-kick into the bottom right corner to put the Tigers in front in the 68th minute.

The lead lasted only six minutes. Julio Enciso worked his way through the Hull back line and fired a left-footed shot from outside the box into the top left corner. Ipswich finished with 61.5 per cent of possession, though both sides managed only three shots on target, and six yellow cards were shared evenly.
