Barcelona v Alavés: Flick considers Frenkie de Jong in defence amid injury crisis

With defenders missing, Hansi Flick is ready to move Frenkie de Jong back into the defensive line - and has made it clear that Alejandro Balde is out of favour.

Key points
  • Barcelona lost El Clásico 2-1 at home and have dropped points against the top sides.
  • Flick sees Frenkie de Jong as an option at centre-back.
  • Alejandro Balde is out of favour: 'He lacks self-confidence', says Flick.

Barcelona return to league action after a bruising run against the top sides, including a 2-1 home defeat to Real Madrid in El Clásico. Hansi Flick has made clear he will not change his approach, but the injury list is forcing creative solutions.

The most striking one involves Frenkie de Jong. The Dutchman returned in El Clásico as a number 10 after his knee injury, and Flick now openly sees him as an option at centre-back if the situation demands it, calling him 'fantastic'.

“He lacks self-confidence.”Hansi Flick on Alejandro Balde

De Jong himself is relaxed about it, up to a point. Speaking about his chances in an overcrowded midfield, he said he is ready to help in defence if needed - but wants there to be no misunderstanding: he is a midfielder.

Flick was less warm about Alejandro Balde. 'He lacks self-confidence,' the German said of the full-back, who is not expected to play a role for the time being.

Alavés come to Barcelona after a performance described in Spain as the worst of the Quique era, rescued at the end by academy player Mañas, who scored his first top-flight goal. Barcelona will expect to dominate - the question is who lines up in their back four.
