PSV Eindhoven 7-0 Willem II: seven different scorers in Eindhoven rout

PSV scored five times after half-time, and three of their seven goals came from corners, as Willem II were swept aside at the Philips Stadion.

PSV Eindhoven – Willem IIEredivisie17:45 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Mauro Júnior 20', Til 44', Mijnans 50', Van Bommel 53', Pepi 73', Sano 77', Cornecion 81'
  • Three PSV goals came from corners
  • Willem II: 4 shots, 1 on target

PSV Eindhoven overwhelmed Willem II 7-0 at the Philips Stadion, with seven different players getting on the scoresheet.

Mauro Júnior opened the scoring in the 20th minute with a right-footed finish from the centre of the box, and Guus Til doubled the lead just before half-time, turning in from close range after Armando Obispo's corner.

The second half was one-way traffic. Sven Mijnans struck into the top right corner in the 50th minute and then set up Ruben van Bommel's header from a corner three minutes later.

Ricardo Pepi headed in the fifth in the 73rd minute from Esmir Bajraktarevic's corner, and then teed up Kodai Sano for the sixth. Shuryjano Cornecion completed the rout in the 81st minute with a shot from a tight angle on the right.

PSV had 70 per cent of the ball, 20 shots and 11 corners, while Willem II managed just four attempts and one on target.
