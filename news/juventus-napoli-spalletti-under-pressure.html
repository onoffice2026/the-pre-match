Juventus v Napoli: Spalletti under pressure, Allegri short at the back

Two defeats in three matches have put Luciano Spalletti's position in question before Sunday's meeting with Napoli, who travel without Badiashile and Spinazzola but with Amir Rrahmani back.

Juventus – NapoliSerie A19:45 UTC
Key points
  • Juventus lost 2-0 at Genoa - their second defeat in three games.
  • Napoli are without Badiashile and Spinazzola; Rrahmani returns.
  • Napoli drew 3-3 with Roma last weekend after leading twice.

Juventus head into Sunday's meeting with Napoli in a familiar state of tension. The 2-0 defeat at Genoa was their second loss in three matches, and the Italian and German press have focused on underlying numbers described as alarming: little attacking threat and a defence that keeps opening up.

Luciano Spalletti did not hide from it. After the Genoa game the coach was openly critical of his team, and Voetbal International described Juventus as 'humiliated' and plunged into a new crisis - with a European tie against AZ coming the following week.

The forward line is a particular concern. The Italian press has named Randal Kolo Muani as the next player likely to lose his place under Spalletti, with reports that the club intends to sign a new striker in January.

Napoli, coached by Massimiliano Allegri, arrive with problems of their own. Defender Benoît Badiashile has a thigh (rectus femoris) injury and Leonardo Spinazzola a hamstring problem, both in the middle of a heavy run of fixtures, although Amir Rrahmani is available again.

There is noise off the pitch too. Aurelio De Laurentiis has said publicly that the club is for sale, a message that has dominated the conversation in Naples. On the field, Napoli let a two-goal lead slip in a 3-3 draw with Roma last weekend - another sign that this match is far from predictable.
