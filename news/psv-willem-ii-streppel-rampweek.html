PSV v Willem II: Streppel returns to a club with 'very little to hold on to'

Bottom of the Eredivisie with two points from ten games and fresh from a 1-6 home defeat, Willem II travel to PSV under a familiar coach: Jurgen Streppel.

Photo: Dutchlad1985 · CC BY-SA 4.0
PSV Eindhoven – Willem IIEredivisie17:45 UTC
Key points
  • Willem II have two points from ten Eredivisie games.
  • They lost 1-6 at home to SC Cambuur last weekend.
  • Jurgen Streppel returns as head coach, succeeding John Stegeman.

Willem II arrive at the Philips Stadion in the deepest trouble of any Eredivisie side. After ten rounds they have just two points, and last weekend fellow promoted club SC Cambuur beat them 6-1 in Tilburg - equalling the biggest Eredivisie win in Cambuur's history.

Captain Hidde ter Avest did not sugar-coat it after what Voetbal International called a 'week of disaster': 'There is really very little to hold on to.'

“There is really very little to hold on to.”Hidde ter Avest, Willem II captain

The club has turned to a familiar face. More than ten years after leaving, Jurgen Streppel, 57, is back as head coach, succeeding John Stegeman with the task of keeping the bottom side in the division. Streppel describes himself as a man who follows his feelings and admits the job is a very heavy one.

Pundits on VI's Rondje Eredivisie debated whether relegation can still be avoided, with the view that raising the white flag after ten games would be very early.

A first assignment away to PSV could hardly be tougher.
