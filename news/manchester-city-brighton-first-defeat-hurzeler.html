Man City v Brighton: leaders respond to first defeat as Hürzeler's side regroup

Manchester City dropped their first league points of the season at Aston Villa. Brighton come to the Etihad after a 2-0 loss at Anfield that left Fabian Hürzeler taking a swipe at Liverpool.

Photo: Arne Müseler, cropped by Blackcat · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Manchester City – Brighton & Hove AlbionPremier League15:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • City lost 2-1 at Aston Villa - their first league defeat of the season.
  • Brighton lost 2-0 at Anfield; Hürzeler said only 'one team' tried to create.
  • Iraola called Brighton 'excellently coached'.

Manchester City return to the Etihad after losing for the first time in the league this season. Nicolas Jackson scored twice as Aston Villa won 2-1 in the eighth round, ending the leaders' perfect record and opening the door for Arsenal to close the gap.

Brighton arrive with a point to prove of their own. They were beaten 2-0 at Anfield last Sunday, and Fabian Hürzeler did not hide his frustration afterwards, suggesting there had been only 'one team' trying to create in the game.

“One team tried to create.”Fabian Hürzeler after Brighton's 2-0 defeat at Anfield

Hürzeler's side still command respect. Before that match, Liverpool coach Andoni Iraola praised his German counterpart at length, describing Brighton as 'excellently coached' and a team doing its job remarkably well.

Saturday's meeting therefore pits a wounded leader against an ambitious side that likes to have the ball - a contrast that should shape the afternoon in Manchester.
