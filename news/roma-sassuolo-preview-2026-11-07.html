Roma v Sassuolo: Koné back as Gasperini keeps rotating

Manu Koné returns to the squad, a third-choice goalkeeper may start, and Alberto Aquilani faces his old club at the Olimpico for the first time as a coach.

Photo: Gary Denham (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 2.0
AS Roma – SassuoloSerie A07.11 19:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Koné back in the Roma squad
  • Possible start for third-choice keeper Vasquez
  • Aquilani's first return to the Olimpico as a coach

Roma host Sassuolo at the Olimpico with Gian Piero Gasperini again expected to turn to the younger members of his squad. Italian reports name Ghilardi or Koulierakis, Lulli, Pisilli and Mora among the players in line for minutes.

The good news for Roma is Manu Koné. The French midfielder, forced off after 17 minutes against Real Madrid with a thigh problem, has recovered and is back in the squad. The goalkeeping situation is less settled: with Gollini doubtful, third-choice Devis Vasquez could get a rare start.

For Sassuolo the evening carries extra weight. Alberto Aquilani returns to the Olimpico as an opposing coach for the first time, at a difficult moment for his side, and admitted the emotion will only fade once the ball starts rolling.
