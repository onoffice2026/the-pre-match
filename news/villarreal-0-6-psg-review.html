Villarreal 0-6 PSG: four goals in 17 minutes and the champions stay perfect

Fabián Ruiz scored twice and Maghnes Akliouche set up two as Paris Saint-Germain moved three points clear at the top.

Photo: Ben Sutherland (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Villarreal – Paris Saint-GermainChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • 4-0 after 17 minutes
  • PSG: 22 shots, 12 on target, 70.6% possession
  • Fabián Ruiz two goals, Akliouche two assists
  • PSG three points clear at the top

Paris Saint-Germain settled the game before Villarreal had found their feet. João Neves opened the scoring from close range after a ninth-minute corner, and by the 17th minute the score was 4-0.

Désiré Doué finished Akliouche's pass in the 12th minute, Senny Mayulu fired into the top corner within 60 seconds of the restart, and Fabián Ruiz added the fourth from another Akliouche assist.

Ruiz made it 5-0 five minutes after half-time from Vitinha's free-kick. Luis Enrique took off Doué, Vitinha and Achraf Hakimi in the 74th minute, and substitute David Boly still completed the rout from Neves' pass.

The numbers match the score: PSG had 70.6% of the ball, 22 shots and 12 on target. Villarreal's 14 shots produced only two on target, and Péter Gulácsi had to make six saves to keep the score at six.

PSG have now won four out of four in this Champions League campaign and ten games in a row in all competitions.
