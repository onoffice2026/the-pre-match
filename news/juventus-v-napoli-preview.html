Juventus v Napoli: Spalletti under pressure after Genoa defeat

Juventus have lost three of their nine Serie A games, and reports describe unrest in the dressing room before the visit of Napoli.

Key points
  • Juventus: three Serie A defeats in nine
  • Reports of player unrest over Spalletti's methods
  • Napoli without Badiashile and Spinazzola

Juventus face Napoli under heavy pressure. The 2-0 defeat at Genoa on Wednesday was their third in nine Serie A matches, and Luciano Spalletti said afterwards that the team had to apologise to the fans.

According to La Stampa, quoted by Voetbal International, some players are unhappy with Spalletti's training methods, feeling that the coach talks too much and leaves too little time for intensity. Foot Mercato reports that Randal Kolo Muani could lose his place and that the club plans to sign a striker in January.

Napoli arrive after a 4-1 win at Monza, but Massimiliano Allegri has lost defender Badiashile and wing-back Leonardo Spinazzola to injuries, according to Corriere dello Sport. Amir Rrahmani is back.

Both sides have won two of their last five games in all competitions.
