Match review

Stoppage-time winner gives Bengaluru a 2-1 victory at FC Goa

Soham Naveen Varshneya struck in the third minute of added time after Borja Martínez and Franchu had traded early goals.

Photo: Fallencaller · CC0
FC GOA – Bengaluru FCIndia 114:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Borja Martínez 12', Franchu 17', Varshneya 90+3'
  • Bengaluru: 13 shots, 6 on target; Goa only 2 on target
  • Five yellow cards, all after the 69th minute

Bengaluru FC left Goa with all three points thanks to a goal deep into stoppage time. Soham Naveen Varshneya scored in the 93rd minute, after a nervy final half-hour that brought five yellow cards.

The opening was lively. Borja Martínez put Bengaluru ahead after 12 minutes and Franchu equalised for Goa five minutes later. After that the game settled into a long stalemate, Goa slightly ahead on possession (52%) but short of ideas: they managed only two shots on target all evening.

Bengaluru were the more dangerous side – 13 shots to 7, six of them on target – and Goa's goalkeeper had to make four saves before the late winner. The visitors' aggression showed too: Manuel de Iriondo, Ricky Meetei and Ashique Kuruniyan were all booked.

Our take: a deserved win on chances. Goa held on for a long time, but a side that tests the keeper only twice at home rarely gets away with it.
