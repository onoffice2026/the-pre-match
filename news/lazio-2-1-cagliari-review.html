Lazio 2-1 Cagliari: Pinamonti header settles it late at the Olimpico

Gustav Isaksen equalised before half-time and Andrea Pinamonti headed the winner in the 83rd minute after M'Bala Nzola had put Cagliari ahead.

Photo: Messapi, cropped by Blackcat · CC BY-SA 4.0
Lazio – CagliariSerie A14:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Isaksen 36', Pinamonti 83' (Lazio); Nzola 20' (Cagliari)
  • Cagliari: Obert sent off in stoppage time
  • Lazio: 73.7% possession

Lazio came from behind to beat Cagliari 2-1 at the Olimpico, winning with a late header from Andrea Pinamonti after controlling almost three quarters of the possession.

Cagliari went ahead against the run of play in the 20th minute. Alieu Fadera led a fast break and M'Bala Nzola finished from very close range into the bottom right corner.

Lazio were level in the 36th minute, when Tijjani Noslin found Gustav Isaksen in the centre of the box and the Dane fired a left-footed shot into the top right corner.

The winner came in the 83rd minute. Alfonso Pedraza delivered the ball and Pinamonti headed in from very close range. Cagliari finished with ten men after Adam Obert received a second yellow card in stoppage time.

Lazio had 73.7 per cent possession but only 11 shots, four on target. Cagliari committed 18 fouls to Lazio's seven.
