AFC Bournemouth 2-0 Leeds United: Trafford error gifts Cherries win

Evanilson capitalised on a goalkeeping mistake and substitute Amine Adli sealed Bournemouth's third straight league win.

Photo: Shaun Ferguson · CC BY-SA 2.0
AFC Bournemouth – Leeds UnitedPremier League15:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Evanilson 31', Amine Adli 90+4'
  • Calvert-Lewin goal ruled out for handball by VAR
  • Leeds managed only 1 shot on target

A costly mistake from James Trafford set Bournemouth on their way to a 2-0 win over Leeds United at the Vitality Stadium.

Leeds thought they had gone ahead after 11 minutes, but Dominic Calvert-Lewin's finish on his 50th appearance for the club was ruled out by VAR for handball.

The decisive moment came in the 31st minute. Trafford gave the ball away after a short goal-kick, Justin Kluivert pounced and Evanilson finished with his right foot into the bottom right corner for his fifth goal in six games.

Leeds had more of the ball, with 56.1 per cent possession, but managed just one shot on target all afternoon. Their manager Daniel Farke was also booked in the first half.

Bournemouth made sure in the fourth minute of stoppage time, when Junior Kroupi set up substitute Amine Adli to finish into the bottom left corner. The win was the Cherries' third in a row in the top flight and lifts them a point above Leeds, whose winless league run stretches to five games.
