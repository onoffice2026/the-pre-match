Match review

SC Cambuur 4-0 Go Ahead Eagles: crosses and a late break sink the visitors

Cambuur led 3-0 at half-time thanks to three goals from wide deliveries and added a fourth on the counter-attack at the Kooi Stadium.

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Baouf 7', Çeka 15', Van Duijl 41', El Arguioui 80'
  • Three goals from crosses, one on the counter-attack
  • Go Ahead Eagles: 1 shot on target

SC Cambuur beat Go Ahead Eagles 4-0, a heavy defeat for a side that had started the evening as slight favourites.

Cambuur went ahead in the 7th minute when Rafik El Arguioui's cross from a corner was headed in from close range by Ismaël Baouf. Eight minutes later Jason Çeka finished into the top right corner from Devid Eugene Bouah's cross.

The third arrived in the 41st minute, when Billy van Duijl headed Ilyès Hamache's cross into the bottom right corner.

Go Ahead Eagles had almost as much of the ball, 48.6 per cent, and ten shots, but only one of them was on target. Cambuur sealed the win in the 80th minute: Sami Bouhoudane released El Arguioui on a fast break and he finished from outside the box.

Cambuur ended with 15 shots, seven on target, and seven corners.
