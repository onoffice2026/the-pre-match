Match review

Ten-man Gladbach fall apart as Hamburg win the bottom-of-the-table clash 3-1

Ko Itakura's second yellow four minutes after half-time turned the game; Nandja and Grønbæk sealed it for Hamburg.

Photo: Photocapy (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 2.0
Hamburg SV – Borussia MönchengladbachBundesliga06.11 19:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Muheim 11', Hack 44', Nandja 59', Grønbæk 70'
  • Itakura sent off after 49 minutes
  • Gladbach 19 shots, but only 6 on target

Hamburg got the start they needed in a game billed as a crisis duel: Miro Muheim's left-footed shot gave them the lead after 11 minutes. Gladbach answered just before the break, Robin Hack levelling in the 44th minute.

The turning point came early in the second half. Ko Itakura, already booked for a bad foul, was sent off for a second one in the 49th minute, and Hamburg made the extra man count – Shafiq Nandja on the hour and Albert Grønbæk in the 70th.

The numbers show a closer game than the score: Gladbach had 19 shots to Hamburg's 18, even with ten men for most of the second half. But Hamburg were more accurate (9 on target to 6) and kept 62% of the ball.

Our take: three points that ease the pressure in Hamburg, and another night that leaves Gladbach bottom with plenty to fix – discipline first.
