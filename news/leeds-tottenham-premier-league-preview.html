Spurs chase a fourth straight win at winless Leeds

Tottenham are smiling again after three wins in a row; Leeds have gone five without one.

Photo: Bill Henderson (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 2.0
Leeds United – Tottenham HotspurPremier League12:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Tottenham: three straight wins, two clean sheets
  • Leeds: five games without a win
  • Odobert back in training after nine months

Tottenham started the season badly again, but three straight wins, 3-1 against Coventry, 2-0 at Chelsea and 2-0 against Crystal Palace last Saturday, have lifted them into mid-table. Jan Paul van Hecke, who scored against Palace, says that is not enough: "We want to get back to the top."

There is more encouragement in the medical room. Wilson Odobert, out since 11 February, has returned to individual training after nine months, and Dejan Kulusevski has been named in Sweden's squad for the first time in two years as he comes back from a 17-month absence with a knee injury. Reports in Spain say Barcelona are still interested in Pedro Porro, if Spurs lower their asking price.

“We want to get back to the top.”Jan Paul van Hecke, Tottenham

Leeds are without a win in five: draws with Manchester United (1-1) and at Sunderland (0-0) were followed by a 2-0 defeat at Bournemouth last weekend. Saturday's early kick-off at Elland Road is 12:30 UK time.
