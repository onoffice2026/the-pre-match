Heerenveen 1-0 NEC Nijmegen: Vente header beats 21 shots

Dylan Vente's second-half header was enough for Heerenveen, while NEC put only two of their 21 attempts on target.

Heerenveen – NEC NijmegenEredivisie17:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goal: Dylan Vente 66'
  • NEC: 21 shots, 2 on target
  • Six yellow cards, three each

Heerenveen beat NEC Nijmegen 1-0 at the Abe Lenstra Stadion despite spending much of the evening defending.

The first half was fiery rather than fluent. Four players were booked between the 32nd and 43rd minutes, two from each side.

The only goal came in the 66th minute, when Levi Smans set up Dylan Vente to head in from very close range.

NEC dominated the ball with 61.5 per cent possession and took 21 shots, but only two of them tested the Heerenveen goalkeeper. Heerenveen were far more economical, with eight attempts and two on target.

Six yellow cards were shown in total, three to each side.
