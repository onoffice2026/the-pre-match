Celta Vigo held 0-0 by Levante despite 23 shots

Key points
  • 0-0 at Balaídos
  • Celta 23 shots, 8 on target
  • Levante 0 shots on target

Celta Vigo controlled the match with 59% of the ball and created far more: 23 shots to six, eight on target, six corners to one.

Levante did not test the Celta goalkeeper once, but they held out for the whole match.

For Levante the point was earned by defending; for Celta, it is a home match that should have been won.
