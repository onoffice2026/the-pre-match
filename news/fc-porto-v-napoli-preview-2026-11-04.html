Porto v Napoli: Allegri's stretched squad faces Farioli's Dragons

Napoli are short of bodies and 'fragile' after defeat to Juventus; Porto want to lift their Champions League tally.

Photo: IngolfBLN · CC BY-SA 2.0
FC Porto – NapoliChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Napoli: only 13 players available, Allegri says
  • Porto: two wins in a row after two European defeats
  • Napoli lost 2-0 at Juventus on the weekend

Napoli travel to Portugal with a thin squad. Massimiliano Allegri said on the eve of the game that he has only thirteen players available, 'not even enough for a rondo', and two regulars are missing after the 2-0 defeat at Juventus.

Matteo Politano admitted Napoli are fragile defensively and concede too easily, but said injuries can be no alibi. He also noted that Porto have fewer points in the Champions League than their performances deserve.

“We are thirteen, we can't even do a rondo.”Massimiliano Allegri

Porto come in on two straight wins, 4-0 against Estoril and 1-0 at Gil Vicente, after European defeats to PSV and Real Betis. Coach Francesco Farioli was coy about Napoli's past interest in him and called Allegri 'one of the greats'.
