Union Berlin 1-1 Cologne: El Mala equalises late at the Alte Försterei

Janik Haberer gave Union an early lead, but Malek El Mala levelled from a corner in the 79th minute.

Photo: Lear 21 · CC BY-SA 3.0
1. FC Union Berlin – FC CologneBundesliga14:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Haberer 10' (Union); El Mala 79' (Cologne)
  • Cologne: 14 shots, 2 on target
  • Union: 4 yellow cards

Union Berlin and Cologne drew 1-1 in the Bundesliga at the Stadion An der Alten Försterei, with the visitors equalising 11 minutes from time.

Union went ahead in the 10th minute. Tim Skarke set up Janik Haberer, who finished from very close range into the centre of the goal.

Cologne then had most of the ball, finishing with 62.8 per cent possession and 14 shots against five for Union. Only two of those efforts were on target, and the hosts defended their lead for most of the match.

The equaliser came from a set piece. In the 79th minute Alessio Castro-Montes's corner reached Malek El Mala, who finished from very close range into the top of the goal.

Each side had two shots on target. Union received four yellow cards and Cologne three, and Cologne won six corners to Union's two.
