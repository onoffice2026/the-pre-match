Barcelona v Aston Villa: Fermín-inspired hosts face a resurgent Villa

Barcelona put six past Alavés, while Aston Villa arrive after a draw with Fulham decided by a Zion Suzuki own goal.

Photo: Paolo Costa Baldi · CC BY-SA 3.0
Barcelona – Aston VillaChampions League20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Fermín López: one goal and two assists against Alavés
  • Villa: Suzuki own goal cost them a win at Fulham
  • Nicolas Jackson: eight goals this season

Barcelona host Aston Villa in the Champions League on Tuesday after a 6-1 win over Alavés, in which Fermín López scored once and set up two more. Mundo Deportivo described him as the player who opened the game up and then blew it apart.

The win also brought an official debut for La Masia graduate Bryan Fariñas, 20, who started at right-back. "It is the dream of any child: the first division with FC Barcelona, the club of my life," he told Mundo Deportivo.

Aston Villa were held by Fulham on Saturday. Nicolas Jackson scored his eighth goal of the season and John McGinn missed a penalty, before goalkeeper Zion Suzuki dropped Alex Iwobi's corner over his own line. The Guardian noted that the result denied Unai Emery's side a fifth win in six games.

Suzuki, who replaced Emiliano Martínez in Villa's goal, now arrives at Camp Nou after the most difficult weekend of his time at the club, Mundo Deportivo wrote. Emery admitted there was work to do ahead of games against Barcelona and Manchester United.
