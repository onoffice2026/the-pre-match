Barcelona v Aston Villa: Flick's side back on track

Barcelona hit Alavés for six; Villa drew with Fulham after Zion Suzuki's own goal.

Key points
  • Barcelona beat Alavés 6-1
  • De Jong used at centre-back
  • Carragher apologised to Villa

Barcelona welcome Aston Villa after a 6-1 win over Alavés that ended two defeats in a row, against Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid. Fermín López scored once and made two assists.

Hansi Flick is short of defenders. Frenkie de Jong started at centre-back on Saturday and was praised by the Spanish press, and 20-year-old Brian Fariñas made his debut at right-back.

Villa drew 1-1 with Fulham after goalkeeper Zion Suzuki dropped a corner over his own line, although Nicolas Jackson scored his eighth goal of the season. Jamie Carragher has apologised to the club for saying Villa will "never be Manchester United" after a threat of legal action.
