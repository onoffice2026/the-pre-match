García Plaza warns Sevilla fans as winless Alavés visit the Sánchez-Pizjuán

Sevilla sit sixth but their coach says the league is built for two clubs at the top; Alavés have gone seven without a win.

Sevilla – AlavésLaLiga20:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Sevilla sixth; Fofana and Isaac available
  • Alavés: seven games without a win, beaten 6-1 at Barcelona
  • Kochorashvili attended Jesús de Miguel's funeral

Luis García Plaza used his pre-match press conference before Friday's game against Alavés to send a message about expectations. Sevilla are sixth, but he warned supporters that LaLiga seems designed to have "two teams far ahead at the top", and said his side must make better use of the spells in which they are the stronger team.

There was good news on fitness: Fofana and Isaac will both be available. Midfielder Giorgi Kochorashvili missed Thursday's training, with the club's permission, to attend the funeral of Tenerife player Jesús de Miguel, who died after a collision in a match against Andorra.

“We have to make more of the moments when we are superior.”Luis García Plaza, Sevilla coach

Sevilla lost 3-1 at Getafe last weekend after wins over Osasuna (2-0) and Sporting Hortaleza in the cup. Alavés are in a poor run: seven matches without a win, the last a 6-1 defeat at Barcelona in which Fermín López scored once and set up two goals, after defeats by Atlético Madrid and Celta and a draw with Málaga.
