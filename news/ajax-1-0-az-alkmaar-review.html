Ajax 1-0 AZ Alkmaar: early Arokodare header settles a tight contest

Tolu Arokodare's ninth-minute header was enough for Ajax, who survived heavy AZ pressure in front of 53,331 at the Johan Cruijff Arena.

Photo: Alf van Beem · CC0
Ajax Amsterdam – AZ AlkmaarEredivisie20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goal: Tolu Arokodare 9'
  • AZ: 19 shots and 8 corners; Ajax: 9 shots and 1 corner
  • Five saves by the Ajax goalkeeper

Ajax beat AZ Alkmaar 1-0 at the Johan Cruijff Arena, protecting an early lead through a long spell of visiting pressure.

The only goal came in the 9th minute. Abdellah Ouazane delivered a cross from the flank and Tolu Arokodare headed it into the bottom right corner.

The first half grew increasingly physical. Weslley Patati and Mees de Wit were booked for AZ, while Ouazane was shown a yellow card for Ajax.

AZ had the better of the match statistically, with 19 shots to Ajax's nine and eight corners to one. The Ajax goalkeeper made five saves to keep the visitors out.

Ajax put only two of their own efforts on target, but the early header proved decisive in front of a crowd of 53,331.
