Cruzeiro bank on a 13-year home record against Bahia

Three straight wins have Artur Jorge's side chasing a Libertadores place; the Mineirão has been a fortress in this fixture.

Photo: Vitor Daniel 07 (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 4.0
Cruzeiro – BahiaBrasileirão06.11 00:30 UTC
Key points
  • Cruzeiro unbeaten at home against Bahia for 13 years
  • Cruzeiro: three league wins in a row
  • Bahia beat São Paulo 3-2 last time out

Cruzeiro host Bahia at the Mineirão on Thursday night (21:30 Brasília time) in round 34 of the Brasileirão with history on their side: they have not lost at home to Bahia for 13 years.

Artur Jorge's team arrive in form. They won the Minas Gerais derby 1-0 at Atlético-MG last week, with Kaio Jorge scoring after four minutes, after beating Remo 1-0 and winning 4-2 at Grêmio. A fourth straight league win would be another important step in the race for a place in next season's Copa Libertadores, although ge reports that Cruzeiro have some absences for the game.

Bahia have taken seven points from their last four outside the 0-3 home defeat to Flamengo: a 3-2 win over São Paulo last week followed a 1-1 draw at Santos, and they beat Mirassol 1-0 in October.
