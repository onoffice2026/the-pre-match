Flying Frosinone, sixth in Serie A, visit a Cagliari side that has lost four of five

Alvini's side have won five home games in a row but lose Antonio Raimondo to a serious ankle injury; Nzola is Cagliari's hope.

Photo: Cristiano Cani (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Cagliari – FrosinoneSerie A14:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Frosinone: five straight home wins, sixth with 20 points
  • Raimondo out with a multi-ligament ankle injury
  • Cagliari: four defeats in their last five

Frosinone are one of the surprises of the Serie A season. Saturday's 2-0 win over Torino, with goals from Fares Ghedjemis and Calò, was their fifth straight home win and their third victory in four games, taking Alvini's side to 20 points and sixth place. In between they drew 4-4 at Atalanta and beat Lecce 5-1.

The win had a cost: forward Antonio Raimondo, 22, one of the revelations of the season, suffered a multi-ligament injury to his left ankle, and the club says it is not a minor one.

Cagliari are heading the other way, without a win in five and with four defeats, the last a 2-1 loss at Lazio in which they led before Lazio came back to go top on their own. They have also lost at Roma (2-4) and at home to Bologna (0-2), and Sebastiano Esposito is still out. Corriere dello Sport says Nzola is becoming the leader of Pisacane's attack and is chasing a third successive goal.
