Match review

Orsini and Briasco give Barracas Central a 2-0 win over Racing

Racing had more of the ball and eight corners, but Barracas Central were sharper in both boxes.

Photo: Unknown · Public domain
Barracas Central – Racing ClubArgentine Primera06.11 18:00 UTC
Key points
  • Orsini 43', Briasco 77'
  • Racing: 8 corners, 2 shots on target
  • Barracas: 17 shots

Barracas Central beat Racing Club 2-0 at home. Nicolás Orsini opened the scoring two minutes before half-time, and Norberto Briasco added the second with a left-footed finish in the 77th minute.

Racing had 56% of the ball and eight corners to one, but managed only two shots on target. Barracas took 17 shots and kept attacking even with the lead.

Our take: a deserved home win. Racing's possession produced too little, and their late yellow cards showed the frustration.
