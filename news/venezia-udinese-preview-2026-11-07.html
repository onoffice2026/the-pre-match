Zanetti's first night at Udinese: a trip to Venice and a club that needs a reaction

Udinese sacked Kosta Runjaic after one point in eight games; Paolo Zanetti starts at Venezia, a club he once coached, on Friday evening.

Photo: kallerna (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 4.0
Venezia – UdineseSerie A19:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Udinese: one point in eight league games, Runjaic sacked after 2-5 v Roma
  • Paolo Zanetti appointed until June 2027, debut in Venice
  • Venezia have lost three straight, the last 0-1 at Como

Serie A's eleventh round opens in the lagoon with a coaching debut. Udinese dismissed Kosta Runjaic on Monday after the 5-2 home defeat to Roma, a game they were losing 4-0 at half-time, and appointed Paolo Zanetti within hours on a contract to June 2027.

The numbers behind the decision were stark: one point from the last eight league matches and a single league win all season, against Monza at the end of August. Zanetti's first message was simple – 'whoever runs, plays' – and he added that he respects Runjaic's work and will not tear it up in a week.

Venezia are not in much better shape. Davide Nicola's side have lost three in a row, the latest a narrow 1-0 at Como that left them unhappy about a penalty decision. Their one recent high was the 1-0 win over Napoli in mid-October, proof that this group can raise itself for a big night at home.

Our take: a new coach usually brings energy for one or two games, and Udinese have more individual quality. But Venezia at home, with the crowd Nicola has asked to 'strengthen their vocal cords', will not be an easy place for a first match. A low-scoring, nervous game looks more likely than an open one.
