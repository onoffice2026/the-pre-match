Kane commits to 2029 and Musiala returns as Bayern head to struggling Mainz

Bayern are unbeaten in 12; Mainz have lost three in a row and conceded nine goals in the process.

Mainz – Bayern MunichBundesliga14:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Kane extends to 2029 and becomes the Bundesliga's best-paid player
  • Musiala back in team training after five weeks
  • Mainz: three defeats in a row, nine goals conceded

Bayern Munich travel to Mainz on Saturday on the back of a big week. On Thursday Harry Kane extended his contract by two seasons, to June 2029. According to L'Équipe his fixed salary has not gone up, but new bonuses make him the best-paid player in Bundesliga history. "We're on the verge of something really special," Kane said. Chief executive Jan-Christian Dreesen also spoke of the value of Kane's Ballon d'Or triumph for the club's sponsors.

There was more good news on the same day: Jamal Musiala returned to full team training for the first time in more than five weeks, having been out since 24 September. Bayern also extended the contract of 19-year-old French forward Noah Codjo-Evora until June 2030.

“We're on the verge of something really special at Bayern.”Harry Kane

On the pitch, Bayern are unbeaten in 12 games; after a 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund on Saturday they responded with a 2-0 win at Atlético Madrid in the Champions League. Mainz are in trouble: three straight defeats, 2-3 against Werder Bremen, 0-2 at Bochum in the cup and 1-4 at Elversberg.
