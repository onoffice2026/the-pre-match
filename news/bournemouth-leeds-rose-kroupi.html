Bournemouth v Leeds: Rose's Cherries on a high after Old Trafford

Bournemouth won 3-0 at Manchester United, having already humbled AC Milan. Leeds arrive after a goalless draw at Sunderland.

Photo: Matthew Jackson · CC BY-SA 3.0
AFC Bournemouth – Leeds UnitedPremier League15:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Bournemouth won 3-0 at Manchester United.
  • They had earlier humbled AC Milan.
  • Éli Junior Kroupi is close to a return from injury.

Bournemouth are flying. Marco Rose's side won 3-0 at Old Trafford last weekend, extending Manchester United's winless run, and had already humbled AC Milan.

There is more good news: Éli Junior Kroupi, injured since the start of the season, is close to a return - and, according to Foot Mercato, has turned down Ivory Coast with an eye on the France national team.

Leeds United come to the south coast after a 0-0 draw at bottom side Sunderland, a point that keeps them solid but offered little going forward.

Against a Bournemouth team brimming with confidence, Leeds will need to show more ambition in attack.
