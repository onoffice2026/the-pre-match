Liverpool v Arsenal: Arteta targets the end of the Anfield curse

Arsenal arrive at Anfield after a midweek cup exit at Fleetwood, while Liverpool are unbeaten in five.

Liverpool – ArsenalPremier League16:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Arsenal's last Premier League win at Anfield came 14 years ago
  • Arsenal knocked out of the Carabao Cup by Fleetwood Town
  • Liverpool unbeaten in 5, Arsenal in 6

Arsenal have not won a Premier League game at Anfield for 14 years, according to Voetbal International, and Mikel Arteta was on the pitch as a player the last time they did, in a 2-0 win. "It is high time," the Arsenal manager said on Friday.

The visitors come into Sunday's game after an awkward week. They drew 1-1 at League Two side Fleetwood Town on Tuesday and went out of the Carabao Cup, a few days after beating Everton 2-0 in the league. Arteta was also booked against Everton for leaving his technical area.

Liverpool are unbeaten in five matches in all competitions. They beat Brighton 2-0 and Villarreal 4-3 before a 1-1 draw with Chelsea on Wednesday, and The Guardian notes that Araújo is winning admirers at Anfield.

Arsenal's own run is six games without defeat, including a 2-2 draw away at Bayern Munich in the Champions League.
