Moreirense 2-1 Estrela: Nile John settles it in stoppage time

Nile John's strike from outside the box five minutes into stoppage time gave Moreirense victory over the pre-match favourites.

Moreirense – EstrelaPrimeira Liga15:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Landerson 50', Nile John 90+5' (Moreirense); Zvonarek 57' (Estrela)
  • Estrela: 1 shot on target
  • Winner in the fifth minute of stoppage time

Moreirense beat Estrela 2-1 with the last kick of a tight game, after the visitors had started as favourites.

The first half ended goalless. Landerson broke the deadlock in the 50th minute, finishing from the centre of the box after a pass from Manuel Mendonça.

Estrela levelled seven minutes later through Lovro Zvonarek, whose left-footed shot went into the bottom left corner. It was their only effort on target in the match.

With a draw looking likely, Nile John won it in stoppage time. Tiago Andrade headed the ball into his path and John struck a right-footed shot from outside the box into the bottom right corner.

Both sides had 11 shots, but Moreirense put four on target to Estrela's one. The result comes shortly after ESPN reported that a group including Thomas Müller is buying the Amadora club.
