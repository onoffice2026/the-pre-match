PSV v Club Brugge: two talent factories go all-in

PSV beat Willem II 7-0 at the weekend; Club Brugge have won seven in a row.

PSV Eindhoven – Club BruggeChampions League20:00 UTC
PredictionProbably more than 2 goals in this game.The Teep, masked: no odds, no percentages. For information only; 18+, play responsibly. · Match centre →
Key points
  • PSV 7-0 Willem II
  • Brugge: 7 straight wins
  • Referee: Irfan Peljto (Bosnia)

Wednesday's game in Eindhoven brings together two clubs with a similar story: years of domestic dominance and now the ambition to go further in Europe.

PSV arrive after a 7-0 thrashing of promoted Willem II, with goals from Ruben van Bommel and Ricardo Pepi among others. Brugge have won their last seven games, including a 3-0 home win over Lens in the Champions League.

Bosnian referee Irfan Peljto takes charge; he has refereed Dutch sides seven times in Europe, never PSV.
