Dortmund carry their European win into a date with Elversberg

Borussia Dortmund are six games unbeaten after beating Real Betis; promoted Elversberg come off a 4-1 win over Mainz.

Photo: Arne Müseler / www.arne-mueseler.com (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 3.0
Borussia Dortmund – SV ElversbergBundesliga07.11 14:30 UTC
Key points
  • Dortmund six unbeaten; 2-1 v Betis in the Champions League
  • Elversberg beat Mainz 4-1 last time out
  • Villarreal reportedly chasing Elversberg striker Mokwa

Borussia Dortmund return to the Bundesliga on Saturday with the glow of a European night. They held on to beat Real Betis 2-1 in the Champions League, with young Konstantinos Karetsas scoring his first goal in the competition – a moment he nearly missed because of a trip to the toilet just before coming on.

Dortmund are unbeaten in six, including a 2-2 draw at Bayern last weekend, and they have the DFB-Pokal round of 16 at Frankfurt to look forward to. Rotation is likely after a demanding week, but the depth looks good.

Elversberg are the surprise of the season. The promoted side beat Mainz 4-1 at home in their last league game, already 3-0 up at half-time, and their centre-forward David Mokwa is reportedly a top January target for Villarreal. Away from home they are less convincing: 0-4 at Leipzig, 0-1 at Union.

Our view: Dortmund at home, in form, are clear favourites. Elversberg will try to play, which can open the game up, but the gap in quality on the road has shown in their big away fixtures.
