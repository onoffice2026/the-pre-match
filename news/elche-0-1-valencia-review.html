Match review

Elche 0-1 Valencia: Sadiq wins it on the break

Umar Sadiq finished a counter-attack set up by Arnaut Danjuma as Valencia won at the Martínez Valero despite having only 30.9 per cent of the ball.

Photo: Ruben Ortega · CC BY-SA 4.0
Elche – ValenciaLaLiga17:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goal: Sadiq 69'
  • Elche: 69.1% possession, 3 shots on target
  • Attendance: 28,360

Valencia won 1-0 at Elche in front of 28,360 at the Estadio Martínez Valero, scoring the only goal on the counter-attack.

Elche dominated possession, finishing with 69.1 per cent of the ball and 16 shots, but only three of those were on target.

Valencia struck in the 69th minute. Arnaut Danjuma set up a fast break and Umar Sadiq finished from very close range into the bottom right corner.

Elche collected three yellow cards, for Germán Valera, Víctor Chust and Facundo Buonanotte. Valencia's Javi Guerra and goalkeeper Stole Dimitrievski were also booked as the visitors held on.
