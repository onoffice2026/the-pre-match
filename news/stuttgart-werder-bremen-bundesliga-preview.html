Hoeneß hails Füllkrug before Stuttgart host top-five Werder

Werder's striker is back in the Germany squad; Stuttgart return from a 2-0 defeat in Istanbul.

Photo: RudolfSimon · CC BY-SA 3.0
VfB Stuttgart – Werder BremenBundesliga17:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Füllkrug recalled by Germany
  • Werder: top five; Weiser injured again, Stage still out
  • Stuttgart won 4-2 at Leverkusen before losing in Istanbul

Niclas Füllkrug has been one of the stories of the Bundesliga season, and on Thursday his goals earned him a recall to the Germany squad for this month's Nations League games. Stuttgart coach Sebastian Hoeneß was full of praise before Saturday's game, calling the Werder striker "a positive presence in the Bundesliga".

Werder are in the top five and kicker credits a team in which good form is spreading from player to player, with Füllkrug scoring and centre-back Amos Pieper solid behind. They beat Hoffenheim 2-0 last weekend and won 3-2 at Mainz before that. The setbacks are in the treatment room: Mitchell Weiser, who had only just made his comeback, is injured again, and Jens Stage has been out since late August. Ludovit Reis, meanwhile, has been called up by Slovakia.

“A positive presence in the Bundesliga.”Sebastian Hoeneß, Stuttgart coach, on Niclas Füllkrug

Stuttgart lost 2-0 at Galatasaray in the Champions League on Tuesday, but before that they won 4-2 at Bayer Leverkusen and 1-0 at Paderborn.
