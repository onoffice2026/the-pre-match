Atlético 0-2 Bayern: 18-year-old Lennart Karl breaks the wall at the Metropolitano

Karl and Konrad Laimer scored in the second half as a depleted Bayern wore down Diego Simeone's side.

Photo: Ungry Young Man (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Atlético Madrid – Bayern MunichChampions League03.11 20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Karl: five UCL goals before 19
  • Laimer seals it on 74 minutes from a Kane pass
  • Bayern 19 shots, Atlético 9
  • Bayern without Kompany, Musiala and Díaz

Atlético kept Bayern out for 54 minutes. Then Lennart Karl pounced on a mistake in the centre of the box and finished with his left foot.

With that goal the 18-year-old became only the fourth player to reach five Champions League goals before turning 19, after Lamine Yamal, Kylian Mbappé and Raúl.

Konrad Laimer made sure 20 minutes later, finishing from a difficult angle on the right after a brilliant pass from Harry Kane.

Bayern were again led by assistant Aaron Danks, with Vincent Kompany still absent on medical grounds, and played without Jamal Musiala and Luis Díaz. They still had 58.5% of the ball and 19 shots to Atlético's nine, and committed only three fouls all night.
