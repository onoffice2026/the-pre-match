Der Klassiker without Kompany: Danks takes Bayern's bench against Dortmund

Vincent Kompany is recovering at home after collapsing at training, so assistant Aaron Danks leads Bayern into Saturday's meeting with Borussia Dortmund - in the same week Harry Kane collected the Ballon d'Or.

Photo: Richard Bartz, Munich aka Makro Freak · CC BY-SA 2.5
Bayern Munich – Borussia DortmundBundesliga17:30 UTC
Key points
  • Vincent Kompany misses the game after collapsing at training; the club cited an irritation of the meninges.
  • Assistant Aaron Danks takes charge from the bench again.
  • Harry Kane arrives as the new Ballon d'Or winner.

Bayern Munich go into the biggest fixture of the German calendar with an unusual figure on the touchline. Head coach Vincent Kompany collapsed during a training session last Friday and, although he has since been discharged from hospital to recover at home, he will not be in the dugout against Borussia Dortmund.

The club said the incident was caused by an irritation of the meninges. Assistant Aaron Danks, who has already stood in once, will again take charge from the bench at the Allianz Arena. Kompany's collapse also led to the postponement of a Bayern II fixture, which was only played this week.

“He is simply insanely good.”Voetbal International panel on Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane

The episode has prompted a wider conversation in Germany about the strain on coaches. Freiburg's Julian Schuster, who faced Bayern recently, spoke at length about self-criticism, self-care and the short tenures in the job, and sent a gesture of support for Kompany with the Bayern party after the game.

On the pitch, Bayern can lean on a striker at the peak of his powers. Harry Kane was presented with the Ballon d'Or this week, and the panel of Voetbal International, reviewing the vote, put it bluntly: he is 'simply insanely good'.

For Dortmund, a trip to Munich without the hosts' head coach looks like an opening, but this is a fixture that rarely needs instructions from the bench to set the temperature. The emotional backdrop of Bayern's week may only sharpen it.
