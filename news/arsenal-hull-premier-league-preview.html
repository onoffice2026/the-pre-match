Arteta wants more after Prague scare as promoted Hull visit the Emirates

Arsenal won at Anfield and in Prague in four days; Hull are winless in eight but unbeaten at home.

Photo: Ank kumar (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 4.0
Arsenal – Hull CityPremier League07.11 15:00 UTC
Key points
  • Arsenal unbeaten in eight; first win at Anfield in 14 years
  • Arteta unhappy with the second half in Prague
  • Hull: eight without a win, unbeaten at home

Arsenal's week has been a good one on paper: a 1-0 win at Liverpool on Sunday, their first at Anfield in 14 years, followed by a 2-1 win at Slavia Prague in the Champions League on Wednesday, with Viktor Gyökeres scoring one and setting up the other. Mikel Arteta was not satisfied. His side led 2-0 at half-time but let Slavia back into the game and needed a double goal-line clearance late on; Arteta said the second half was "far below our standard". It was their third away game in seven days, and The Guardian's podcast noted they looked tired.

Hull, promoted alongside Coventry and Ipswich, have not won in eight league games but are still unbeaten at home, most recently drawing 2-2 with Ipswich and 1-1 with Brentford. Away from home it has been harder, including a 1-0 defeat at Fulham. Iroegbunam is still confident: "It's possible to beat Arsenal - we'll give it our all."

“It's possible to beat Arsenal - we'll give it our all.”Tim Iroegbunam, Hull City
