Saturday's surprises explained: Milan, Dortmund, Cambuur and Moreirense

Inter, Bayern Munich, Go Ahead Eagles and Estrela all started as favourites and failed to win. A look at what turned each game, and at the results that went to script.

Photo: Vincenzo.togni · CC BY-SA 4.0
Key points
  • Milan won the derby with 7 shots to Inter's 19
  • Bayern were 71% favourites; Kane missed a penalty and three players went off injured
  • Go Ahead Eagles: 1 shot on target in a 4-0 defeat

Four of Saturday's results in the major European leagues went against the pre-match forecasts, and a fifth came close. In each case the scoreline has a clear explanation in the match itself.

AC Milan 3-1 Inter. Pre-match estimates gave Milan only a 27 per cent chance of winning the derby, with Inter at 46 per cent. The game was effectively decided inside ten minutes: Christian Pulisic headed in Diego Moreira's cross in the 4th minute and Adrien Rabiot turned in Moreira's corner in the 10th. Inter then did what favourites do, with 19 shots, 11 corners and 56.9 per cent of the ball, but Mike Maignan made seven saves and Moreira's free-kick in the 43rd minute restored the two-goal cushion. Milan won with only seven shots of their own.

Bayern Munich 2-2 Borussia Dortmund. Bayern were given a 71 per cent chance of victory and Dortmund just 13 per cent. Three moments changed that. Harry Kane hit the bar with a penalty in the 56th minute, and Serhou Guirassy equalised four minutes later. Bayern also lost Ismael Saibari, Serge Gnabry and Michael Olise to injuries during the game, after starting without the ill Vincent Kompany on the touchline, Luis Díaz and Manuel Neuer. Fábio Silva's equaliser in the 89th minute, confirmed by VAR, completed the turnaround.

SC Cambuur 4-0 Go Ahead Eagles. The visitors were marginal favourites, 39 per cent against 37, but Cambuur scored twice in the first 15 minutes, both from crosses, and led 3-0 at the break. Go Ahead Eagles managed one shot on target all evening.

Moreirense 2-1 Estrela. Estrela started as favourites at 40 per cent, with Moreirense at 31. The match was level until stoppage time, when Nile John struck from outside the box. Estrela had 11 shots but only one on target, the goal by Lovro Zvonarek.

Aston Villa 1-1 Fulham was the near miss. Villa were favoured at 45 per cent, but John McGinn hit the bar with a penalty in the 16th minute and goalkeeper Zion Suzuki dropped Alex Iwobi's corner over his own line in the 64th minute.

Elsewhere the favourites delivered. Barcelona (86 per cent) beat Alavés 6-1, PSV Eindhoven (88 per cent) beat Willem II 7-0, FC Porto (81 per cent) beat Estoril 4-0 and Real Betis (73 per cent) beat Málaga 2-1. AS Roma, at 61 per cent, won 5-2 in Udine after leading 4-0 at half-time. Ajax (54 per cent) and Heerenveen (45 per cent) won 1-0 despite being out-shot, while Tottenham's 2-0 win over Crystal Palace was shaped by Jean-Philippe Mateta's red card in the 13th minute.

The pattern is consistent: the surprises came from early goals against the run of play, penalties missed by the favourites and individual errors rather than from one side being clearly better over 90 minutes.
