Atlético Madrid v Bayern Munich: injury worries for the Bavarians

Bayern drew with Dortmund and lost three players to injury, while Atlético arrive on seven straight league wins.

Atlético Madrid – Bayern MunichChampions League20:00 UTC
PredictionProbably between 3 and 6 goals in this game.The Teep, masked: no odds, no percentages. For information only; 18+, play responsibly. · Match centre →
Key points
  • Saibari and Gnabry likely out
  • Bayern unbeaten in 12
  • Atlético: seven league wins in a row

Bayern Munich travel to Madrid with injury concerns. Ismael Saibari and Serge Gnabry are likely to be out for some time after the 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund, Voetbal International reports, while Michael Olise seems to have come off better.

Harry Kane presented the Ballon d'Or to the Allianz Arena crowd before Saturday's Klassiker, then missed a penalty and hit the post. Bayern are still unbeaten in 12 games.

Atlético beat Levante 2-0 on Saturday for a seventh straight league win, Mundo Deportivo reports, and are unbeaten in nine. They drew 1-1 at Stuttgart in their last Champions League game.
