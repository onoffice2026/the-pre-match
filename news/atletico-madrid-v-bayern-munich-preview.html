Atlético Madrid v Bayern Munich: injuries pile up for the Bundesliga leaders

Bayern travel to Madrid without Ismael Saibari and Serge Gnabry, and Michael Olise is a doubt after Der Klassiker.

Photo: Fernandopascullo · CC BY-SA 4.0
Atlético Madrid – Bayern MunichChampions League03.11 20:00 UTC
Key points
  • Out for Bayern: Saibari, Gnabry, Neuer, Musiala, Luis Díaz
  • Olise a doubt with a knee problem
  • Kane missed a penalty in the 2-2 draw with Dortmund

Bayern Munich arrive in Madrid for Tuesday's Champions League game with a growing injury list. Ismael Saibari has had successful surgery on a torn ligament and Serge Gnabry has a hamstring injury, L'Equipe reported, adding to the absences of Manuel Neuer, Jamal Musiala and Luis Díaz.

Michael Olise also went off injured against Borussia Dortmund, with a problem in his right knee, but Foot Mercato reported that he may have avoided a long lay-off.

The weekend brought a frustrating 2-2 draw with Dortmund. Harry Kane presented his Ballon d'Or to the Bayern supporters before kick-off, then hit the bar with a penalty and struck the post with another chance. The draw left the two rivals level on 18 points, with Bayern still top on goal difference.

Kane, who became the first English player to win the Ballon d'Or since Michael Owen in 2001, remains the main threat Atlético must contain.
