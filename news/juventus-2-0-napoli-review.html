Juventus 2-0 Napoli: Alajbegovic and Boga strike from distance

Two second-half goals from outside the box eased the pressure on Luciano Spalletti as Napoli finished with ten men.

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Alajbegovic 70', Boga 87'
  • Marianucci (Napoli) sent off on 75' for a second yellow card
  • Juventus: 13 corners

Juventus beat Napoli 2-0 at the Allianz Stadium with two long-range goals in the second half, easing the pressure on head coach Luciano Spalletti. Juve had won only one of their previous four Serie A games and were jeered by their own fans earlier in the second period.

Napoli started brightly and Guglielmo Vicario made a fine save from Lorenzo Lucca's diving header. At the other end, Juan Cabal hit the post from close range, but neither side found a breakthrough before half-time.

The deadlock was broken in the 70th minute, when Bosnian teenager Kerim Alajbegovic took Edon Zhegrova's pass and struck a 20-yard shot into the corner for his first Serie A goal.

Five minutes later Napoli were down to ten men, Luca Marianucci receiving a second yellow card for a challenge on Cabal. Jérémie Boga, who had replaced Alajbegovic, sealed the win in the 87th minute, cutting in from the left and curling a shot into the far corner from Andrea Cambiaso's pass.

Juventus forced 13 corners to Napoli's four. It was Napoli's fourth league defeat of the season.
