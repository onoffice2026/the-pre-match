Frosinone v Torino: the promoted side in sixth meet an Abate side in form

Frosinone climbed to sixth after a 5-1 win over Lecce. Torino beat Monza 3-2 with goals from Fitz-Jim, Belghali and Vlasic.

Frosinone – TorinoSerie A14:00 UTC
Key points
  • Frosinone beat Lecce 5-1 and are sixth.
  • Antonio Raimondo is joint top scorer in Serie A.
  • Torino beat Monza 3-2; Kian Fitz-Jim scored his first Serie A goal.

Promoted Frosinone host Torino in sixth place in Serie A after a 5-1 home win over Lecce on Thursday night. Massimiliano Alvini's side have been one of the surprises of the opening weeks, and striker Antonio Raimondo is joint top scorer in the division.

Torino arrive after a 3-2 win over Monza. Kian Fitz-Jim, left out of the previous two games by coach Ignazio Abate, returned to the starting line-up and repaid him with the opening goal - his first in Serie A. Rafik Belghali and Nikola Vlasic also scored, before a late own goal made the result look closer than the game was.

Two teams in a good moment should make for an open afternoon.
