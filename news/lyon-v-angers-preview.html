Lyon v Angers: Fonseca's future in question as injuries pile up

Reports link Paulo Fonseca with Sporting CP, and Lyon go into the game with Ernest Nuamah expected to lead the attack.

Photo: Sebleouf · CC BY-SA 4.0
Lyon – AngersLigue 101.11 16:15 UTC
Key points
  • Lyon have lost their last two matches
  • Fonseca reported in talks with Sporting CP
  • Angers without El-Ouazzani

Lyon host Angers after two straight defeats, 2-0 at Paris Saint-Germain and 3-0 at Hoffenheim, and with questions over their coach. L'Équipe reports that Paulo Fonseca is in advanced talks with Sporting CP, and Thiago Motta has been mentioned as a possible replacement.

Injuries have hit the attack. Loïs Openda was hurt earlier this month and Corentin Tolisso picked up a minor injury, so L'Équipe expects Ernest Nuamah to play as centre-forward.

Angers are without Amine El-Ouazzani, who has an adductor injury and will miss two matches, and coach Stéphane Gilli could change his system again.

The match was moved from its original slot at the request of the authorities because of nationwide protests on Saturday.
