Match review

Lyon 2-0 Angers: Tolisso sets up Nuamah and scores the second

Ernest Nuamah scored before half-time and Corentin Tolisso headed in late as Lyon beat Angers at the Groupama Stadium.

Photo: Sebleouf · CC BY-SA 4.0
Lyon – AngersLigue 116:15 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Nuamah 38', Tolisso 86'
  • Tolisso: one goal and one assist
  • Lyon: 21 shots, 63.8% possession

Lyon beat Angers 2-0 at the Groupama Stadium, with Corentin Tolisso involved in both goals.

Tolisso set up the opener in the 38th minute, when Ernest Nuamah finished with his right foot into the bottom left corner.

Lyon controlled the game, finishing with 63.8 per cent possession and 21 shots, but only five were on target and the second goal did not arrive until the 86th minute. Tolisso headed in from very close range, set up by Felix Bacher after a corner.

Angers, without Amine el-Ouazzani according to L'Equipe before the match, managed eight shots and two on target. Jordan Lefort was the only player booked, in the 88th minute.
