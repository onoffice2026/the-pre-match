Match review

Al Hassan Touré hat-trick as Sydney FC beat Central Coast 4-1

Three goals in 19 minutes either side of half-time settled the Friday derby; Gabriel Popovic added a fourth from distance.

Photo: Dicklyon · CC BY-SA 4.0
Sydney FC – Central Coast Mariners FCA-League08:40 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Touré: penalty (31'), 42', 50' – a 19-minute hat-trick
  • Central Coast had 53% of the ball and 15 shots, but lost 4-1
  • Popovic 85', Lavale penalty in stoppage time

Sydney FC needed barely an hour to put the Central Coast Mariners away at the Allianz Stadium. Al Hassan Touré did most of the work: a penalty on 31 minutes, a close-range finish at the end of a fast break three minutes before the break, and a third from the centre of the box five minutes after it.

The pattern was clear. Central Coast had more of the ball (53%) and more shots (15 to 11), but Sydney were far sharper going forward – two of Touré's goals came from quick counter-attacks, set up by Akol Akon and Oriola Sunday.

Gabriel Popovic made it 4-0 with a left-footed strike from outside the box on 85 minutes, assisted by Tiago Quintal, before Oliver Lavale's stoppage-time penalty gave the visitors a late consolation.

Our take: a performance built on efficiency rather than control. Sydney turned six shots on target into four goals; Central Coast will look at how easily they were opened up on the break.
