Excelsior 0-9 FC Twente: Weghorst scores four after early red card

Wout Weghorst scored four times and Sondre Ørjasæter provided five assists as ten-man Excelsior were overwhelmed at Woudestein.

Photo: Wikifrits · Public domain
Excelsior – FC TwenteEredivisie13:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Weghorst 12', 32', 33', 62' (pen), Van den Belt 39', Rots 45+1', 82', Bawuah 49', Zerrouki 71' (pen)
  • Sondre Ørjasæter: five assists
  • Excelsior: red card in the 5th minute, no shots

FC Twente beat Excelsior 9-0 in the Eredivisie at Stadion Woudestein after the hosts were reduced to ten men inside five minutes. Nesto Groen was sent off in the 5th minute, and Excelsior did not record a single shot all afternoon.

Wout Weghorst opened the scoring in the 12th minute with a header from Sondre Ørjasæter's cross. He added two more in the 32nd and 33rd minutes, both again set up by Ørjasæter, to complete a hat-trick in just over 20 minutes of play.

Thomas van den Belt made it four in the 39th minute, and Daan Rots scored a fifth in first-half stoppage time from another Ørjasæter pass. Jordy Bawuah added the sixth in the 49th minute, Ørjasæter's fifth assist of the match.

Weghorst scored his fourth from the penalty spot in the 62nd minute, Ramiz Zerrouki converted a second penalty in the 71st minute, and Rots completed the scoring in the 82nd minute after a through ball from Van den Belt.

Twente had 79.8 per cent possession and 28 shots, 15 of them on target. The Excelsior goalkeeper made six saves, and the hosts did not win a single corner.
