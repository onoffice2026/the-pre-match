Rayo Vallecano 3-0 Celta Vigo: De Frutos double powers Rayo

Jorge de Frutos scored twice and Unai López added a third as Rayo cruised past a Celta side who failed to hit the target.

Rayo Vallecano – Celta VigoLaLiga15:15 UTC

Key points
  • Goals: Jorge de Frutos 36', 55'; Unai López 73'
  • Two of the goals came from fast breaks
  • Celta had 11 shots and none on target

Rayo Vallecano produced an efficient display at the Estadio de Vallecas, beating Celta Vigo 3-0 with goals of real quality.

Jorge de Frutos opened the scoring in the 36th minute at the end of a fast break, collecting Sergio Camello's through ball and finishing left-footed from outside the box into the bottom left corner.

De Frutos struck again in the 55th minute, this time with his right foot from the centre of the box after a pass from Andrei Ratiu, sending the ball into the top right corner.

Rayo completed the scoring on another counter in the 73rd minute. Gnangoro supplied the pass and Unai López, booked in the first half, beat the goalkeeper with a right-footed shot from distance.

The statistics underline the gulf in threat. Rayo had 14 shots with eight on target; Celta took 11 shots but did not put a single one on target, despite holding 47.3 per cent of the ball.
