Feyenoord v Fortuna Sittard: leaders host Buijs as he chases a club record

Feyenoord left Eindhoven as moral winners after a 1-1 draw. Fortuna coach Danny Buijs can become the most successful coach in the club's Eredivisie history.

Key points
  • Feyenoord lead the Eredivisie, four points ahead of PSV.
  • Anis Hadj Moussa scored the equaliser at PSV.
  • Danny Buijs shares Fortuna's record of 36 Eredivisie wins with Bert van Marwijk.

Feyenoord host Fortuna Sittard as Eredivisie leaders, four points clear of champions PSV after a 1-1 draw in Eindhoven that left the Rotterdam side feeling like the moral winners.

Anis Hadj Moussa was again decisive: barely visible in the first half, he cut inside and found the far corner for the equaliser. Voetbal International's pundits called him the biggest difference-maker in the Eredivisie.

Fortuna Sittard arrive with a piece of history at stake. Coach Danny Buijs currently shares the club record for Eredivisie wins with Bert van Marwijk, on 36. One more would make him the outright record holder - although a trip to De Kuip is a tough place to try.

The title race, VI's panel argued, will stay tense until the end.
