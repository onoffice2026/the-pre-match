Spurs' winning run meets a Leeds side still waiting for a win

Roberto De Zerbi's Tottenham have won three in a row without conceding in two; Leeds have gone five league games without a victory.

Photo: Geoff Charles (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 4.0
Leeds United – Tottenham HotspurPremier League12:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Spurs: three straight wins, including 2-0 at Chelsea
  • Leeds: five league games without a win
  • Odobert back in training after nine months out

Saturday's early kick-off at Elland Road puts a side in a hurry against one stuck in neutral. Tottenham beat Crystal Palace 2-0 last weekend after a 2-0 win at Chelsea, and before that they handled Coventry 3-1. Leeds' last five league games have brought three draws and two defeats, the latest 0-2 at Bournemouth.

Off the pitch, Spurs' week has been noisy. Richarlison, frozen out by De Zerbi, wants to leave in January, and Barcelona are reported to be watching Pedro Porro – at a lower price than Tottenham want. There is also the Harry Kane footnote: even after his new deal at Bayern to 2029, Spurs are said to keep a right to match any offer from a rival club.

The better news for Tottenham is Wilson Odobert, back in individual training after nine months out. He will not play here, but it hints at depth coming back for the winter.

The verdict: Leeds at home are hard to break down – three of their last five ended level – but they score too little to worry a confident Spurs. Tottenham are the likelier winners, with Leeds' best chance a scrappy, low-scoring game.
