Match review

Bologna 2-1 Monza: Skorupski save settles a three-penalty contest

Bologna came from behind through Tommaso Pobega and a Federico Bernardeschi penalty, then Lukasz Skorupski saved from the spot.

Photo: Vincenzo.togni · CC BY-SA 4.0
Bologna – MonzaSerie A14:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Varela 6' pen (Monza); Pobega 33', Bernardeschi 59' pen (Bologna)
  • Skorupski saved Cutrone's penalty on 81'
  • Seven yellow cards; Monza committed 19 fouls

Penalties shaped an eventful afternoon at the Renato Dall'Ara, where Bologna came from behind to beat Monza 2-1.

Monza made a dream start. Gustavo Varela converted a penalty in the sixth minute, sending a right-footed kick into the bottom right corner.

Bologna levelled in the 33rd minute from a set piece. Juan Miranda crossed after a corner and Tommaso Pobega, booked ten minutes earlier, finished with his left foot into the bottom left corner.

The hosts were awarded their own penalty in the 59th minute and Federico Bernardeschi scored it with his left foot. Monza had a chance to equalise from the spot in the 81st minute, but Lukasz Skorupski dived to his right to save Patrick Cutrone's effort.

It was a physical game with 32 fouls and seven yellow cards, 19 of the fouls committed by Monza. Bologna held 59.4 per cent of possession and had ten shots, five on target, against Monza's six shots and four on target.
