Villarreal v PSG: the 'steamroller' arrives at La Cerámica

Paris Saint-Germain have won nine in a row and lead the league phase; Luis Enrique warns against complacency, Iñigo Pérez calls them the best team in the competition.

Villarreal – Paris Saint-GermainChampions League20:00 UTC
PredictionProbably between 3 and 6 goals in this game.The Teep, masked: no odds, no percentages. For information only; 18+, play responsibly. · Match centre →
Key points
  • PSG: 9 straight wins, 3 out of 3 in the league phase
  • Dembélé, Marquinhos and Nuno Mendes miss the trip
  • Villarreal lost 1-0 at home to Espanyol last time out

PSG travel to Villarreal top of the Champions League table after three wins out of three, and with nine straight victories in all competitions, the latest a 5-1 at Le Havre.

Luis Enrique's message on Monday was that his side will not win unless they are at their best level. Villarreal coach Iñigo Pérez went further, calling Paris a steamroller that smells fear and does not forgive.

“If we are not at our maximum level, we won't win tomorrow”Luis Enrique

The Parisians travel without Ousmane Dembélé, Marquinhos and Nuno Mendes. Willian Pacho said he needed time to recover from a heavy 2025-26 season but now feels himself again.

Villarreal are not in their best moment: they lost 1-0 at home to Espanyol in LaLiga, and defender Pau Navarro had to apologise for comments after that game. Former Marseille midfielder Pape Gueye faces PSG again.
