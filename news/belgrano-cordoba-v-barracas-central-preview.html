Belgrano v Barracas Central: hosts look to end a two-game slide

Belgrano have lost their last two league games without scoring, while Barracas Central arrive unbeaten in four.

Photo: Jmmuguerza · CC BY-SA 4.0
Belgrano (Córdoba) – Barracas CentralArgentine Primera21:30 UTC
Key points
  • Belgrano: two defeats in a row, no goals scored
  • Barracas Central: unbeaten in four
  • Belgrano beat Talleres 1-0 in the Córdoba derby on 4 October

Belgrano host Barracas Central in Córdoba on Sunday night looking to stop a short slide. After three straight wins, including 2-0 at Racing Club and 1-0 at Talleres in the Córdoba derby, they lost 3-0 at home to River Plate and 1-0 at Aldosivi.

Barracas Central come in on a run of four games without defeat. They won 3-2 at Atlético Tucumán, beat Huracán 2-0 at home, drew 1-1 at Sarmiento and then beat Estudiantes de Río Cuarto 2-1 last weekend.

Belgrano's defence has conceded four goals in their last two games after keeping clean sheets against Racing Club and Talleres, while Barracas Central have scored eight goals in their last four.

The hosts' only league defeat at home in that run came against River Plate, who won 3-0 in Córdoba on 18 October.
