Werder Bremen 2-0 TSG Hoffenheim: Füllkrug and Dinkçi strike

Niclas Füllkrug and Eren Dinkçi scored in the second half as Werder Bremen beat a Hoffenheim side who dominated the ball.

Photo: BrianKohn · CC BY-SA 3.0 de
Werder Bremen – TSG HoffenheimBundesliga14:30 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Niclas Füllkrug 57', Eren Dinkçi 82'
  • Füllkrug's goal assisted by goalkeeper Karl Hein, confirmed by VAR
  • Hoffenheim: 16 shots, 2 on target

Werder Bremen beat TSG Hoffenheim 2-0 at the Weserstadion, scoring twice in the second half after a goalless opening period.

The opener came in the 57th minute and had an unusual source. Goalkeeper Karl Hein provided the assist and Niclas Füllkrug finished with his left foot into the top left corner. The goal was confirmed after a VAR review.

Bremen made sure in the 82nd minute. Marco Grüll set up Eren Dinkçi, who fired a right-footed shot from the centre of the box into the top right corner.

Hoffenheim had 58.6 per cent possession and 16 shots, but only two were on target. Bremen were more direct, attempting 18 shots and winning eight corners.

All three yellow cards went to the hosts: Marco Friedl in the 60th minute, then Arthur and Youri Regeer in stoppage time.
