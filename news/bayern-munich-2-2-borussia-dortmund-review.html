Bayern Munich 2-2 Borussia Dortmund: Kane misses penalty, Silva rescues Dortmund

Harry Kane hit the bar from the spot and Fábio Silva struck a minute from time as Der Klassiker ended level at the Allianz Arena.

Photo: Richard Bartz, Munich aka Makro Freak · CC BY-SA 2.5
Bayern Munich – Borussia DortmundBundesliga17:30 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Goals: Laimer 38', Upamecano 66' (Bayern); Guirassy 60', Fábio Silva 89' (Dortmund)
  • Harry Kane hit the bar with a penalty on 56'
  • Bayern lost Saibari, Gnabry and Olise to injuries

Days after being named the Ballon d'Or winner, Harry Kane missed a penalty as Bayern Munich were held 2-2 by Borussia Dortmund at the Allianz Arena. The draw keeps Dortmund unbeaten and level at the top of the Bundesliga.

Konrad Laimer opened the scoring in the 38th minute with a right-footed finish from the right side of the six-yard box, moments after Jobe Bellingham had a goal ruled out for Dortmund.

Kane had the chance to double the lead in the 56th minute, but his penalty struck the bar. Dortmund punished the miss in the 60th minute, when Serhou Guirassy finished low into the bottom left corner from Carney Chukwuemeka's pass.

Kane made amends with a headed assist from a corner, and Dayot Upamecano volleyed Bayern back in front in the 66th minute. Dortmund kept going, and substitute Fábio Silva levelled in the 89th minute from Konstantinos Karetsas's corner, with the goal confirmed after a VAR review.

The point came at a cost for Bayern, who lost Ismael Saibari, Serge Gnabry and Michael Olise to injuries during the match. The hosts, still without head coach Vincent Kompany on the touchline, face Atlético Madrid in the Champions League on Tuesday.
