Galatasaray v Stuttgart: 'All hell will break loose' in Istanbul

Galatasaray have won four in a row; Stuttgart arrive after a 4-2 win at Leverkusen and remember last season's night at Fenerbahçe.

Galatasaray – VfB StuttgartChampions League17:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Galatasaray: 4 straight wins
  • Stuttgart won 4-2 at Leverkusen
  • Sané and Gündoğan could play

Stuttgart expect a hostile evening. Maximilian Mittelstädt says he is looking forward to the atmosphere but warned of the whistles to come; last season the team lost at Fenerbahçe, largely because of the noise in the same city.

Stuttgart arrive in good shape after a 4-2 win at Bayer Leverkusen on Saturday, with two goals from Ermedin Demirović.

Galatasaray have won their last four games, including the derby against Fenerbahçe and a 4-2 win at Lille in the Champions League. Leroy Sané and İlkay Gündoğan could both be involved.

The game kicks off early, at 17:45 GMT.
