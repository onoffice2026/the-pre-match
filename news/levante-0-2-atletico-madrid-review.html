Match review

Levante 0-2 Atlético Madrid: Grimaldo and Giuliano Simeone seal it

Atlético Madrid were clinical in Valencia, scoring either side of the break while Levante fired 20 shots for no reward.

Photo: Francesc Fort · CC BY-SA 4.0
Levante – Atlético MadridLaLiga13:00 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Alejandro Grimaldo 36', Giuliano Simeone 62'
  • No yellow or red cards; seven fouls per side
  • Levante had 20 shots but only 4 on target

Atlético Madrid left the Estadi Ciutat de València with a 2-0 win, making the most of their chances against a Levante side who shot often but rarely troubled the goalkeeper.

The breakthrough arrived in the 36th minute. Alejandro Grimaldo found space on the left side of the box and lifted a left-footed shot into the top left corner.

Atlético doubled their advantage in the 62nd minute. Álex Baena threaded a through ball for Giuliano Simeone, who finished with his left foot from the centre of the box.

Levante's numbers told the story of their afternoon: 20 shots, but only four on target. Atlético needed just 11 attempts, five of them on target, and enjoyed 54 per cent of possession.

It was a remarkably clean contest, too. Each side committed only seven fouls and the referee did not need to show a single card.
