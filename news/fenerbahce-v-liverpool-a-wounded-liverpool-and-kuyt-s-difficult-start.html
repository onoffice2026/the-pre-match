Fenerbahçe v Liverpool: a wounded Liverpool and Kuyt's difficult start

Liverpool are still angry about the penalty they did not get against Arsenal; Fenerbahçe lost Dirk Kuyt's first game as interim coach.

Fenerbahce – LiverpoolChampions League17:45 UTC
PredictionProbably between 3 and 6 goals in this game.The Teep, masked: no odds, no percentages. For information only; 18+, play responsibly. · Match centre →
Key points
  • Liverpool lost 0-1 at home to Arsenal
  • Kuyt lost his first game as Fenerbahçe interim coach
  • Talisca out injured

Liverpool travel to Istanbul after a 1-0 home defeat by Arsenal, Arsenal's first win at Anfield in 14 years. The talk since has been about two penalty claims that were turned down, one for a shirt-pull on Alexander Isak.

Virgil van Dijk was furious at the decision, and Dominik Szoboszlai asked whether players are now expected to dive to win a penalty. Andoni Iraola said he would tell his players to go down.

Fenerbahçe are in a worse place. Dirk Kuyt, promoted from assistant after İsmail Kartal left, lost his first game 2-1 to Göztepe, with two goals conceded in two minutes. It was a second league defeat in a row.

Fenerbahçe are without the injured Talisca, who scored 19 goals for them over the last year.
