Udinese 2-5 AS Roma: four goals in 27 minutes settle it in Udine

Roma scored four times before half-time at the Stadio Friuli, and Udinese's second-half fightback came too late.

Key points
  • Goals: Cristante 10', Mora 31', Malen 33', Ndicka 37', Ghilardi 90+2' (Roma); Zaniolo 50', Palma 78' (Udinese)
  • Roma led 4-0 at half-time
  • Matías Soulé: two assists

AS Roma produced a devastating first half at the Stadio Friuli, scoring four times in 27 minutes on their way to a 5-2 win over Udinese.

Bryan Cristante opened the scoring in the 10th minute, heading Wesley's cross into the bottom left corner. Cristante then turned provider for Rodrigo Mora, who made it 2-0 in the 31st minute.

Two minutes later Donyell Malen finished from the centre of the box after a pass from Matías Soulé, and Soulé's corner was headed in by Evan Ndicka in the 37th minute to make it 4-0 at the break.

Udinese showed spirit after the interval. Nicolò Zaniolo scored against his former club in the 50th minute, and Matteo Palma added a second in the 78th minute from Hassane Kamara's corner.

Roma had the final word in stoppage time, when Daniele Ghilardi finished from the right side of the box after a pass from Santiago Castro. Udinese had 15 shots, seven on target, but Roma put nine of their 14 attempts on goal.
