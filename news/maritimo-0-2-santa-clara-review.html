Marítimo 0-2 Santa Clara: Ribeiro and Lopes win it in Funchal

Tiago Ribeiro scored before half-time and Vinícius Lopes struck from outside the box as Santa Clara won at the Estádio do Marítimo.

Key points
  • Goals: Ribeiro 36', Lopes 60'
  • Marítimo: 17 shots, 6 on target
  • Six yellow cards, three each

Santa Clara beat Marítimo 2-0 in Funchal.

Tiago Ribeiro opened the scoring in the 36th minute, finishing into the bottom right corner after a pass from Guilherme Romão.

Vinícius Lopes doubled the lead in the 60th minute, running onto Emanuel Moreira's through ball and striking from outside the box into the bottom right corner.

Marítimo had more of the ball and 17 shots, six of them on target, but the Santa Clara goalkeeper made six saves. The match produced six yellow cards, three for each side.
