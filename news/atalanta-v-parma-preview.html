Atalanta v Parma: Sarri under pressure amid an injury crisis

Atalanta are without a win in four and have lost Gianluca Scamacca, while reports in Bergamo mention Igor Tudor.

Key points
  • Atalanta winless in four
  • Scamacca injured
  • Parma without Pellegrino

Atalanta face Parma with Maurizio Sarri under pressure. Corriere dello Sport reports that Igor Tudor's name is circulating in Bergamo, and the club are without a win in four games.

The squad is stretched: Gianluca Scamacca is the latest injury, and Atalanta played Fiorentina with ten players unavailable before Charles De Ketelaere led a comeback to a 2-2 draw.

Parma are also winless in four and are without striker Mateo Pellegrino, out since early September.
