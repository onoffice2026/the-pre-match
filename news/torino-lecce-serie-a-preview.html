Torino and Lecce meet with both sides on losing runs

Two defeats in a row for Torino, three for Lecce: Saturday in Turin is about stopping the slide.

Photo: TThaarup · CC0
Torino – LecceSerie A14:00 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Torino: two straight defeats
  • Lecce: three straight defeats, nine goals conceded
  • Aboukhlal targeted by racist abuse online

Torino have lost their last two games, 1-3 at home to Como and 0-2 at Frosinone last Saturday, after beating Monza 3-2. The Frosinone defeat was followed by an ugly episode off the pitch: Zakaria Aboukhlal received racist abuse on social media after Frosinone forward Antonio Raimondo was seriously injured in the game.

Lecce's run is worse. Eusebio Di Francesco's side have lost three in a row: 0-2 at home to Juventus, 1-5 at Frosinone and 1-2 at home to Genoa last weekend, when Osmajic and Junior Messias put Daniele De Rossi's side two up inside half an hour before Maleh pulled one back before the break.

With both teams conceding freely, a first win in weeks is the target for each, and kick-off in Turin is at 15:00 local time.
