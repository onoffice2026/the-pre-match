Bodø/Glimt v Lille: title race heat meets Giroud's late show

Bodø/Glimt won a fiery top game against Viking; Lille beat Lens with an 86th-minute goal from Olivier Giroud.

Key points
  • Bodø/Glimt beat Viking 3-2
  • Giroud's late winner against Lens
  • Lille lost their last two Champions League games

Bodø/Glimt host Lille in the middle of a heated Norwegian title race. On Friday they beat Viking 3-2 in a top game in which both coaches were sent off.

Lille won the Nord derby against Lens 1-0 thanks to Olivier Giroud, who scored in the 86th minute off the bench. The 40-year-old said he does not accept a role as a substitute.

Davide Ancelotti's side have been exposed in the Champions League, The Guardian notes: they lost 3-0 at Arsenal and 4-2 at home to Galatasaray.
