Bodø/Glimt v Lille: Giroud the hero again for Lille

Olivier Giroud came off the bench to win the northern derby against Lens, while Bodø/Glimt won a fiery title clash with Viking.

Key points
  • Giroud scored the winner against Lens on 86'
  • Lille up to second in Ligue 1
  • Bodø/Glimt beat title rivals Viking

Lille travel to Norway for Tuesday's Champions League game after winning the northern derby against RC Lens 1-0. Olivier Giroud, 40, came off the bench and scored in the 86th minute, taking Lille up to second place in Ligue 1, Record reported.

Giroud told Voetbal International he is not resigned to a role as a substitute, saying his body still works.

Bodø/Glimt are in a tight title race with Viking FK. They won a heated top-of-the-table game on Friday in which both coaches were sent off.
