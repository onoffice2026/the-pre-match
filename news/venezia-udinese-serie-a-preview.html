Zanetti's first test: Udinese, 19th and winless in eight, visit struggling Venezia

Kosta Runjaic paid for the 5-2 home defeat to Roma; his successor has a contract to the end of the season and a clear message.

Photo: Zakarie Faibis · CC BY-SA 4.0
Venezia – UdineseSerie A19:45 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Paolo Zanetti replaces Kosta Runjaic at Udinese
  • Udinese: 19th, eight games without a win
  • Venezia: three straight defeats without a goal

Udinese go to Venice on Friday with a new coach. Kosta Runjaic was dismissed on Monday after a little over two seasons in charge, following the 5-2 home defeat by Roma, and Paolo Zanetti, formerly of Verona, has been appointed until the end of the season with the job of keeping the club, currently 19th, in Serie A.

"With me, the ones who run are the ones who play. Lots of work, few words," Zanetti said at his presentation, adding that he respected Runjaic's work and would not change too much of it. Runjaic said in a farewell message that Udinese would always have a special place in his heart. The numbers explain the decision: eight games without a win, including home defeats by Lecce (0-3) and AC Milan (0-2).

“With me, the ones who run are the ones who play. Lots of work, few words.”Paolo Zanetti, Udinese coach

Venezia are not in much better shape. After beating Napoli 1-0 on 17 October they have lost three in a row without scoring, 0-1 at Genoa, 0-2 against Inter and 0-1 at Como. There was some good news for Udinese defender Oumar Solet, who received his first call-up from Hervé Renard to Ivory Coast for the AFCON 2027 qualifier against Gambia.
