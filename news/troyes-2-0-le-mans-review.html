Match review

Troyes 2-0 Le Mans: Diop and Boura goals beat the possession side

Le Mans had more than 60 per cent of the ball but only one shot on target as Troyes won at the Stade de l'Aube.

Photo: TaraO · CC BY 2.5
Troyes – Le MansLigue 119:45 UTC
Key points
  • Goals: Diop 33', Boura 71'
  • Le Mans: 60.9% possession, 1 shot on target
  • Seven yellow cards in total

Troyes beat Le Mans 2-0 at the Stade de l'Aube in a game where the visitors had the ball and the hosts had the chances.

Mouhamed Diop opened the scoring in the 33rd minute, finishing from the centre of the box after a pass from Timothé Nkada.

Ismaël Boura, booked two minutes earlier, doubled the lead in the 71st minute when he turned in from very close range after a corner.

Le Mans finished with 60.9 per cent possession but only three shots, one on target, against 14 shots and eight on target for Troyes. Before the match, Foot Mercato reported that Le Mans defender Djibril Sidibé had suffered a fracture.

It was a scrappy contest with seven yellow cards, three for Troyes and four for Le Mans.
