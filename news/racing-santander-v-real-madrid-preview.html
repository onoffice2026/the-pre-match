Racing Santander v Real Madrid: Mbappé storm follows the leaders' run

Real Madrid have won five in a row, but the build-up has been dominated by Kylian Mbappé's apology and a broadcasting row in China.

Photo: Maria Victoria Gomez Fernandez · CC BY 2.5
Racing Santander – Real MadridLaLiga15:15 UTC

Match centre →

Key points
  • Real Madrid: five wins in a row
  • Mbappé apologised after a video about Mao Zedong
  • CCTV stopped broadcasting LaLiga matches

Real Madrid travel to Racing Santander on a run of five straight wins, including a 2-1 victory at Barcelona last weekend and a 4-1 win over RB Leipzig in Europe.

Off the pitch, the week has been dominated by Kylian Mbappé. The forward apologised after a video in which he joked about a meme linking him to Mao Zedong, and two Chinese brands ended their deals with him, The Guardian reported.

Chinese state television CCTV did not show any LaLiga match on Saturday, according to EFE via Mundo Deportivo, and Marca reports that Real Madrid's game on Sunday will not be broadcast there either.

Racing come into the game in decent form, having beaten Espanyol 2-1 in the league and Ribadesella 2-0 in midweek.
