Galatasaray v Stuttgart: Demirovic double lifts the visitors

Stuttgart won 4-2 at Bayer Leverkusen on Saturday, while Rafael Leão has opened his scoring account for Galatasaray.

Key points
  • Stuttgart won 4-2 at Leverkusen
  • Demirovic scored twice
  • Rafael Leão's first Galatasaray goal came from the penalty spot

VfB Stuttgart travel to Istanbul for Tuesday's Champions League game after a statement win in the Bundesliga. They beat Bayer Leverkusen 4-2 away on Saturday, with Ermedin Demirovic scoring twice, a result L'Equipe described as Stuttgart finally taking off.

Galatasaray have a new scorer to call on: Rafael Leão scored his first goal for the club on Friday, converting a penalty to complete a heavy win, Record reported.

The Turkish club have also had trouble off the pitch. They were fined after showing a Fenerbahçe badge with three stars on the stadium screen, and Brown and Bardakcı received bans, according to Index.hr.
