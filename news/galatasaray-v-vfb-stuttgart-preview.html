Galatasaray v Stuttgart: in-form hosts meet the Leverkusen conquerors

Galatasaray have won four in a row; Stuttgart arrive after a 4-2 win at Bayer Leverkusen.

Key points
  • Galatasaray: four wins in a row
  • Stuttgart won 4-2 at Leverkusen
  • Demirović scored twice

Galatasaray host VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League on a run of four straight wins, including a 2-1 derby win over Fenerbahçe and a 4-2 victory at Lille.

Stuttgart had won only two of seven games before Saturday, kicker notes, but beat Bayer Leverkusen 4-2 away thanks to Ermedin Demirović's double and a specific game plan. "We knew they could not cope with nasty duels," said Deniz Undav.

The Germans drew 1-1 with Atlético Madrid in the previous round of the league phase.
