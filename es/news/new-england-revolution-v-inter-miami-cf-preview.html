New England v Inter Miami: Messi cleared to play

An MLS rule lets Lionel Messi play despite a fifth yellow card, with Chicago Fire one point behind Inter Miami in the East.

Claves
  • Messi available despite a fifth yellow card
  • Chicago Fire: 55 points, one behind Inter Miami
  • One week of the regular season left

Lionel Messi is available for Inter Miami's game at New England Revolution. The 39-year-old would normally have been suspended after a fifth yellow card, but a special MLS rule allows him to play, Voetbal International reported.

The pressure is on in the Eastern Conference. Chicago Fire won 4-2 at Colorado Rapids, with Jonathan Bamba scoring twice and Robert Lewandowski also on target, and now have 55 points, one behind Inter Miami.

There is one week left of the regular season, so every point counts in the race for the top places.
