FC Augsburg 1-2 SC Freiburg: Irié header completes Freiburg comeback

Freiburg turned around a first-half deficit with two second-half goals from just two shots on target.

Photo: Futbol vic · CC BY-SA 3.0
FC Augsburg – SC FreiburgBundesliga14:30 UTC
Claves
  • Goals: Gregoritsch 18' (Augsburg); Matanovic 65', Irié 85' (Freiburg)
  • Freiburg scored with both of their shots on target
  • All three yellow cards went to Freiburg

SC Freiburg came from behind to win 2-1 at the WWK Arena, scoring with both of their shots on target.

Augsburg took the lead in the 18th minute. Arijon Ibrahimovic delivered a cross and Michael Gregoritsch finished with his left foot from the centre of the box into the top left corner.

Freiburg were level in the 65th minute when Yannik Engelhardt set up Igor Matanovic, who drilled a right-footed shot into the bottom left corner.

The winner arrived five minutes from time. Vincenzo Grifo crossed and Cyriaque Irié rose to head into the top right corner from close range.

Augsburg could feel hard done by. They had 51.5 per cent possession, 15 shots and five on target, but Freiburg's efficiency proved decisive. Freiburg picked up all three of the match's yellow cards, with Maximilian Eggestein, Matthias Ginter and Grifo all booked.
