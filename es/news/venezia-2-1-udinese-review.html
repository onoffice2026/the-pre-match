Zanetti's Udinese debut ends in defeat as Kike Pérez wins it for Venezia

Toma Basic opened the scoring, an own goal levelled it, and Pérez's strike from distance gave Venezia a 2-1 win.

Claves
  • Basic 53', Moreno own goal 57', Pérez 65'
  • Udinese still without a win under their new coach
  • Venezia end a run of three defeats

Paolo Zanetti's first game in charge of Udinese ended the way so many of their recent ones have. After a goalless first half, Toma Basic put Venezia ahead in the 53rd minute with a left-footed shot from the centre of the box.

Udinese were level within four minutes, Matías Moreno turning the ball into his own net, confirmed after a VAR review. But Kike Pérez restored Venezia's lead on 65 minutes with a left-footed shot from outside the box into the top corner.

Udinese had more of the ball (54%) and more corners (7 to 3), but managed only five shots on target. Venezia's three-game losing run is over.

Our take: a new coach brings energy, not instant results. Udinese still have one league win all season; Venezia, at home, showed the edge that beat Napoli last month.
