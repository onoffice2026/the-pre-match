Toulouse finally turn their football into goals: 3-1 against Strasbourg

Hidalgo's goal on the stroke of half-time opened it, McKenzie and Jørgensen finished the job after Reyna's reply.

Toulouse answered the question L'Équipe asked this week – whether they could win without changing their style – with a 3-1 win. Santiago Hidalgo broke the deadlock in first-half stoppage time, and Mark McKenzie headed in the second from close range in the 56th minute.

Strasbourg, fresh from their 5-1 against Auxerre, pulled one back through Gio Reyna in the 67th minute, but Thomas Jørgensen volleyed the third nine minutes later.

It was a scrappy, physical night – six yellow cards in all – and Toulouse won it on efficiency: five shots on target from ten, against Strasbourg's four from nine. They also had seven corners to one.

Our take: an important win for Toulouse after one point from two games. Strasbourg's revival hits a speed bump, but they kept scoring away from home.
