Elversberg's BVB-supporting coach brings his giant-killers to Dortmund

Karetsas's first Champions League goal beat Betis; promoted Elversberg arrive after hammering Mainz.

Claves
  • Dortmund unbeaten in six; Karetsas scored the winner against Betis
  • Elversberg beat Mainz 4-1
  • Villarreal reportedly chasing David Mokwa

For Elversberg coach Vincent Wagner, Saturday is personal. A lifelong Borussia Dortmund fan, he joked that it will be "a new record" for how close he has ever been to the pitch at the Westfalenstadion, but he wants his newly promoted side to hurt his favourite club.

Elversberg showed what they can do last Friday, leading Mainz 3-0 at half-time on the way to a 4-1 win. They had lost their two games before that, 0-4 at RB Leipzig and 0-2 at home to Darmstadt in the cup. Wagner used the Mainz win to criticise the fixture load on his young internationals, saying they are being "put through the meat grinder". Striker David Mokwa is reportedly one of Villarreal's top targets for January.

Dortmund are unbeaten in six. On Wednesday Konstantinos Karetsas scored the winner against Real Betis, 2-1, his first competitive goal for the club; L'Équipe reports that a toilet break almost made him miss his chance to come on. Coach Niko Kovač said he could "turn a blind eye" for the youngster. Before that, BVB drew 2-2 at Bayern.
