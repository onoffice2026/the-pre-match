Barcelona 3-0 Aston Villa: Yamal starts it, Raphinha finishes it

Lamine Yamal scored after seven minutes and Raphinha added two in the second half as Villa managed one shot on target.

Photo: Tsutomu Takasu · CC BY 2.0
Barcelona – Aston VillaChampions League20:00 UTC

Centro del partido →

Claves
  • Yamal scores after 7 minutes
  • Raphinha two goals in 12 minutes
  • Villa: 1 shot on target from 9
  • Three Villa yellow cards before half-time

Barcelona took control early. Lamine Yamal scored with his left foot from the centre of the box in the seventh minute.

Villa collected three yellow cards before half-time, for Ian Maatsen, Emiliano Buendía and Nicolas Jackson, as they struggled to stop Barcelona's passing.

Raphinha put the game away after the break: he scored from Fermín López's assist in the 52nd minute and from João Cancelo's pass at close range in the 64th.

Barcelona had 64.7% of possession and eight shots on target from 16. Villa won eight corners but hit the target only once.
