Trabzonspor 2-0 Gaziantep: Savic and Muçi score

Stefan Savic scored just before half-time and Ernest Muçi doubled the lead as Trabzonspor beat Gaziantep at Papara Park.

Claves
  • Goals: Savic 45+2', Muçi 61'
  • Both teams: 6 shots on target
  • Trabzonspor: 18 shots

Trabzonspor beat Gaziantep FK 2-0 at Papara Park.

Stefan Savic gave the hosts the lead in first-half stoppage time, and Ernest Muçi made it 2-0 in the 61st minute.

The match was more even than the score suggests: both teams had six shots on target, and each goalkeeper had plenty to do. Trabzonspor finished with 57.4 per cent of the ball and 18 shots to Gaziantep's 13.

Juninho Bacuna was the only player booked, for Gaziantep in the 60th minute.
