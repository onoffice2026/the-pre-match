Crystal Palace v Liverpool: Iraola asks for calm after two defeats

Liverpool have lost back-to-back games; Ryan Gravenberch could still play some part at Selhurst Park.

Claves
  • Liverpool have lost two in a row
  • Gravenberch may get minutes
  • Transfer talk around De Winter and Van Dijk

Liverpool travel to Selhurst Park after two defeats in a row, and Andoni Iraola has spent the week asking for calm while demanding a reaction from his players.

Iraola stayed vague on Ryan Gravenberch's fitness. The midfielder was left out of the Netherlands squad for the coming internationals, but the Liverpool manager would not rule out minutes for him against Palace.

“We have to react.”Andoni Iraola

Off the pitch, transfer stories keep circling: reports link Liverpool with AC Milan defender Koni De Winter, while Virgil van Dijk's future is again under discussion.
