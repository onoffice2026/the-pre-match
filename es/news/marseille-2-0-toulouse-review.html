Match review

Marseille 2-0 Toulouse: Harit scores in the first minute

Amine Harit and Amine Gouiri put Marseille two up inside 25 minutes, and the hosts held on despite Toulouse having more of the ball.

Photo: Dronepicr · CC BY 3.0
Marseille – ToulouseLigue 119:45 UTC
Claves
  • Goals: Harit 1', Gouiri 25'
  • Gouiri: one goal and one assist
  • Toulouse: 7 shots on target, 6 saves for Marseille

Marseille beat Toulouse 2-0 at the Stade Vélodrome, with both goals coming in the first 25 minutes.

Amine Harit gave the hosts the perfect start in the 1st minute, striking from outside the box into the bottom left corner after a pass from Amine Gouiri.

Gouiri then scored himself in the 25th minute, finishing from the centre of the box after Igor Paixão's headed pass.

Toulouse responded well. They finished with 56.4 per cent possession, 14 shots and seven on target, but the Marseille goalkeeper made six saves to keep a clean sheet.

Pierre-Emile Højbjerg and CJ Egan-Riley were booked for Marseille, Santiago Hidalgo for Toulouse.
