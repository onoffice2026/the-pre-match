Hamburg and Gladbach meet at the bottom with nerves on both sides

Friday night in the Volksparkstadion pits 15th against last: Hamburg have lost three in a row, Gladbach travel without their top scorer Haris Tabakovic.

Claves
  • 15th v 18th: Hamburg host the bottom club on Friday, 20:30 CET
  • Hamburg lost both games to Frankfurt this week (1-3, 1-3)
  • Gladbach without top scorer Tabakovic; 17-year-old Mohya called up by Germany

The Bundesliga opens its ninth round with a game nobody in either city wanted to be this important. Hamburg sit 15th, Borussia Mönchengladbach are bottom, and the German press has already settled on the label: a crisis duel.

Hamburg's week explains the mood. They lost twice to Eintracht Frankfurt in five days – first in the cup, then in the league, 1-3 both times – after a 0-1 defeat at Paderborn. That ended a short revival built on wins over Hoffenheim and Stuttgart in October, and it has left the home crowd impatient again.

Gladbach have problems of their own, starting up front. Haris Tabakovic, who scored 11 times in the last year – about a fifth of the team's goals – is out for a longer spell. Their last league outing was a goalless home draw with Paderborn, which sums up the issue: they create too little without him. The one bright story is Wael Mohya, the midfielder who turns 18 at the end of December and has just been called up by Germany.

The view here: Hamburg at home, with the crowd behind them, are the more dangerous side, and Gladbach without Tabakovic look short of a finisher. But this is a game shaped by fear of losing, and a tight first half would surprise nobody. A late goal decides it more often than not in matches like this.
