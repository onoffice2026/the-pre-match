Málaga under pressure at La Rosaleda as Racing arrive without Andrés Martín

Málaga are living their second-worst start in history and graffiti appeared at the stadium this week; Racing Santander lose their leading scorer.

Málaga – Racing SantanderLaLiga13:00 UTC

Centro del partido →

Claves
  • Málaga's second-worst start in their history; graffiti at La Rosaleda
  • Racing coach José Alberto has never lost to Málaga
  • Racing's top scorer Andrés Martín out

The mood around Málaga is tense. The club has made its second-worst start to a top-flight season, graffiti appeared at La Rosaleda this week, and Manuel Pellegrini added his voice to the debate, saying the club should have bet more on experience to avoid suffering in Primera.

Recent results explain the pressure: a 2-5 home defeat to Real Sociedad, draws with Espanyol and Alavés, and a 2-1 loss at Betis last weekend. Racing's coach José Alberto López returns to La Rosaleda, where he once worked, and he has never lost against Málaga.

Racing Santander arrive after a 3-0 home defeat to Real Madrid, a game remembered for Yan Diomandé's spectacular volley for Madrid. The bigger problem is Andrés Martín: their top scorer – 18 goals in the last year, about a fifth of the team's total – is out for a longer spell.

The view here: both sides need the points, and Málaga at home have the crowd, if it stays patient. Racing without Martín lose their main threat, which makes a tight, low-scoring game the most likely script.
