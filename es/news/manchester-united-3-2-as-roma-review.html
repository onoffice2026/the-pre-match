Manchester United 3-2 Roma: Cunha's late double, then a red card

Roma led 2-0 after 17 minutes; Bruno Fernandes' penalty and two Matheus Cunha goals in three minutes turned it around at Old Trafford.

Photo: Paolo Camera (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Manchester United – AS RomaChampions League20:00 UTC

Centro del partido →

Claves
  • Roma 2-0 up after 17 minutes
  • Bruno Fernandes penalty on 58'
  • Cunha scores twice (83', 86'), then sent off
  • Two penalties, one red card

Roma started fast. Matías Soulé scored in the 11th minute from Rodrigo Moreno's assist, and Donyell Malen converted a penalty six minutes later.

United got back into it from the spot in the 58th minute, Bruno Fernandes shooting into the high centre of the goal.

The comeback came late. Matheus Cunha scored from very close range in the 83rd minute and again from the right side of the box three minutes later.

Cunha's night ended badly: he was booked for his celebration and then sent off with a second yellow card in stoppage time. He will miss United's next Champions League game.

United had 15 shots to Roma's seven. The game produced nine yellow cards, one red card and two penalties.
