Hamburg look to end a three-game losing run against unbeaten-in-three Gladbach

Friday night at the Volksparkstadion: the hosts have lost three in a row, Gladbach are without top scorer Haris Tabakovic.

Claves
  • Hamburg: three defeats in a row
  • Gladbach: three games unbeaten
  • Tabakovic (11 goals in a year) still out

Hamburg open the Bundesliga weekend on Friday needing a reaction. Their last three games have all been defeats: 0-1 at Paderborn and two 1-3 losses to Eintracht Frankfurt in the space of a week, the second of which, on 1 November, lifted Frankfurt back into the early race for the European places. Their last win was 1-0 against VfB Stuttgart on 17 October.

Borussia Mönchengladbach are unbeaten in three, with a 3-2 win at Stuttgart, a 2-0 cup win over Karlsruher SC and a goalless draw with Paderborn last Saturday. They will again be without striker Haris Tabakovic, who is out long-term; he has scored 11 goals in the last year, about a fifth of the team's total.
