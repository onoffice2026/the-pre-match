Udinese v Roma: unbeaten Roma carry their comeback spirit north

Roma are still unbeaten in Serie A after twice coming from behind to draw 3-3 at Napoli. They visit Udinese on Saturday evening.

Photo: Matteo.favi · CC BY-SA 4.0
Udinese – AS RomaSerie A17:00 UTC
Claves
  • Roma remain unbeaten in Serie A.
  • They came from 0-2 down to draw 3-3 at Napoli; Balerdi scored late.
  • Napoli coach Allegri was sent off for protesting the equaliser.

AS Roma travel to Udinese as one of the few sides in Serie A still without a league defeat. Last weekend at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona they recovered from 0-2 down against Napoli, then answered Napoli's renewed lead to secure a 3-3 draw.

Leonardo Balerdi scored the late equaliser, with Donyell Malen providing an assist. The goal infuriated Napoli coach Massimiliano Allegri, who felt there had been a foul on Sam Beukema in the build-up and was shown a red card for his protests.

Roma's young talents are drawing attention too. The Italian press compared a headed goal by Rodrigo Mora at the Olimpico to Lionel Messi's famous header in the 2009 Champions League final - a reminder that timing can beat height.

Udinese will have to deal with a team that keeps finding a way back into games.
