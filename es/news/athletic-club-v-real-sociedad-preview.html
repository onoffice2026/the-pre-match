Athletic Club v Real Sociedad: the first Basque derby of the season

Real Sociedad arrive at San Mamés with four straight league wins, and Athletic still remember last season's four derby defeats.

Photo: Vivaespaña984 · CC BY-SA 4.0
Athletic Club – Real SociedadLaLiga20:00 UTC
Claves
  • Real Sociedad: four league wins in a row
  • Athletic won none of four derbies in 2025-26
  • Athletic beat Getafe 4-0 last weekend

San Mamés hosts the first Basque derby of the season on Sunday night, and the visitors arrive in form. Real Sociedad have won four league games in a row, Marca reports, and are unbeaten in six matches in all competitions.

Mundo Deportivo recalls that Real Sociedad were Athletic's nemesis in 2025-26. The two sides met four times and Athletic won none of them, including a Copa del Rey semi-final.

Athletic are building momentum, though. They beat Getafe 4-0 last weekend, the biggest win so far under Edin Terzić according to kicker, and won 6-1 at AE Prat in midweek.

The two clubs have also agreed to recreate a famous image from 1976 before kick-off, with the same flag and the same protagonists.
