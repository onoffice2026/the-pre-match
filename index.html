Frosinone are one of the surprises of the Serie A season. Saturday's 2-0 win over Torino, with goals from Fares Ghedjemis and Calò, was their fifth straight home win and their third victory in four games, taking Alvini's side to 20 points and sixth place. In between they drew 4-4 at Atalanta and beat Lecce 5-1.
