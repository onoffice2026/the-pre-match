Eintracht Frankfurt v Hamburg: a rematch five days after the cup tie

Frankfurt won 3-1 in Hamburg in the DFB-Pokal on Tuesday, and HSV's injury list has only grown since.

Photo: Patrik Meyer · CC BY-SA 4.0
Eintracht Frankfurt – Hamburg SVBundesliga16:30 UTC
Das Wichtigste
  • Frankfurt beat HSV 3-1 in the cup on Tuesday
  • HSV without Nadir, Vieira and Poulsen
  • Can Uzun set up two goals in the cup tie

Eintracht Frankfurt and Hamburg meet again only five days after Frankfurt won 3-1 at the Volksparkstadion in the DFB-Pokal. Can Uzun set up two goals that night and earned praise from his coach.

Referee Tobias Stieler later admitted that a penalty would have been the better decision in one of several disputed moments that went Frankfurt's way.

Hamburg's injury list keeps growing. Bilal Nadir and Fabio Vieira are out long-term, Yussuf Poulsen misses this week, six regulars were absent for the cup tie and Sebastiaan Bornauw went off after half-time, kicker reports.

HSV have now lost two in a row after three straight wins.
