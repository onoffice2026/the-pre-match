Wirtz at the centre as Liverpool win 3-1 at Selhurst Park

Mac Allister, Wirtz and Isak scored; Palace had more of the ball but only a late penalty to show for it.

Photo: joncandy (via Openverse) · CC BY-SA 2.0
Crystal Palace – LiverpoolPremier League14:00 UTC

Spielzentrale →

Das Wichtigste
  • Wirtz: goal + assist
  • Palace 19 shots, 7 on target
  • Nketiah penalty 90+5'

Liverpool broke the deadlock five minutes before half-time from a set piece: Dominik Szoboszlai's delivery found Alexis Mac Allister, whose header went straight down the middle.

Florian Wirtz doubled the lead in the 54th minute from the centre of the box after a cross from Bradley Barcola, and then turned provider in the 89th, releasing Alexander Isak on a fast break for the third.

Crystal Palace were far from passive - 53% of possession, 19 shots, seven on target - but the goalkeeper was busy and the finishing was not there. Eddie Nketiah's penalty deep in stoppage time only reduced the margin.

Six yellow cards in a scrappy second half, four of them for Liverpool, showed how hard the visitors had to work to protect the result.
