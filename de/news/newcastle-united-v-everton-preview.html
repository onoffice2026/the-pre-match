Newcastle v Everton: a quick rematch after Everton's 3-1 win

Newcastle host Everton on Monday night, four days after losing 3-1 away to the same opponents.

Das Wichtigste
  • Everton beat Newcastle 3-1 on Thursday
  • Newcastle: three defeats in their last five
  • Everton: two wins in their last five

Newcastle United and Everton meet for the second time in five days on Monday night. Everton won the first meeting 3-1 at home on Thursday, and Newcastle now have the chance to respond at St James' Park.

Newcastle have been inconsistent. They beat Crystal Palace 2-0 away, but have lost three of their last five games, including a 1-0 home defeat by Aston Villa and a 4-1 loss at Coventry City.

Everton's win over Newcastle came after two defeats without scoring, 2-0 against Chelsea and 2-0 at Arsenal. Before that they drew 2-2 at Hull City and beat Ipswich Town 1-0.
