Palmeiras must answer Flamengo's six-point statement against Remo

With five rounds left, Abel Ferreira's side no longer control the title race and cannot afford another slip at home.

Photo: Sepguilherme · CC BY-SA 4.0
Palmeiras – RemoBrasileirão22:30 UTC

Spielzentrale →

Das Wichtigste
  • Flamengo lead Palmeiras by six points with five rounds left
  • Ramón Sosa expected to miss out; Abel considering rotation
  • Remo have eight comeback wins this season

Flamengo did their part on Wednesday, beating Grêmio 3-1 at the Maracanã to move six points clear at the top of the Brasileirão, 76 to 70. That leaves Palmeiras needing a win against Remo at the Nubank Parque on Thursday (19:30 Brasília time) to keep the title race open.

Palmeiras no longer depend only on themselves, and the fixture comes in the middle of a Copa do Brasil semi-final, so Abel Ferreira is weighing whether to rotate his squad. Ramón Sosa is progressing in his recovery but is expected to miss out. Recent league form has been uneven: 2-0 wins over Athletico-PR and Red Bull Bragantino, a 0-1 home defeat to Fluminense and a 0-0 draw at Santos.

“He built a legacy that will be eternal.”Abel Ferreira, Palmeiras coach, on Cristiano Ronaldo

Remo are no easy opponent for a nervous home side. They had been the Série A team with the most comeback wins this season until Vasco matched their total of eight on Wednesday, and they beat Botafogo 2-1 last week after draws with Vasco and Bragantino and a narrow 0-1 loss at Cruzeiro.

Palmeiras will also wear a new, all-green shirt for the first time, a design made exclusively for club members. Before the game, Abel paid tribute to Cristiano Ronaldo, who announced his retirement from the Portugal national team on Thursday, saying he had "built a legacy that will be eternal".
