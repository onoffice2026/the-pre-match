São Paulo v Corinthians: a derby with Seedorf fighting relegation

Corinthians are below the relegation line with five games left; new coach Clarence Seedorf has Memphis Depay's backing.

São Paulo – CorinthiansBrasileirão00:30 UTC

Spielzentrale →

Das Wichtigste
  • Corinthians below the relegation line, five games left
  • Seedorf new Corinthians coach
  • São Paulo: one win in the last three

The Majestoso of round 34 kicks off on Wednesday at 21:30 Brasília time, at Vila Belmiro, according to ge.globo.

Corinthians are in trouble. Clarence Seedorf took charge with five games left and the club below the relegation line in Série A. Memphis Depay called the new coach a crucial piece for Corinthians.

They come in after a 2-0 win over Mirassol, but before that lost to Vasco (3-2), Palmeiras (4-1) and Internacional (2-1).

São Paulo are inconsistent too: a 3-2 defeat to Bahia and a 1-1 draw with Mirassol followed two wins over Vasco and Vitória.
