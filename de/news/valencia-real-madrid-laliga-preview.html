Real Madrid ride seven straight wins into Mestalla as Sadiq revives Valencia

Another late comeback in Athens kept Madrid perfect; Valencia finally won under Javier Aguirre.

Photo: Archivo Marca (No Copyright) · Public domain
Valencia – Real MadridLaLiga20:00 UTC

Spielzentrale →

Das Wichtigste
  • Real Madrid: seven wins in a row, unbeaten in the Champions League
  • Valencia won 1-0 at Elche for Aguirre's first victory
  • Valencia one point from safety

Real Madrid have already turned the page on Wednesday's 3-2 win in Athens and started preparing for Saturday's trip to Mestalla (21:00 local time). Trailing 2-1 at AEK, they won it in the final seconds, another comeback in a competition where they remain unbeaten. It was a seventh straight win in all competitions, a run that includes 2-1 at Barcelona and 3-0 at Racing Santander.

Not everything is smooth: Vinícius Júnior had another poor night in Athens and is under heavy criticism in the Madrid press. Elsewhere, Rafael Nadal is set to join the club's board in the coming weeks once members approve his appointment, and Franco Mastantuono, on loan at Fiorentina, said Madrid had treated him "incredibly".

“Real Madrid treated me incredibly.”Franco Mastantuono, on loan at Fiorentina

Valencia needed a lift and got one at Elche last Sunday. Umar Sadiq, back after two months out injured, scored a superb winner for a 1-0 victory that gave Javier Aguirre his first win as coach. It was only Valencia's second league win of the season and left them a point from safety, after a 1-2 home defeat by Villarreal and draws with Athletic Club and Racing.
