Elche v Valencia: Aguirre still chasing a first win

Valencia are winless in four, while Elche are unbeaten in five ahead of the derby at the Martínez Valero.

Photo: Ruben Ortega · CC BY-SA 4.0
Elche – ValenciaLaLiga17:30 UTC
Das Wichtigste
  • Valencia winless in four
  • Elche unbeaten in five
  • Mikautadze's late goal beat Valencia last week

Valencia head to Elche looking for Javier Aguirre's first victory as coach. Mundo Deportivo describes the trip to the Martínez Valero as a new chance to change the course of an unstable season.

Valencia lost 2-1 to Villarreal last weekend after a stoppage-time winner from Georges Mikautadze, and they are winless in four games. Loan goalkeeper Van Oevelen has a knee sprain.

Elche are unbeaten in five, including a 3-0 win at Rayo Vallecano and a goalless draw at Villarreal.
