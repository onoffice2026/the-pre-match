Eight goals, two comebacks: United rescue a 4-4 draw with Villa

Two down inside four minutes and 4-2 behind in stoppage time, Manchester United still took a point at Old Trafford.

Photo: stacey.cavanagh (via Openverse) · CC BY 2.0
Manchester United – Aston VillaPremier League16:30 UTC

Das Wichtigste
  • Villa 2-0 up after 4 minutes
  • Zirkzee 90+2', Dorgu 90+6'
  • United 26 shots, 9 on target

Aston Villa started at full speed. Alysson scored in the first minute after a pass from John McGinn, and Nicolas Jackson made it 2-0 three minutes later from close range after a set piece.

United answered before the break. Bruno Fernandes converted a penalty in the 16th minute and Youri Tielemans levelled in the 44th, so the teams went in at 2-2.

Villa took control again early in the second half: McGinn scored from the spot in the 48th minute and Leon Goretzka made it 4-2 five minutes later, set up by Jackson.

With the clock running out, Joshua Zirkzee pulled one back in the 92nd minute and Patrick Dorgu headed the equaliser from close range in the 96th, from a Fernandes cross. United finished with 26 shots, nine on target, and eight corners to Villa's one; they go into the international break still in the relegation zone.
