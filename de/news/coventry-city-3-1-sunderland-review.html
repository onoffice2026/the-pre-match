Coventry City 3-1 Sunderland: Sky Blues climb out of the drop zone

Goals from Tatsuhiro Sakamoto, Taiwo Awoniyi and Jack Rudoni lifted Coventry out of the bottom three and left ten-man Sunderland at the foot of the table.

Photo: Amakuru · CC BY-SA 4.0
Coventry City – SunderlandPremier League15:00 UTC
Das Wichtigste
  • Goals: Sakamoto 17', Awoniyi 37', Rudoni 52' (Coventry); Le Fée 67' pen (Sunderland)
  • Omar Alderete sent off for a second yellow card in stoppage time
  • Sunderland: 17 shots, only 2 on target

Coventry City climbed out of the relegation zone with a 3-1 win over Sunderland, a result that leaves the visitors bottom of the Premier League.

Frank Lampard's side went ahead in the 17th minute when Trai Hume's weak clearance fell for Tatsuhiro Sakamoto, who arrowed a left-footed shot from outside the box into the bottom left corner for his first Premier League goal. Sunderland should have levelled soon after, but Brian Brobbey dragged wide with the goal at his mercy.

Taiwo Awoniyi made it 2-0 in the 37th minute, pouncing on a loose pass and powering past challenges before his shot beat Robin Roefs via a deflection. It was his first league goal for the club.

Jack Rudoni effectively settled matters in the 52nd minute with a left-footed strike from distance, set up by Victor Torp. Sunderland were handed a lifeline in the 67th minute when Ethan Pinnock blocked with an arm and Enzo Le Fée converted the penalty into the top left corner.

Sunderland enjoyed 56.5 per cent of possession and had 17 shots, but only two were on target. Their frustration boiled over in stoppage time, when Omar Alderete was booked in the 94th minute and then sent off for a second yellow card in the 99th.
