Strasbourg 5-1 Auxerre: Yassine scores twice as Strasbourg run riot

Gessime Yassine scored twice and set up another as Strasbourg beat ten-man Auxerre 5-1 at the Stade de la Meinau.

Photo: Kevin.B · CC BY-SA 4.0
Strasbourg – AJ AuxerreLigue 114:00 UTC
Das Wichtigste
  • Goals: Essugo 41', Godo 55', Harder 62' (pen), Yassine 77', 85' (Strasbourg); Archer 68' (Auxerre)
  • Auxerre: Dioubaté sent off in the 74th minute
  • Strasbourg: 19 shots to Auxerre's 6

Strasbourg beat Auxerre 5-1 in Ligue 1 at the Stade de la Meinau, scoring four times after half-time against opponents who finished with ten men.

Dário Essugo broke the deadlock in the 41st minute, finishing from the centre of the box after a pass from Oso. Ten minutes after the restart Martial Godo doubled the lead on a fast break set up by Gessime Yassine, and Conrad Harder converted a penalty in the 62nd minute.

Auxerre pulled one back in the 68th minute when Cameron Archer finished from Romain Faivre's headed pass, but their hopes ended in the 74th minute when Lamfia Dioubaté was shown a straight red card.

Yassine then scored twice in eight minutes. He finished from Sebastian Nanasi's pass in the 77th minute and headed in Oso's cross from close range in the 85th minute.

Strasbourg had 64.3 per cent possession and 19 shots, against six for Auxerre, who received four yellow cards as well as the red card and did not win a corner.
