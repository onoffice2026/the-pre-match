Marseille v Toulouse: OM look to steady after the Le Havre scare

Marseille led Le Havre 4-0 and nearly lost it; Toulouse arrive unbeaten in five Ligue 1 games.

Photo: Dronepicr · CC BY 3.0
Marseille – ToulouseLigue 119:45 UTC
Das Wichtigste
  • OM nearly threw away a 4-0 lead against Le Havre
  • Greenwood among the absentees
  • Toulouse unbeaten in five

Marseille host Toulouse at the Vélodrome after a chaotic win over Le Havre last weekend. OM led 4-0 with half an hour to play and held on to win 4-3, and Bruno Genesio was furious with his young substitutes afterwards.

Mason Greenwood, who has 18 goals in the last 365 days, is listed among the absentees. Off the pitch, Foot Mercato reports that the squad has refused a new financial sacrifice as the club tries to cut its wage bill under pressure from the DNCG.

Toulouse are unbeaten in five games: they drew 0-0 with Troyes last time out after a 3-0 win at Le Mans.

Marseille have alternated wins and defeats over their last five matches, losing both of their recent European games 1-0.
